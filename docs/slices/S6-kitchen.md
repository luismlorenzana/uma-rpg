> **Superseded by D144 (2026-10-16):** dishes and the Kitchen are gone; Star Gems cut at the Lapidary open Extra Stats ([after the farm](../design/after-the-farm.md)).

> **Status: built and deploying, 2026-10-10.** Design: kitchen.md (deleted by D144), game design §8.4 and §10,
> D58.

## Goal

Cook dishes from refined ingredients, feed them to Umas, and build each Uma's Extra Stats on the Sun-and-planets map.

## What the owner sees

- **The Kitchen** (Farmlands) opens with slots like any facility: pick an Uma, a dish (with what it feeds and how many
  the Warehouse covers), how many, **Start**. Ingredients are reserved; dishes land on the Warehouse's **Dishes** shelf.
- **The Observatory** (Town) shows each Uma's map: the Sun, eight planets with ten moons, and the bridges.
  **Eat** a dish to unlock its next node; tap unlocked nodes to switch them on or off (up to 5 + level/2). The panel
  under the map shows what her active nodes give.
- Profiles link to her map (**Extra Stats**). Fights use the points: more HP and damage, evasion from Speed, a head
  start on the Limit Break.

## Exit check

Historical check; releases use the [current phone script](../testing/phone-check.md).

Refine ingredients, cook a grade-1 dish of two kinds, feed them to an Uma, switch a node off and on, and win a fight
with her.
