# S4 — Farmlands and Wilds

> **Status: built and deploying, 2026-10-10** (the owner asked for S4–S7 straight through). Design:
> [farm.md](../design/farm.md), [pacing.md](../design/pacing.md), [items.md](../design/items.md), D54–D56.

## Goal

The passive layer: Umas gather and refine while you're away, level their professions, fill the Warehouse, and
Laurels from battles buy items and shortcuts at the Exchange.

## What the owner sees

- **The 16 sites** (Farmlands and Wilds) and **the Pantry and Workbench** open. Each page shows its family or job,
  level and slots. **Assign** an Uma (her profession level and aptitude speed show), pick an item (higher grades
  open with her level), pick how many, **Start**. Running jobs show progress and time left with **Collect**,
  **Rush** and **Recall**. **Upgrade** shows its cost and adds a slot, then speed.
- **The Hub** shows each working Uma's face with a progress ring on her place, **Ready** tags, and a count of ready
  places on each page tab. A place's quick look lists its jobs with **Collect**.
- **The Warehouse** lists every item you own, family by family, with the item's details on tap.
- **The Exchange** sells raw items of reached chapters, Rush Tickets and Sweep Tickets for Laurels. Battles pay more
  Laurels now (10 a regular fight, 50 a champion).
- **Parties**: working members show **Busy**, can be recalled from the Party Hall, and keep their party from fighting.
  On the chapter map, a beaten regular fight offers **Sweep**.

## Exit check

Historical check; releases use the [current phone script](../testing/phone-check.md).

On the iPhone: assign two Umas to a site and one to the Pantry, close the app, come back to **Ready** tags, collect,
refine, buy a Rush Ticket and rush a job, upgrade a site, and sweep a beaten fight.
