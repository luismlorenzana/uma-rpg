Classes by tier

The classes from Tier 1 (live, engine v21) through Tiers 2 and 3, as classes.md (round 2: T2C31–T2C64) proposes: every Tier 1 class promotes on the Ascend into a Keeper and an Expander, sixteen in all, and Tier 3 deepens them. Tier 1's numbers are the live code's; Lv 50 sheets come from the balance harness's on-curve Uma. Nothing in Tiers 2 and 3 is built. The sixteen, by family

Each Tier 1 class's two children. Mirrors share a rule; every class keeps her parent's slots' count, lean, trees, hall and Limit Break scale.

Lv 50 sheets, by tier (SB1)

On curve: her preset, G10 × tier, every Extra Stats node × tier, no template. A Tier 2 class wears her parent’s sheet (leans never change).

Offense holds SB1 exactly (×1.80, ×2.61). HP grows ×1.73 and ×2.47, MP ×1.72 and ×2.45: their base has a flat part.

MP and healing by tier

A depth-5 act's casts on a full bar, Lv 50

Today later tiers cost skill MP × the tier (×2, ×3). T2C17: ×1.7 and ×2.4, the bar's own growth.

A healer's healing scale, on-curve Lv 50

×(1 + offense ÷ divisor). Today ÷ 300 in every tier; T2C18 divides by 300 × the tier's multiplier.
