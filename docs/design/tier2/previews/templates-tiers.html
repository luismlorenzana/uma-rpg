Her calling with no Tier 0

A Tier 2 or 3 world has no Tier 0 and no Tier 1 class to fight as (CV10's rule, carried by round 2). Her calling is her racing profile's, so it doesn't move: it stops being a class and stays her family's name (T2U25). Her family there is both children, keeper and expander, of each of her family's Tier 1 classes; kin's children stay outside (T2U21). Struck through: the classes she can't fight as there.

The budget, measured on engine v21

A scratch run of the balance harness: both of the owner's parties (D263), on curve, every region stage at the level, 6 seeds, Auto. A gain is damage a turn over the same party without templates (regular fights twice, sub-bosses and bosses once). ★ alone adds her ★ to her class's own preset; matched rebuilds the preset with her family first, with her moon and an Even pace Flourish. Noise about ±0.5 points.

Damage

About +2% a turn at Lv 50 in every tier from the ★ alone (+1.0 to +3.6 · +0.5 to +3.3 · +1.3 to +3.4%). Matched, +1.2 · +1.3 · +1.5% on average: an Orb or her own stat makes the ★ free, an Insignia takes a stat slot and pays on sub-bosses and bosses, and the Fleet Paladin's Charm costs −0.9 to −2.8.

Defense

A matched party takes 4–6% less from Lv 30, once her moon opens (0 at Lv 10). The Flourish mends 0.1–0.9% of her max HP a fight on the world's stages: its 4% is the hard fight's cap. Node II is 3.8–4.0% of her defense.
