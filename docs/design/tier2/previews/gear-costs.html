Why her class's half: splitting a hybrid's pieces never pays (T2G8)

WP14 let the classes between pick a half per piece from Tier 2. Measured at Tier 2's Lv 50 (each class in her owner party on curve; each piece's 9 offense moved to her other half), her damage a turn against her class's half:

Single series: the damage she loses, in her row's cells. Heals: the Paladin's within noise (−1.6% to +1.3%), the Cleric's −1.6% to −7.0%.

Why: a hit is offense² ÷ (offense + defense), so a point on her stronger channel is worth more than one on her weaker; and since D264 the Pendant and the Charm already let a hybrid add her other half's stat from a slot (from Tier 2's G5, both at once with a Twofold form). The choice retires: five fewer toggles.

What a rank costs: 30 refined a line, in every tier (T2G16)

One rank of everything on two Umas, one on three Arms and one on three Tomes (D264). Tiers 2 and 3 add nothing to make.
