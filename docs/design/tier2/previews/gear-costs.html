Armor by tier, and what a grade costs

From gear.md (T2G29, T2G39, T2G40). From Tier 2 every grade takes its line's two items of that grade (professions' T2P29): the first raises the base, the second the Tier 2 part. Types and forms are settings and take nothing. Armor has no Tier 2 path: its second item, the Armor Lining, carries the rest of its × 1.8.

Her half: the stat her lead channel reads

A cross and a pure variant read one stat on every hit, so her armor's offense reaches every hit she makes. A Tier 3 class wears her Tier 1 ancestor's half (T2G61).

Why armor has no Tier 2 path

Professions assumed a lining adding each piece's offense to her other half too (T2P30). At Tier 2's G10 that is +45 of her other stat, which the harness prices at about +5% damage for every class that reads two stats (the M.ATK half of three Twofold slots, +54, is worth 6.5% to the Arms Spellblade) and nothing for the four that read one (the Berserker, the Archmage, the two crosses, the four pure variants). A free slot's worth for twelve of twenty classes breaks R2-11's "worth the same". Round 1 also measured that splitting a piece's offense never pays (−0.8 to −2.2% a piece).

So the Armor Lining carries the tier step, professions' own fallback: the Fittings raise Tier 1's values, the Lining the rest. The Hides line keeps its 30 a rank and armor's numbers are exactly the table above.
