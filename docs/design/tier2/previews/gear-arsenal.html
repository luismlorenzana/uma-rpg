The two kits (T2G54)

Gladiator · from the Spellfist · 3 Arms on ATK

Thaumaturge · from the Battlemage · 3 Tomes on M.ATK

T2G3's one two-kind weapon, read by class: two for these two. Her kinds and both partners all different; each type at its own rate (Keen ×1.70, Tempered and Evolved ×1.50 at G10); one grade a kind; the kind cap on the Orb family alone. Tempered for Evolved on either reaches the same five kinds.

What the fix moves (T2G55, T2G56)

Arsenal stays as classes wrote it. The fix is the Suggested types: T2G50's rule (one two-kind weapon or none, the fewest kinds in one hand over every mix) run under the bound, every Tier 3 class searched.
