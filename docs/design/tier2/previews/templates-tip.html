Where it sits: her lead tree's tip fork

A Tier 2 class: her class tree (branch, focus, three class skills; T2C) and her three weapon trees. Tier 2's trees give every fork a third line, C, and every line's depth-5 tip a pick, Power or Break (CP19, carried by T2C). On her first slot's tree, as a class of her family, the fork offers her stride's ★ tip too. She holds one line a tree; the ★ rides the tip of the one she holds.

Her lead tree, by class

Her first slot is always her lead channel, so her lead tree is a weapon of her lead half. On her class preset (D263) it is the one marked; she may put another weapon of that channel first, and the tip follows (T2U10, T2U12). In a class branch she takes the branch's set, and its first slot leads (T2C). The tip's shape today, line A and line B, is what the ★ modifies.

The three tips, against Power and Break

Sidegrades (CP15: reach, hits or support, never power alone), each an engine rule that exists today. Drawn on a one-foe, three-hit tip (a Brawler's Greatsword line A) against three weak foes with shields of 6 (filled pips are shield left; each weak hit removes one); a volley tip below Spillover. Priced against Power in Tier 2's simulation (T2B): within 3 points.

Tier 3: both held lines, and their capstones

Tier 3's trees let her hold two of a tree's three lines, each with its own tip pick and a depth-6 capstone at Class Rank 50 (CP20, carried by T2C). The ★ is offered at both tips of her lead tree; a capstone built on a ★ tip keeps its effect, as a capstone keeps its tip's pick. The master slot (CP22) may hold it like any capstone. Her other two trees: Power and Break only (T2U13).
