How the forty-two were measured (T2B41, T2B42)

R3-8T2B21 kept

On the harness, not on paper. Round 2's fork was deleted, so it was rebuilt and checked first: on Tier 1's rules it plays Party 1 at Lv 50 exactly as engine v21 (192 fights, 5.094 rounds a fight on both). Every rule of classes' round 3 runs on it, every Uma built by the live harness with Tier 2's MP and healing divisor, gear's types and forms and Extra Stats' Prowess presets. Every honeycomb stage of the level (48 fights, 24 sub-bosses, 24 bosses), 16 seeds: 1,536 fights a party and level.

A class's gain is the party's damage a round (HP taken off foes, no overkill) with her in her parent's seat, minus with her parent, over her parent's V = D + S. Fights weigh 2, sub-bosses and bosses 1.

The noise

A second set of 16 seeds reads every gain within 1.4 points of the first (0.5 typical): a cell within a point of the band's edge is at the edge.

Left out

Class lines, line C, Break tips and the extra equipped slots (every class gets them); two lines held in Tier 3; the Rift but for its wards.

Merged mid-round

Gear's round-3 types and presets and Extra Stats' Offense for the pure variants were re-read on the affected classes (T2B66): all sixteen hold if the Enchanter, the Marshal and the Animist keep their keeper's Dual.
