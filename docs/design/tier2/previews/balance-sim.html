What it means

With no Tier 2 tools, no term is needed: a Tier 2 world is Tier 1's × tier and the build budget restarts with her grades. The term (T2B4, T2B5) prices only what the set adds.

The pace is the one gap today: with no Tier 0 bank a later tier opens depths two to three levels sooner (§ below). T2B3 puts it on Tier 1's curve.

Breaks: rounds to her first Break within ±0.6 of Tier 1's in every cell, so today's shields hold until the two-kind weapons arrive (T2B7).
