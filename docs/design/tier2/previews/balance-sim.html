How party value is measured (T2B20, T2B21)

R2-11amends T2C56

On the harness, not on paper. The sixteen's rules run on a scratch fork of engine v21 with the live Auto; every Uma is built by the live harness (gear, Extra Stats, a Learner's Limit Break, Class Rank on Tier 1's pace) with Tier 2's MP (×1.7) and healing divisor (÷ 540), gear's Suggested types and forms and Extra Stats' Prowess presets. Every stage of the level, 16 seeds: 1,280 fights a party.

A class's gain is the party's damage a round with her in her parent's seat, minus with her parent, over her parent's V. Fights weigh 2, sub-bosses and bosses 1. V = D + S: D her own damage a round, in BT (an on-curve Brawler's at the same tier and level); S what the other four lose when her statuses, mends, wards and keeper twist are switched off.

Emulated exactly

Momentum (a factor beside the statuses, a stack after each turn, lost on a KO); the cross (every hit on her stat, defense, weakness and statuses by the kind's channel, her other channel's trees); both halves' familiar; both summons lingering; the support level's statuses, Barrier and farewell mend; the keepers' extra stack; the battle healers' hand and shares; Area and Multi-hit at step 4b; Pure and Dual weak multipliers by grade; the forms and Kindred.

Left out

Class lines, line C, Break tips and Tier 2's extra equipped slots (every class gets them; the build re-measures); two lines held in Tier 3; Vow. Tier 3's rider step uses Tier 2's rates × 2.6.
