Promotion and switching

The Ascend is the Tier 2 promotion (R2-5; classes.md §4.9, T2C46–T2C50). She picks one Tier 2 class among both children of every Tier 1 class she brought to Class Rank 50, switches among them after, and opens more with the door. Set her Tier 1 record on the left; the sheet and the Classes tree follow.

Her Tier 1 record

The Ascend sheet · Tier 2 class

Held from arrival at Class Rank 1, each on its preset. Suggested: the keeper of the class she ascends as (progression's T2W18), else her family's own Rank 50 class's keeper. A child of a kin class is never Suggested and outside her Flourish.

The Classes sheet in Tier 2: switching and the door

Tap a held class to fight as it. Give it Class Rank 20 to open the door: the children of her parent's reclass neighbours open (dashed). Closed classes say how they open.

The shared ladder (XR1): her first 3,600 Class EXP raise every held class to Rank 10 together; from 10 each climbs alone. Class Rank 20 on one class is 3,520 more, about the Tier 1 door's 3,600.

The door's map

The reclass's map (D290, D291) lifted one tier: a Tier 1 class's neighbours are the Tier 1 classes its Tier 0 parents promote into.

Tier 3

No new class and no promotion (T2C57): she ascends as her Tier 2 class; every Tier 2 class she held in Tier 2 is open and held in Tier 3 at Class Rank 1; the door works as in Tier 2. Tier 3 deepens every class alike: two lines held, the crown at Class Rank 50, the master slot and her family's mastery.
