Build a five

How it's counted

Measured (T2B21)

  • D, her damage a round of the fight, in BT (a knockout costs her turns; her kills are hers).
  • S, what the other four lose when her statuses, wards, mends and keeper twist are switched off: overlap, the cap of 3 and the build-up are in it.
  • A class's gain: the party's damage a round with her in her parent's seat, minus with her parent, over her parent's V (D + S). Fights weigh 2, sub-bosses and bosses 1.
  • The band (T2B22): each of the twelve damage and support classes within ±5 points of the twelve's mean gain at Lv 10, 30 and 50; the four healers on the stress check.

The builder's estimate

For a five that isn't one of C1–C7, the builder adds each member's measured swap: Σ (her gain × her parent's V) ÷ Σ her parents' D, at each level. On C1–C7 it lands within about a point of the harness (22 of 24 cells within 2.3 points), except where both cross-wielders meet (C3 and C7 at Lv 30 and 50, about 3 points under the harness, which credits the pair more than their sum): read it as a floor there.

The model (T2C56, analytic)

  • V = D + S + B a turn: D her parent's Tier 1 damage a turn ÷ 993 × her rule's factor; S Σ v × strength × her part × the others' D (Armor Break and Mind Break 5%, Brave and Faith 6% at full); B her Breaks, 6% or 7% of D for the hybrid keepers.
  • H, HP a round she can mend or ward (half her casts depth 5, 35% depth 4, 15% depth 3; healing scale 1.64; 0.8 acts a round). The builder shows it for every five: the harness reads sustain on the stress check instead.
