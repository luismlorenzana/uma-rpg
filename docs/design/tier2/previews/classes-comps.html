The formula (T2C56)

V = D + S + B for each member, healing capacity H beside it.

  • D, her damage: her parent's Tier 1 damage a turn ÷ 993 × her rule's factor (Momentum 1 + step × 1.88; a cross 1.05 × 1.012 or × 1.018; a support 0.85 or 0.9; a battle healer 1.2; a Summoner keeper 1.02).
  • S, what her statuses add to the other four: Σ v × strength × her part × the others' D. v at full: Armor Break 5%, Mind Break 5%, Brave and Faith 6%. Strength on the tapped foe and the rest: widely 1.0 · 0.5, a healer's path 0.5 · 0.25, a hybrid's path 0.35 · 0, a Spellblade support 1.0 · 0.5, a Summoner support 1.0 · 1.0, a keeper healer ×1.2; the tapped foe takes 60% of the damage; two carriers: the stronger whole, the rest a quarter.
  • B, her Breaks: 6% (Spellblade keepers) or 7% (Summoner keepers) of her D.
  • H, HP a round she can mend or ward (half her casts depth 5, 35% depth 4, 15% depth 3; healing scale 1.64; 0.8 acts a round). On curve it counts at ε ≈ 0.58 foe HP an HP saved, near nothing: healers and the Summoners' supports are levelled on the stress check.

Calibration: the model gives a Tier 1 healer's statuses 9% of the others' damage, the harness 9.8% (Paladin) and 12.3% (Cleric); a path beside the healer's same status 0.5–1%, alone 3–4% (§2.7).
