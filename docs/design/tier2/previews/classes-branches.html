The twenty Tier 2 classes

Round 3 (classes.md §4): every Tier 1 class promotes on the Ascend into two or three. The Brawler and the Sorcerer into a keeper and an expander; each hybrid into a keeper, a support and a pure variant (new); each healer into a keeper and a battle healer. The Spellfist and the Battlemage are reshaped to single big hits. Cells are each class's seat gain over her parent at Lv 10 · 30 · 50; red is outside the band (the mean ± 5 points). The twenty, by parent

Momentum: the pure keepers' self-buff (T2C34)

+6% a stack on her own hits, a stack after each of her turns, three at most (+18%), the fight long, lost when she falls; never a party buff. The Berserker and the Archmage in Tier 2; in Tier 3 the Ravager and the Astromancer keep it, and the four pure variants' Tier 3 classes take it (Dervish, Starweaver, Imperator, Mythlord).

Measured (balance, T2B26): her damage +11.3% at Lv 50 (+9.1% fights, +13.3% bosses); her seat +5.6 · +2.0 · +5.2% (Berserker), +5.8 · +3.4 · +5.1% (Archmage).

Single big hits: the Spellfist and the Battlemage (T2C71–T2C73)

Pinpoint: +2% a depth on an act that strikes only the tapped foe

A factor at step 5, beside the statuses and Momentum; it reaches her Limit Break. Tier 3's kept children (Spellhammer, Spellknight): +4% a depth.
