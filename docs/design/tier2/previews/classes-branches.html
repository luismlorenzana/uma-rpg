Class branches, sets and parties

Tier 2's class branch for the eight Tier 1 classes (classes.md §4.2–§4.6, T2C4–T2C13): ten branches, two a family, each a weapon group's focus (+15%), a set and a class line; the owner's two comps flipped member by member; and what the Halls add to either set.

The two weapon groups

Ringthe ring's three twin sets · Heavy, Sweep, Scatter

Pure damage for a Spellblade or a Summoner; every-skill statuses for a healer (Greatsword, Lance, Fire).

Light/DarkLight, Dark and Twilight sets · Double, Volley

D282's secondary weapons: the path's statuses for the hybrids and the healers. The volleys give it its own reach.

The ten branches

A family's two classes share its two branches: names, line, crown and mastery; each casts it with her own hand. The line opens at Class Rank 20 · 30 · 40 and is cast through one of her focus weapons.

Every class's two sets

Outlined: the kinds her branch boosts. Branch 1 is her standard preset (D263), its group the one holding two of three; branch 2 her reverse set, picked by a script for the most party mixes at all ten kinds.

Party builder: the owner's two comps

Every count is one kind a weapon, so it holds whatever weapon branch she takes. 33 of the 64 mixes hold all ten kinds (16 and 17).

Hall coverage of both sets

The sixteen seats' kinds (D261). A seat adds a kind when its Limit Break carries one her three weapons miss. LB+ (+20% at Bond 10) from Tier 2.
