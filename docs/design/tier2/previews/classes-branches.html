The sixteen Tier 2 classes

Each Tier 1 class promotes on the Ascend into a Keeper, who keeps her identity with one sharpening rule, and an Expander, who widens it with one twist (classes.md §4, T2C31–T2C45). Mirrors share a rule: eight rules, sixteen classes. Every class keeps her parent's three slots' count, lean, armor half, trees, hall and Limit Break scale.

Both halves: the hybrid keepers

Blademaster · RunebladeKeeper

Tier 1: her bound familiar lands one extra hit on her lead half, the same total. Keeper: on both halves. A weak other half chips one more shield point; a Pierce tree's hit splits in two, both piercing.

Worth: the same damage, about 0.5 more chips a turn; the model credits +6% of her damage to Breaks.

Warlord · InvokerKeeper

Tier 1: her lead summon lingers and strikes every foe in farewell at her next pair. Keeper: both summons linger. Two farewells, one of each kind, on every foe: each weak foe chips one more point a pair.

Worth: +2% damage, about one more chip a turn on three foes; the model credits +7% to Breaks.

The supports: a little of the healers' kit

Her healing scale is her higher of ATK and M.ATK ÷ (300 × the tier's multiplier), about ×1.67 on curve at Lv 50. A support is no healer: one healer a party is unchanged.
