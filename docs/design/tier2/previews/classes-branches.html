The sixteen Tier 2 classes

Each Tier 1 class promotes on the Ascend into a Keeper, who keeps her identity with one sharpening rule, and an Expander, who widens it with one twist (classes.md §4, T2C31–T2C45). Mirrors share a rule: eight rules, sixteen classes; a rule's knob, her hand, is measured per class. Every class keeps her parent's three slots' count, lean, armor half, trees, hall and Limit Break scale. Kits are at balance's knobs (T2B24–T2B29); each gain is measured on its harness, her parent's seat, Tier 2 Lv 10 · 30 · 50.

Momentum: the pure keepers' self-buff

Her damage by the turns she gets in a fight

6%

A stack after each of her turns, three at most, the fight long, lost when she falls. Her first act has none. +6% a stack (T2B26; drafted +5%).

At Lv 50, on the harness's turns a fight

Turns a fight read from Tier 1's harness at Lv 50 (both comps, 6 seeds a stage). Measured (her parent's seat): the Berserker +5.6 · +2.0 · +5.2%, the Archmage +5.8 · +3.4 · +5.1% at Lv 10 · 30 · 50; at +5% they were a point short of the band's middle at Lv 50 (+4.5, +4.1%). At +10% a stack the keepers would lead their expanders by about 13% of their own damage.

What crossing is worth on its own (Tier 2 Lv 50, analytic)

Her best hit a turn on one regular, every Tier 2 weakness list equally likely (strikers and guards 1 Arm + 2 elements, casters and skirmishers 2 Arms + 1), foe defenses from rpgFoe at lifetime Lv 100. A breadth pick: the hand carries her into the band. Measured (her parent's seat, Lv 10 · 30 · 50): at ×1.05 on the Heavy twins the Spellfist +10.4 · +6.7 · +3.0%, the Battlemage −2.5 · −4.7 · −11.4%; at the knobs the Spellfist +6.5 · +4.5 · +4.3%, the Battlemage +10.9 · +8.3 · −1.8%. Her Arm meets the higher DEF of Tier 2's strikers and guards, and a hand never reaches her Limit Break.

Both halves: the hybrid keepers

Blademaster · RunebladeKeeper

Tier 1: her bound familiar lands one extra hit on her lead half, the same total. Keeper: on both halves, and it pierces (T2B28). A weak other half chips one more shield point; the familiar's hit skips the unbroken halving on every tree. Hands +0.03 a half: 0.75 + 0.47 · 0.75 + 0.51.

Measured: the drafted rule alone was worth about 0% (the same damage in more hits); with the Pierce and the hands the Blademaster +9.0 · +4.5 · +3.6%, the Runeblade +6.8 · +2.3 · +2.8%.

Warlord · InvokerKeeper

Tier 1: her lead summon lingers and strikes every foe in farewell at her next pair. Keeper: both summons linger. Two farewells, one of each kind, on every foe: each weak foe chips one more point a pair. Hands +0.03 a half (T2B29): 0.60 + 0.49 · 0.60 + 0.53.

Measured: the second farewell alone is worth 1–3%; with the hands the Warlord +8.6 · +2.0 · +6.8%, the Invoker +8.6 · +3.6 · +3.0%.

The supports: a little of the healers' kit

Her healing scale is her higher of ATK and M.ATK ÷ (300 × the tier's multiplier), about ×1.67 on curve at Lv 50. A support is no healer: one healer a party is unchanged.

Statuses: four levels

D282's three levels, with support added between the path and widely (R2-3's "wider buffs and debuffs"). Five statuses, +10% a stack, three at most, two rounds; no retired status returns. The support level tops up (T2B24): a pair class casts her lone other-channel weapon every act, so a built-up status sat at 3 by the third round (the drafted healers' ladder put the Sentinel +34 to +50%).
