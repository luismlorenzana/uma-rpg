The trees: a Great form and a reach form, then the step

Great keeps the base's reach, ×1.3 (Pure's way). The other changes what it reaches (Dual's way). Tier 3's step is one rule: +0.1 a grade on each, +0.2 on the Orb forms, +0.1% on the Insignias. G10 values shown, Tier 1 · 2 · 3.

Rows by grade, one slot

What each wins (measured at each tier's Lv 50)

Each class in her owner party on curve, her three slots at G10 in one set, the others bare; her damage a turn against the same Uma with no accessory, on every region stage at that level (40 fights, 20 sub-bosses, 20 bosses, 4 seeds). The mean weighs fights twice. Onset needs a new rule: its cells are estimates (three Onsets × her measured first-act share), in italics. The outlined cell is each column's best.

Shading is one hue, light to dark by gain; every cell carries its number. Stat = her armor half's (a Pendant for the Brawler, the Arms hybrids and the Paladin; a Charm for the others). Twofold Orb ×3 on the pair of her kinds that wins her mean (the Summoners' Bow and Dark, the Arms Spellblade's Axe and Thunder…); with Twofold, on her first and third weapons. Great Orb on the preset's Orb kind.

Best cells (8 classes × 4 columns). Tier 2: the Great stat forms 10, Twofold 10, the Great Orb 8, the Great Insignia 7, Onset 6 (estimated), the Twofold Orb 3. Tier 3 (the step): the Great stat forms 10, Onset 8 (estimated), the Great Insignia 7, the Twofold Orb 5, the Great Orb 4, Twofold 3. A cell counts every form in its best set. Every branch someone's best in both tiers; none in more than a third. Great for a pure class, Twofold for a hybrid; the healers keep the Great form (their heals read it).
