The accessory tree: a base and its riders

From gear.md (T2G30–T2G38). Every form is its family's base (the Accessory Setting's row) plus one rider (the Accessory Inlay's), opened by the slot's grade: two riders from a slot's first Tier 2 grade, a third at G5, and in Tier 3 a step on every rider and a fourth at G5. Kindred alone gives its whole row to the party. A form is a setting: one tap, free.

The four trees

Rows by grade, one slot

`tierScaled(rate × grade)`, as today's rows round. A cell before its ring is the base's alone (a dash for a form that isn't open). Kindred's number reaches every Uma in her party.

What each wins, measured at the tier's Lv 50

Each class in her owner party on curve, her three slots at G10 in one set, the others bare; her damage a turn against the same Uma with no accessory, on every region stage at that level (40 fights, 20 sub-bosses, 20 bosses, 4 seeds); the mean weighs fights twice. Party value is the party's damage a turn (R2-11): a self form's is her gain × her share, Kindred's measured. Italic cells are estimates (her base measured, the rider × her share of the matching damage). The outlined cell is each row's best of these columns; the next table has the best of every set measured, mixes included.

One hue, light to dark by gain; every cell carries its number. Each row is a Tier 1 parent's profile (round 2, the 40-region world), standing for the classes that keep it (the Brawler's for the Berserker and the Ravager, the hybrids' for their keepers and, at a lower share, their supports, the healers' for every healer class). Round 3's crosses and pure pairs are below. Stat sets wear her armor half's family.

Best set per cell

Kindred: support from a slot

  • Her slot's +1 shared by the party's five: +0.2 ATK and +0.2 M.ATK a grade on every Uma's hits, herself included (+4 a slot at Tier 2's G10, +12 for three). Hits only, as her ★.
  • The party takes its strongest Kindred, never the sum, so two supporters don't stack. Frozen when the fight starts.
  • Measured: three Kindreds give the party +3.2–3.3% damage a turn at Tier 2 Lv 50 (Tier 3, at the same 0.2: +2.8–3.5%), whoever wears them: more than any self form gives an Uma doing under about a sixth of the party's damage (both healers, the supports), less than the damage dealers' own (+3.6–5.4%).
  • At +0.3 a grade it took half the party-value cells; at +0.2 it is the healers' and the supports' best and nobody else's.

Round 3's new profiles, measured (T2G59, T2G60)

The single-hit crosses and the pure pairs aren't built, so each is a proxy on the live engine, her parent's seat in the owner's party, on curve, three slots at G10 against the same Uma bare (48 fights, 24 sub-bosses, 24 bosses, 4 seeds). The crosses: a Brawler on Greatsword, Katana, Bow on line B, and a Sorcerer on Fire, Blizzard, Light. The pure pairs: an Arms Spellblade and an Arms Summoner with both halves on ATK. Arsenal is the Brawler's and the Sorcerer's profile with more weak hits. Each cell: fights · sub-bosses · bosses · the mean (fights twice), % of her damage a turn.

A form counted in each best set (by her damage), round 2's 32 cells and the new 16. Kindred stays the supports' and the healers' party pick (8 and 7 party cells).

D266 holds. Great becomes the most-won form, 18 and 17 of 48 cells, because every new profile reads one stat on every hit; none wins over half, and every form is still someone's best. The forms that suit: Great on one stat; the one-foe pure pair's Orb a Twofold Orb on her lead two, the area pair's a Great Orb; Tier 3's rules multiply her hits, so they move no form's rank (the keeper healers' mends and Barriers ×1.3, in Overflow's place, multiply Vow's heal lean).
