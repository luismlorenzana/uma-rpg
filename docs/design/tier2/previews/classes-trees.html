Trees by tier

Any of the twenty Tier 2 classes and the twenty-two Tier 3 classes on any kind she can slot, as her Tier 1 ancestor's tree plays it today and as Tiers 2 and 3 grow it (classes.md round 3, §4.9–§4.11, §6.2; T2C11–T2C22, T2C45, T2C51–T2C53, T2C74, T2C78–T2C81). A later class plays her ancestors' trees: the Spellfist's Tome plays the Sorcerer's, the Battlemage's Arm the Brawler's, a pure variant her parent's five trees of her channel (line C her lead half's word on every one), the Gladiator the Brawler's Arms and the Thaumaturge the Sorcerer's Tomes. Every name comes from D298's grammar; no tree or skill name is added. Dashed nodes are new; a purple ring is a crown. Other pages: the class tree · the twenty-two.
