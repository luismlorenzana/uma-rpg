The branch lists and what each is worth

From Tier 2, every node but her moon's wears a branch of its body's list, crafted once and then swapped freely (extra-stats.md, T2E21–T2E38). This page holds the rules and the measurements behind them: the live balance harness at Tier 2 Lv 50, the owner's two parties at their presets, on curve, Auto, 8 seeds of every stage at that level, only the measured Uma changed (D266's way). A Tier 2 world plays Tier 1's rules × tier today.

A node's three steps (T2E21)

The owner: unlocking is one thing, slotting the stat something else; craft and unlock, then select and swap freely. Two steps feed the farm, the third is free.

1Unlock

Its jewels, as Tier 1: a Planet node three Planet Jewels, a moon node one Moon Jewel

In order, at its level, three a rank. An open node gives Tier 1's gift × 1.8 or × 2.6. Her moon takes none.

2Slot

One Stardust of the node's grade a jewel, once (from Tier 2)

Made from Fungi at the Lapidary (professions T2P30). Until slotted the node still gives its gift: a slow Fungi line costs choice, never stats.

3Choose

Free, any time outside a fight

Any branch of its body's list. She may pick in advance on a closed or unslotted node; it wears the pick once slotted. One tap; "Wear on every node" sets a body.

One lane a body (T2E22)

A branch re-spends its own body's gift inside its purpose. No pick turns defense into offense or back, so the build budget the foes are priced on (T2B1) holds whatever she slots. On curve the back line takes 1.6–2.2% of her HP a fight: a lane-crossing row would let her cash every defense into damage.

The five new kinds, in the damage stack (T2E29–T2E33)

Her hit, step by step (WP31, battleDamage)

  1. 1Offense: her ATK or M.ATK + Might + Orb forms + ★ + Prowess's Full, Split or Trio on the hit's own kind (built: flat)
  2. 2offense² ÷ (offense + defense) × power
  3. 3Weak ×1.3
  4. 4Insignias, one factor
  5. 4bArea or Multi-hit, by the hit's cast, one factor each, every source summed: × (1 + Area) on an area hit, × (1 + Multi-hit) on a multi-hit hit, never both
  6. 5–8Statuses (a self-buff here, so it multiplies Area and Multi-hit and never stacks into them) · specials · unbroken ×0.5 · the Rift's ward
  7. 9Her evasion, then Area guard on a hit she takes from a foe's every-Uma move: × (1 − Area guard)
  8. 10Rounded, at least 1
  9. afterLife steal: her share of the damage it dealt (the foe's HP at most) heals her, rounded down, her missing HP at most
  10. roundMP back: at each round's end, with the base regen, her bar × the share, frozen as flat MP a round

Every row someone's best

The hybrids' lift

At her split, Prowess is worth 9.8–10.9% to a hybrid and 16.5–17.5% to a pure class: a split leaves a third of the points on the channel her other half reads. At her best row it is 13.0–17.5% for every damage class: +3.0 to +4.7 points for a hybrid, the only power slotting adds.

The healers

No shape reaches their one-foe skills, and Offense also lifts their mends, so it is theirs; a kind row wins some of their damage cases (the Paladin's sub-bosses, the Cleric's regular fights and sub-bosses) but not their mends.

Vigor and Guard: defense and MP (T2E25, T2E26)

On curve nobody falls (her lowest HP 87–99% a fight), so HP's worth is insurance the harness can't show; MP is what binds once fights chain.

What each Guard row cuts from the damage she takes

The back line takes almost nothing in regular fights; in sub-boss and boss fights 86–100% of it is foes' every-Uma moves, today nearly all magical. Area guard −12% ≈ 11.4% for them. Life steal 3% heals the Brawler 90 of the 186 she takes a fight.

MP in a chain: 12 stages, MP carried, no Spring

At 3% MP back beats MP ×2 for every class; at 1.5% it loses for every class over 12 stages; at 2.0% the two tie (interpolated). MP ×2 wins a short session from a Spring, MP back a long one; Springs are five a continent on a two-hour recharge.

Presets for the sixteen classes (T2E37)

The Standard preset sets every node: Prowess by her kit; the Planet, Vigor and Guard at Tier 1's gifts (the all-rounders, and HP for the fights the harness can't show). Names are classes' (T2C31–T2C64).
