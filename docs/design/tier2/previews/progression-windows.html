How a window is drawn (live, P84)

A made-up window of placeholder panes, lit by the game's own rule: a pane is drawn when its map value is the lead (0) or at most 250 × lit ÷ (steps − 1); the face (255) only when every step is lit; any other pane is dark glass, (0.3 c + 0.7 y) × 0.16 + (9, 11, 18). The map ranks panes outside in: the frame farthest from her head first, the sunburst from its rim inward, her figure bottom up. Change the step count: the same map lights any board.

The pane map (outfit-glass-order): darker first, white the face

What the art knows: only the count. It never knows which step lit, so a step that read a choice would gain nothing on the glass, and a board can change what its steps ask in any tier with no rebake. The face waits for the last step, so every step must be reachable on a normal run.

Why a step reads progress, never a choice (T2W27)

Steps latch for good (CO2): the first time her state meets one, it's hers, dated that day. Play a made-up Uma: real steps light as she progresses and never go dark; the red, dashed tile is a step the board does not have, one that would read a free pick, to show what latching does to it.

Each new system, and where the board reads it (T2W24)

A choice counts only as breadth any option meets equally: "two of one line", "three classes", "five weapons", "her three Crafts to 60". No step names a class, a type, a form, a stat or a profession. A choice celebrated without gating is CO30's medal phase.

Craft: her three professions together (T2W26)

Craft I–V ask her three Craft Levels to add up to 12 · 24 · 36 · 48 · 60. Craft EXP a level is nearly flat (1,425 + 15 × level), so spreading costs no more than Tier 1's single Craft; a specialist pays at most 14%, because her last ten levels must come from a second profession. Days are her own slot time, nonstop (2 EXP a base minute, the places' ×1.25).
