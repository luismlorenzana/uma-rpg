Highlighted slots change at the ring that opens them. The Orb on presetKind's kind: her first weapon's, a Summoner's lead that twins her lone weapon; the one-foe pure pair's a Twofold Orb on her lead two (T2G59). Below a slot's G5 a support wears her keeper's set; Vow opens at Tier 3's G5.

Her ★ family first

From her first Tier 2 grade a Twofold form of her ★ family gives both stats, so the preset puts her ★ first for every class (T2G35). On a one-stat class the Twofold's other stat is unread, but its base and her ★ still beat a Great: a Spellfist whose ★ is the Charm takes +18 ATK and her ★ +9 for a Great Pendant's +23.
