The Ascend sheet: promotion into Tier 2 and Tier 3

Each Ascend is her promotion. Into Tier 2 she picks a child of a Tier 1 class she brought to Class Rank 50 (two or three each: 6 to 20 open); into Tier 3 a Tier 3 child of a Tier 2 class she brought to Rank 50 (one each, two for the Spellfist and the Battlemage: 3 to 22). Her weapons and their types come on the same page, every pick pre-filled, so a party of five goes up in one tap (T2W16, T2W33–T2W41).

Play it: a party of five, and two more

Party 1 is the owner's first comp from D263 in Tier 1 (Brawler, Sorcerer, Arms Spellblade, Tome Summoner, Paladin), all five ready; into Tier 3 the same five come from their Tier 2 classes. Uma F is ready in another party but at work; Uma G is ready alone. Tap a row to open her page; pick a class, a type, a line; Back returns. The comps on the right set Party 1 in one go: his two swaps, "a Brawler with a Spellblade" and "a Spellblade with the Paladin" (R3-7).

Direction

Party 1's comp (R3-7)

Roles by T2W44: damage, support, heal. Kinds count her three slots and every two-kind weapon's partner (one a loadout, the Gladiator and the Thaumaturge two). The check line on the sheet says the same in six words or fewer.

What the sheet writes, per Uma (T2W33, T2W38, T2W21)

What she keeps, picks and restarts (T2W21)

Picks are settings, set or confirmed on the sheet; progress restarts. Into Tier 3 she picks again: a new class, its preset and Suggested types, her forms and stat picks carried as settings.

The classes open to her (T2W33, T2W36, T2W38)

Into Tier 2: every child of every Tier 1 class at Class Rank 50

Two for the Brawler, the Sorcerer and the healers; three for each hybrid. Class V guarantees three Tier 1 classes at R50, so 6 to 9 open at least; all eight give all twenty. Read before she leaves, written to the new world, fixed but for the door (T2C48).

Into Tier 3: the Tier 3 children of every Tier 2 class at Class Rank 50

One each, two for the Spellfist and the Battlemage. Tier 2's Class V guarantees three, so 3 to 5 open; all twenty give all twenty-two. Fixed but for the Tier 3 door (T2C90: a held Tier 3 class at Class Rank 20 opens every Tier 3 class of her Tier 1 line and its reclass neighbours' lines).

Suggested, in both tiers (T2W36)

The child of the class she fights as, when that class is at R50 and of her family: into Tier 2 its keeper, into Tier 3 its Tier 3 child (the Spellfist's and the Battlemage's kept child, the Spellhammer and the Spellknight). Else the same child of her family's first R50 class in the class tree's order; else of her first R50 class (a kin class only when none of her family stands at R50). So a promotion with the defaults changes nothing the owner didn't pick, and every other card is one tap away.

Umas already in a later world at its switch (T2W23, T2W40)

Tier 2: each opens every child of the class she fights as and of every Tier 1 class she has at R50 there, and becomes her class's keeper at that class's Class Rank. Tier 3: each becomes her Tier 1 class's keeper's Tier 3 child (a Brawler the Ravager, a Paladin the Bastion) at its Class Rank, and opens the Tier 3 children of the keepers of every Tier 1 class she has at R50 there. Suggested types; "New: Tier 2 classes" or "New: Tier 3 classes" until her Class tab opens; no sheet forced; her lit steps stay (CO2).

Ascending into Tier 3 before its toolbox (T2W41)

Tier 2's toolbox ships first. From its switch the Tier 3 Ascend writes her Tier 3 open set and the Tier 2 class she ascended as; she fights as its Tier 1 ancestor until Tier 3's switch ("Tier 3 classes arrive with Tier 3's update"), then becomes that class's Tier 3 child at the ancestor's Class Rank. Nothing she earned in Tier 2 is lost.
