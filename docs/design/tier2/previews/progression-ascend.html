Promotion on the Ascend sheet

From Tier 2, the Ascend is her promotion (R2-5, R2-6): on the Gallery's Ascend sheet each ready Uma picks her Tier 2 class, one of the two children of a Tier 1 class she brought to Class Rank 50, and her three weapons' Tier 2 types. Every pick is pre-filled, so a party of five still goes in one tap. Every Uma here is made up; the sixteen classes are shown by role (their names are classes.md's); the weapon types are round 1's Pure and Dual, assumed until gear.md's round 2.

Play it: a party of five, and two more

Party 1 is the owner's first comp from D263 (Brawler, Sorcerer, Arms Spellblade, Tome Summoner, Paladin), all five ready. Uma F is ready in another party but at work; Uma G is ready alone. Direction

Party 1's comp (R2-12)

Roles by T2B's party value (R2-11): damage, support, heal. Kinds count her three slots and a Dual's partner (one Dual a loadout, T2G3). The check line on the sheet says the same in six words or fewer.

What the sheet writes, per Uma (T2W17, T2W21)

What she keeps, picks and restarts (T2W21)

Picks are settings, set or confirmed on the sheet; progress restarts. It reconciles the cross-reference's XR5: going up from Tier 1 there is nothing of Tier 2's to carry, so the sheet sets it; going up from Tier 2, what she had pre-fills the same pages.

The classes open to her (T2W17, T2W18)

Both children of every Tier 1 class at Class Rank 50

Class V guarantees three Tier 1 classes at R50, so at least 6 open; all eight give all sixteen. The sheet reads her Tier 1 ranks before she leaves (she arrives at Class Rank 1) and writes her open set into Tier 2, fixed for the tier: no Tier 1 class fights there, so there's no parent left to rank. She switches freely among them on her Class tab, each on its own Class Rank; Ranks 1–10 rise together (XR1).

Suggested: the keeper of the class she fights as

When that class is at R50; else the keeper of her highest-ranked R50 class (ties: her family's, then the tree's order). So a promotion with the defaults changes nothing the owner didn't pick: Tier 1's comps arrive as Tier 1's comps, and the expander is one tap away.

Umas already in a Tier 2 world at its switch (T2W23)

Before round 2 is built an ascended Uma plays her Tier 1 class × tier. When Tier 2's toolbox switches on, each opens both children of the class she fights as and of every Tier 1 class she has at R50 there, and becomes her class's keeper at that class's Class Rank: nothing lost, no sheet forced. Other open classes stand at Rank 1 (or the shared ladder's); her weapons keep their kinds with Suggested types; her sub-profession is Suggested. Her Gallery tile and Window sheet say "New: Tier 2 classes" until her Class tab is opened. Her lit steps stay (CO2).
