Where slots bind

Full windows a day by roster size, when the roster runs at its hours. Tier 2 at four slots a place would stop at 46 Umas; at eight it runs to 91, Tier 1's ceiling on Tier 1's work.

A 24-party world (120 Umas): Tier 1 83.7 days for every full window; Tier 2 83.7 with eight slots, 167.3 with four.

The fighting party's grades

The day each grade is held, a world of twenty at work (today's callings), the party's needs first. A grade opens when one Uma has worked its branch's profession from the start.

The party holds each grade within 1.3 days of its opening in Tier 2 (0.8 in Tier 1): Craft Level, not volume, is its wall. The run's whole set of windows is volume-bound.
