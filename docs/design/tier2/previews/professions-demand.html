One Uma, by tier and line

Refined items. A full window's five weapons split 2.5 Arms and 2.5 Tomes on a roster's average, so Metal and Vegetables carry 250 and the rest 150. The same in every tier: only what the items raise scales (T2P2).

Per item beside the totals: an Arms Gem or Tome Ink's chain is 8h 02m of slot time at Craft Lv 25, Armor Fittings 2h 25m, a Setting or a jewel 4h 01m.
