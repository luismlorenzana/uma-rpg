Ten branches, two a line

The top row is Tier 1's materials as built; the bottom row is new in Tier 2 (dashed), each from an uma-art family the game doesn't use yet. Each branch is its own profession with its own Craft Level (T2P31).

Twenty grades a line

Every grade opens at Craft Lv 5g − 4 of its own profession and is worn at Uma Lv 5g − 4. A made item takes its branch's refined items of its own grade (FL7); a jewel's and a Stardust's grade is its node's (Planet n is 2n − 1, a moon's node n 2n).

What a grade takes from Tier 2

Every grade of every piece takes its line's two items of that grade, one tap (T2P29). The second item powers the path she has set, whatever it is, so a path swaps freely and every branch carries the same work.
