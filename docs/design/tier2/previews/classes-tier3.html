The twenty-two Tier 3 classes

Round 3 (classes.md §6, T2C65 and T2C78–T2C92): Tier 3 is a promotion. Every Tier 2 class promotes into one Tier 3 class with a new name, the Spellfist and the Battlemage into two. A Tier 3 class keeps her Tier 2 parent's kit and rule and adds one rule from Class Rank 1; at Class Rank 50 she is crowned. Gains are analytic, for balance to measure next. "More varied physical weapons": Arsenal (T2C85)

The Gladiator (from the Spellfist)

The Thaumaturge (from the Battlemage)

The rule: gear allows one two-kind weapon in a class's slots (T2G3); the Gladiator and the Thaumaturge may wear two, so three weapons carry all five kinds of her channel. Every Tier 2–3 foe is weak to at least one Arm and one element (the live dealer), so every foe has a weak kind in her hands, against about 90% for a class with one Dual. Her two-kind weapons hit weak at ×1.5 (Tempered or Evolved, G10), not the Keen ×1.7: a little less on her best kinds, every foe in reach, and every foe she touches chips. Hand ×1.05; analytic gain over her parent +5.4% and +5.2%.

Tier 3's budget and band (T2C92)

Round 2 measured the crowns and masteries at +17% at Tier 3 Lv 50. Round 3 keeps the crown on every class and puts one rule in each mastery's place: the keepers' line (T1: Ravager, Astromancer, Bladelord, Hierarch, Bastion) is round 2's crowned C1 exactly, measured 4.82 BT a round. The band: each damage and support class's gain over her Tier 2 parent within ±5 points of the eighteen's mean; the healers on the stress check.

Gains analytic: her rule's step on her damage × the seat's pass-through (0.55 a pure seat, 0.85 a hybrid's: round 2's measured ratios); the supports' from round 2's measured keepers' gains. Balance re-sizes Tier 3's term below Lv 40 by about +0.03.
