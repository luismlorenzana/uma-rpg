The star chart with branches

The live Stars tab, star for star, as extra-stats.md grows it. A node has three steps (T2E21): its jewels open it, as Tier 1, and it gives Tier 1's gift; from Tier 2 one Stardust a jewel slots it, once; then it wears any branch of its body's list, swapped freely. The map is Tier 1's 22 nodes in every tier: round 1's weapon moon is Prowess's kind rows now (T2E27). Tap a star, its Branch row, a crest, Set all, or Branches under the totals.

A node's three steps

  • Unlocking is Tier 1's in every tier: a Planet node takes three Planet Jewels, a moon node one Moon Jewel, in order, at its level. An open node gives Tier 1's gift (× 1.8, × 2.6). Her moon takes none.
  • Slotting, from Tier 2: one Stardust of the node's grade a jewel (a Planet node three, a moon node one), once (professions T2P30). Her moon never.
  • Choosing is free: the Branch row opens the body's list; a tap wears a row and the sheet returns to the node. Wear on every node sets the body in one more tap. An unslotted or closed node keeps her pick and wears it once slotted.
  • A white diamond at a star's lower right: its branch isn't Tier 1's gift.

The lists (T2E23–T2E27, T2E44)

  • Planet: All six · DEF and M.DEF · HP and MP. ATK and M.ATK stay on every row; the doubled pair takes the other pair's points.
  • Prowess: Offense (her split) · Area +1.5% a point · Multi-hit +0.6% a point · Full (1.5 × the points on one kind) · Split (0.7 of Full on each of two) · Trio in Tier 3 (half of Full on all three).
  • Vigor: HP and MP · HP · MP · MP back (+0.1 … +0.7% of her bar a round) · Life steal (0.2 … 1%).
  • Guard: DEF and M.DEF · DEF · M.DEF · Area guard (−0.8 … −4% on foes' every-Uma moves).
  • ★ Her moon: no branch: her template is read, never picked.

Presets (T2E51, T2E38)

A class's preset slots Prowess by her kit, measured on the harness: Offense for every one-stat class (round 3's four pure variants included) and the healers, Multi-hit for the Spellblades' keepers and supports, Area for the Summoners'. The Planet, Vigor and Guard keep Tier 1's gifts. Prowess's kind rows list her kit's top pair first (the Brawler's Lance + Axe, not her Orb's Greatsword). The Branches sheet puts her preset, or Tier 1's gifts, on every node in one tap.

Every tier at once

A Brawler's full map at each tier's Lv 50, slotted, on a mix of branches, drawn from the same table: the chart never changes shape.

Four states and a mark

Colour says which body, fill says how far along, the diamond says a branch other than the gift. Green stays Ready's alone (PP21, PP23): Ready is a jewel or a Stardust she can set now.

The geometry, checked

Computed on this page from the live table, as star-chart.test.ts computes it, plus the one check branches add: every mark inside its star's 44 pt target and off every other star. At 375 wide the chart is 343 px, so a viewBox unit is 0.953 px and 44 pt is 46.2 units.
