The star chart in Tiers 1, 2 and 3

The live Stars tab's chart, star for star, with the weapon moons added where extra-stats.md puts them (T2E9): on an outer orbit below the core. Tier 1's 22 stars never move; Tier 2's chart grows to 360 × 470 for one crescent, Tier 3's to 360 × 500 for two. Every check the live star-chart.test.ts makes runs below on the tier shown. Tap a star, a crest, Set all, or a weapon moon's Kinds row.

What each tier adds to the chart

  • Tier 1 As live: the Planet on its ring, Prowess, Vigor and Guard on one orbit of 132, her moon's pair at the top right. 22 stars, 360 × 350.
  • Tier 2 One weapon moon, a steel crescent straight below the Planet on an outer orbit of 172, its label its kinds. 27 stars, 360 × 470.
  • Tier 3 Two, steel and lavender, on one outer orbit of 220 at 90° ± 22°, labels from the edges. 32 stars, 360 × 500.

The rules this page plays

  • Three jewels a rank in every tier (T2E2): an odd rank's three Planet Jewels open the Planet (3 · 2 · 1 by tier) and each weapon moon (1); an even rank's three Moon Jewels open the three moons. Her moon takes none.
  • A weapon moon's numerals are the Planet's: I at Lv 1, II at 11, III at 21, IV at 31, V at 41. A Planet Jewel III opens Planet III or a weapon moon's III.
  • Set all keeps its live rule: each jewel to the body furthest behind among those it fits, map order on a tie.
  • Kinds, one tap (T2E5): six rows from her three weapons; a tap wears the row and the sheet returns to the node with its new values.
  • Values are the live code's Tier 1 points × 1.8 and × 2.6, rounded per node; a Split node is 0.7 of the Full one.

Why not the top-left corner

The profile polish kept the corner empty for the weapon moons (PP31). Under its own tests it holds one five-star body, but its label has one line between Prowess's label and Vigor's: room for "Greatsword 5/5", not "Greatsword · Lightning 5/5", and no room for Tier 3's second moon. The corner stays free for a later body (T2E19).
