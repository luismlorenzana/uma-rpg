What opens when, in every later tier

The Ascend sheet

  • Her Tier 2 class (a keeper or an expander), then her three weapons and each one's type, Suggested pre-set.
  • She arrives at G0: Pure at ×1.30, a Dual's partner at ×1.10.

A grade's raise (Lv 5g − 4)

  • Two items a grade: Might or a stat from the first, the type or the rider from the second.
  • A slot's G1: the tree's first ring (Great and its sibling).

A slot's G5 (Lv 21)

  • Tier 2: Kindred, Seeker, Finisher.
  • Tier 3: Vow, Limit Orb, Apex; Tier 2's forms open from G1 with their step.

Any time, outside fights

  • Swap a weapon's type or kind, or a slot's form: one tap, free.
  • One two-kind weapon in her slots; the party takes its strongest Kindred.
