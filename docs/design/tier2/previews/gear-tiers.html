What a grade's G5 opens, in every tier

G1–G4 (Uma Lv 1–16)

  • One made item raises one slot one grade, as Tier 1.
  • A weapon: its Tier 1 line in Tier 2; its Tier 2 branch at Tier 3's Might in Tier 3.
  • An accessory slot: the four bases (Tier 2); the forms she knows at their Tier 2 rate (Tier 3).

G5 (Uma Lv 21)

  • A weapon's G5 item takes its branch: Pure or Dual (Tier 2); Keen, Tempered or Evolved (Tier 3).
  • An accessory slot's G5 Setting opens its family's two branches (Tier 2) or their step (Tier 3). No item.
  • Armor: nothing new.

G6–G10 (Uma Lv 26–46)

  • The line's items as before, × tier.
  • One two-kind weapon in her slots; her ★ family pays on any weapons (a Twofold form).
  • A re-forge is one more G5 item; a form swap is one tap.
