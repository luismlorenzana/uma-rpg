The weapon moon as a pick

Round 1 added a weapon moon in Tier 2 and a second in Tier 3. Round 2 makes it Prowess's kind rows (T2E27): Full puts 1.5 × the node's points on one of her three kinds, Split 0.7 of that on each of a pair, and Tier 3 adds Trio, half of Full on all three. The map stays 22 nodes in every tier, as progression (T2W24) assumes. From extra-stats.md §4.7.

Round 1: a body

  • 27 and 32 nodes, the Planet sharing its rank's jewels, an outer orbit on the chart, a crescent crest to draw.
  • Added power: Full +90, Split +63 each on top of the whole map (+11.8–15.2% of her damage at her best pick).
  • Its kinds set for the whole moon; Tier 3 a second, Full + Split.

Round 2: Prowess's rows

  • 22 nodes in every tier; the chart, the jewels and the window's Stars track as built.
  • No added power: a kind row re-spends Prowess's points (+81 Full, +57 Split each at Tier 2), so it competes with Offense, Area and Multi-hit (the lane rule, T2E22).
  • Node by node; Tier 3's Trio serves all three of her weapons (EP26's aim).

Every pick, measured

Each class's damage a turn with five nodes on one pick, against none, at round 1's sizes; × 0.9 for the kind rows. Her best pick is marked, her preset's Orb kind tagged. Choose a kind of fight above.

Her kit's kinds against her Orb's

Round 1's preset put the moon on her Orb's kind (presetKind) and her other weapon of its half. Her kit's damage says otherwise for four classes, so the kind rows list her top pair first (T2E38): Split wins when her two top kinds are close, Full when her top kind deals over about 2⅓ times her second.

The kind rows against Prowess's other rows

At five nodes, her damage a turn (D266's mean, regular fights twice). The kind rows win where Offense is weak and her shapes don't fit: the Summoners' sub-bosses and bosses, and the healers' damage (their mends read Offense).

Tier 3's Trio (T2E44)

Half of the node's Full on each of her three kinds, 1.5 of Full in all against Split's 1.4. From the Tier 2 picks (a flat on a kind scales linearly): Trio ties Split for an even kit (the Sorcerer 12.6% against 12.8%, the Tome Spellblade 11.8% against 11.8%), wins the Arms Spellblade's bosses (13.7% against 13.6%), and loses wherever two kinds carry her. The even kit's row; no preset's.

No kind cap (T2E6)

The kind rows sit outside T2G14's cap on Orb forms, as round 1's moons did. The most on one kind is three Orb forms and five Full nodes: at Tier 3, 3 × the Great Orb and +117, under the engine's 1,000 a kind. Never a heal, never a weakness: a kind row reads a hit's own kind, its Limit Breaks' too.
