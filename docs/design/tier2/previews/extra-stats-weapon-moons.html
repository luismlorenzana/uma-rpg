The weapon moons

Tier 2's one and Tier 3's two: five nodes of flat offense on her hits of a kind, Full on one or Split at 0.7 on two, on the Planet's ranks and its jewels. What they add beside her Might and her Orbs, how she picks their kinds in one tap, and what the model says they're worth. From extra-stats.md §4–§5.

Beside Might and the Orbs, and why no kind cap

A Brawler's Greatsword offense at each tier's Lv 50 (the balance party's average outfit, on-curve G10, the whole map, no template). A weapon moon joins her hit's offense in the damage stack's step 1, as an Orb does; it never heals and never adds a kind. The most any one kind can carry is three Orbs and a Full moon: about 30% of her offense on it, so no cap is needed (T2E6).

Full or Split

A Full moon gives its kind about 1.4 times what a Split gives each of its two. So Split wins while her damage spreads, and Full wins once her Full kind carries enough of it. Slide her lead kind's share of her damage; her other two share the rest evenly. (A simpler spread than §4.9's, which weights her weapons by her hands: for the crossover only. The per-hit gains are the model's, Tier 2's Lv 50.)

Every node, Full and Split

Opens at Lv 1, 11, 21, 31, 41 (the Planet's ranks), one Planet Jewel a node in every tier. A Split's two kinds share the five nodes and five jewels.

What the model says they add

The model (a draft for balance.md's simulation): a hit is offense² ÷ (offense + defense) against a regular striker at the tier's Lv 50; her act is her three weapons evenly, each at her hand for its half. Every gain holds within 0.3 points across tiers, since SB1 scales both sides. Hover a bar; the table holds every number.

Her kinds, in one tap

      Set all, with the Planet's jewels shared

      Set all keeps its live rule (T2E13): each jewel to the body furthest behind among those it fits, map order on a tie. On the Planet's side the Planet comes first in map order; when the stock can't cover its count, the weapon moons take the jewel, so nothing that fits is left.
