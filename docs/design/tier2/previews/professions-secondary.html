Three professions and the sub-profession

professions.md (T2P31–T2P36). From Tier 2 each branch is its own profession with its own Craft Level, and a job pays its EXP to the profession of the material it works. An Uma levels three: her trade's two branches, fixed by her calling, and one sub-profession, a branch of another line she picks and changes freely. It replaces round 1's secondary line (R2-9).

She levels what she works

Each profession on Tier 1's curve: 735 job-hours (24.5 days nonstop) to Lv 50. The work her own window takes in Tier 2 levels her trade's two to Lv 50, as Tier 1's work levels its one.

Work to EXP is Tier 1's ratio: 954 job-hours for one Craft Level in Tier 1, 1,908 for two professions in Tier 2 (1.30 either way). Her sub-profession is what she gives beyond her own line.

The Craft track, for progression

Her window's Craft steps are progression's (R2-10). The recommendation reads Tier 1's rule on her best of three (T2P42).

Nonstop days in her own slot. Her own full window's farm is 63.6 slot-days of work, so either rule fits inside a run; the second delays the fighting party's windows past Tier 1's floor.

A Tier 2 run of twenty, set up

Twenty at today's callings (4 · 3 · 5 · 3 · 5), working for their own full windows. Without sub-professions the three Healers bind at 0.18 full windows a day (111.6 days). With them the roster runs at its own hours, 0.31 a day: 63.6 days. Suggested names the lines in the order they're short.

Each line's need a day against its own trade

Six Wood and Stone Umas take sub-professions: three all day, three a few hours. The trade's own Umas spread their hours over their two branches as each needs.

Why this shape

  • A branch, not a line: the owner's three professions, literally (her line's two and one more); a whole other line would be four.
  • A job pays its own profession: "gain Exp differently for each branch". A share to the others would make the sub-profession a formality; a rate by branch would break the ten-branch rule.
  • No reserved grades: a new sub-profession starts at Lv 1 (G5 on day 8.8 of its own work, G10 on 22.1), so the trade's Umas reach the top grades first without a rule.
  • Free to change, its level kept: ascending one Uma at a time moves head counts every few days; a fee or a reset only taxes balancing, and a kept level is no exploit (the hours are the same whichever branch levels).
  • Suggested by hands over work: the trade's Umas count half (each works two branches), the work is a full window's refined from the branch (250 on the weapon lines' four, 150 on the rest).
