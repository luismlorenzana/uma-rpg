The secondary line

professions.md (T2P12–T2P17). From Tier 2 an Uma may hold one secondary: another line's three places, gather, process and make, at G1–G8. G9 and G10 stay that line's own trade's. One Craft Level for both; free to change; cleared on Ascend.

A Tier 2 run of twenty, set up

Twenty at today's callings (Metal 4 · Vegetables 3 · Wood 5 · Fish 3 · Stone 5), each working toward her full window. The dashed box is what the line needs a day at 0.63 full windows a day; the bars are who gives it, in job-hours.

Its own trade (line colour)Secondaries from Wood and Stone

Without secondaries the Healers' three bind: 0.36 full windows a day, 55.8 days for the twenty. With them: 0.63 a day, 31.8 days. Six of the ten Wood and Stone Umas hold secondaries; two work theirs all day, four split their days. Each line's G9–G10 (a fifth: 32 job-hours a day on Vegetables) stays inside its own trade's hours.

Why this shape

A line, not a place

A thin trade needs all three of its places. One pick covers a trio; a one-place secondary would need three Umas for one line and twelve choices instead of four.

G9 and G10 stay the trade's

The top two grades are a fifth of every line's work, the share the old draft kept for "the main one's higher tiers" (PP15), and they open last, so the trade keeps its mastery.

Free to change

Umas ascend one at a time, so a world's head counts move every few days. A fee (the old 1,000 Laurels × tier) would only tax keeping the lines balanced.
