Weaknesses and shields: one gate, two rules

WK8 kept, changedSB6 kept, changedT2B7 · T2B8

WK8 gives every Tier 2–3 foe three kinds. On today's weapons that leaves a member nothing weak on one stage in six, the owner's original complaint. With T2G's two-kind weapon the answers return to Tier 1's. So the lists narrow, and the shields grow, at the level her weapons widen.
