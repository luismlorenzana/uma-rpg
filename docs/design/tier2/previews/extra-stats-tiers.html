Extra Stats from Tier 1 to Tier 3

Tier 1's map as built, × 1.8 and × 2.6 in the tiers above, with one weapon moon in Tier 2 and two in Tier 3. They sit on the Planet's ranks and share its jewels, so every rank sets three jewels in every tier and an Uma's map always takes 30 jewels and 150 refined Stone. From extra-stats.md.

At a glance

Three jewels a rank, in every tier

A rank is one grade; the jewels are the same ten in every tier. Odd ranks are the Planet's side: in Tier 2 the Planet gives up one of its three Planet Jewels to the weapon moon, in Tier 3 one more to the second. Even ranks are the three moons', and her moon opens with their II and IV.

Every node, every tier

Points a node; HP is 5 a point. Tier 2 and 3 are Tier 1 × 1.8 and × 2.6, rounded per node (the live tierScaled); a Split node is 0.7 of the Full one, rounded. The weapon moons' Tier 1 row is their size before the tier: they exist only from Tier 2.

What the map grants at Lv 50, by class

Every node open, her moon apart (mapPoints on the core). HP, MP, DEF and M.DEF are the same for every class; Prowess splits ATK and M.ATK by her active class. The weapon moons are her preset's (T2E7).

Level by level

Something opens every five levels in every tier. Left: a stat with its moon (the Planet and a full moon: HP, MP, DEF and M.DEF for every class). Right: the weapon moons, which open on the Planet's levels and finish at Lv 41. Hover for the values; the tables above hold every number.

Her moon by tier

Two nodes in every tier, opened with the three moons' II and IV, no jewel. Node I reads her knack, node II her calling's root. Fleet and Gritty are shares and stay flat. Her uniqueness as a whole is templates.md's (T2U); these are the code's values.

Her window's Stars track

The step ids and the record don't change (T2E14); each step reads the tier's map. "The whole map" means every node of it.

The Lapidary's demand

One maxed Uma at Craft Lv 25's manual times (the ledger, professions-v4.sim.ts): every place 50.2 h, 753 h in all, each trade 20%. Three rules for the weapon moons' jewels, side by side.
