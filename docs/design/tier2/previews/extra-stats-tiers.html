Three jewels and three Stardust a rank

A rank is one grade. Tier 1's three jewels in every tier (D264); from Tier 2 each jewel brings one Stardust of its grade, which slots its node (professions T2P30). Her moon takes neither. Stone's and Fungi's work stay equal, 30 refined a rank on two Umas.

Every branch, every node

Each node's value by its numeral. A doubled pair takes twice the node's points; Split and Trio round from the Full node; MP back is a fifteenth of a percent a point, rounded to a tenth. The default branch (Tier 1's gift) is first on each body.

Prowess's Offense for a Brawler (100/0); other splits below.

Prowess's Offense by class, five nodes

Offense splits by her class as Tier 1's Prowess does (PROWESS_SPLIT), on round 3's forty-two classes (T2E50): a class whose every hit reads one stat puts all of Prowess on it (the Brawler's and the Sorcerer's lines, the four pure variants, the Gladiator and the Thaumaturge); every other class keeps her Tier 1 ancestor's split.
