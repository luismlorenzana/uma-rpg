The Ascend sheet's weapons step

Progression owns the sheet (T2W19, T2W39); this is what gear puts on it. Pick a tier and a class; tap a slot to see its type list with what each answers. Into Tier 3 the page pre-fills the new class's Suggested types; "As in Tier 2" maps hers.

Answers: her share of the tier's regulars with this type, her other slots on Suggested. One tap wears a row.

Types by grade: the second item raises the type

A later grade takes an Arms Gem (or Tome Ink) for Might and an Arms Whetstone (or Tome Reagent) for the type, +0.02 on its weak multiplier a grade. A Dual's own kind keeps ×1.3; its partner's weak hits grow from ×1.1.

Lines: Pure, Dual's partner (its own kind ×1.3 flat), Keen, Tempered and Evolved, both kinds. Tier 1's ×1.3 dashed.

The partner table (kept from round 1)

Every partner is of its weapon's own channel and the other group, so every two-kind weapon bridges Ring (Heavy, Sweep, Scatter) and Light/Dark (Double, Volley). One two-kind weapon in her slots in every later tier; the Gladiator and the Thaumaturge two (Arsenal, T2G54).

Suggested types for the twenty (T2G50, T2G51)

One two-kind weapon or none, searched over every mix of the owner's two parties (each seat any child of its Tier 1 class: 144 mixes) for the fewest kinds in one hand, under the coverage bound (T2G52). Round 2's keepers and battle healers keep their Dual; the Enchanter, the Marshal and the Animist their keeper sibling's, as balance's measure asks (T2B66: the search's Pure ×3 and Blizzard + Light cost them their band). New marks round 3's changes.

Suggested types for the twenty-two (T2G56)

The same rule on Tier 3's 324 mixes, every class searched. The four pure Tier 3 lines go Keen ×3: the fix for Arsenal (T2G55, its page).

Coverage in Tier 2: the bound, per party (T2G52, T2G53)

Live weakness dealer, every region stage of Tier 2 on today's honeycomb (2,880 stages: about 12,180 regulars, 240 sub-bosses, 260 bosses). The shields' pricing: sub-bosses 7 while answers ≤ 4.66, bosses 9 while ≤ 4.48 (CP8's rule, T2B7).

The bound in Tier 2, on balance's kits (T2B66): both parties' means under their Tier 1 party (4.25 against 4.31, 4.27 against 4.35); 138 of the 144 mixes inside the shields' pricing, six bosses at its edge (4.48–4.51: a Sentinel or a Marshal beside the Berserker and the Archmage), accepted, since every fix through other types moves a kit balance measured (T2G53). The swap comps put one to three kinds in one hand, the price of single big hits; C10 drops Thunder, which the party hint names. Tier 3 and Arsenal: their own page.
