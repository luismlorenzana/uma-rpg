Types by grade: the second item raises the type

A Tier 2 grade takes an Arms Gem (or Tome Ink) for Might and an Arms Whetstone (or Tome Reagent) for the type, +0.02 on its weak multiplier a grade. A Dual's own kind keeps ×1.3; its partner's weak hits grow from ×1.1.

Lines: Pure, Dual's partner (its own kind ×1.3 flat), Keen, Tempered and Evolved, both kinds. Tier 1's ×1.3 dashed.

The partner table (kept from round 1)

Every partner is of its weapon's own channel and the other group, so every two-kind weapon bridges Ring (Heavy, Sweep, Scatter) and Light/Dark (Double, Volley). One two-kind weapon in her slots in every later tier.

Suggested types for the sixteen

Weapons are classes.md's presets (T2C54); gear sets the types. Suggested is the Dual that leaves the fewest kinds in one hand over all 64 keeper and expander mixes of the owner's two parties.

Coverage: answers a regular (sub-boss · boss)

Live weakness dealer, every region stage of a tier (2,400 stages: about 10,100 regulars, 200 sub-bosses, 220 bosses). Tier 1: Party 1 4.32, Party 2 4.35; with every kind graded and re-picked every stage, 5.00.

The bound holds: every comp answers a regular at or under Tier 1's 4.32–4.35; the Tier 1-like comps keep every kind in two hands, the expander comps one to three kinds in one hand (classes' presets hold the Greatsword three or four times). Re-typing every stage (free) lifts a party about 0.4; Tier 1's free kind swaps already reach 5.00 with every kind graded. Sub-bosses and bosses gain about 15%, so their shields rise one from Tier 2 Lv 1 (T2B7).
