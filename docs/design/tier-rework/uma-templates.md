## 1. Today

What an Uma's racing profile does in play today ([classes](../../game/classes.md), [loops](../../game/loops.md)):

- **Her running style picks her starting class** (`save/class.ts` `startingClass`, D162, D180): Front → Brawler, Pace →
  Mage, Late → Paladin when her base Power beats her Wit, else Cleric, End → Summoner. Spell Blade is never a start. An Uma
  who never picked fights as her style's class (`styleClass`).
- **Base stats and growth rates make her RPG stats** (`projectRpgStats`): each is base ÷ 5 + lifetime levels × 1.5 ×
  (1 + growth). Real variance, which nobody reads as identity.
- **Distance and surface do nothing.** `STYLE_CLASSES`, `DISTANCE_GEAR` and `learningTime` (`roster/aptitudes.ts`)
  feed the Racing tab's "Learns fastest" rows, leftovers of retired training (D102, D139).
- **Her unique skill:** text on the Racing tab, nothing in play. [Q10](../../open-questions.md) sketched distance as a
  hard limit and the original's skills as passives; variance U7 replaced that sketch, and this doc keeps it replaced.
- **Master data by outfit id** (uma-platform `Outfit`): `aptitudes` (`surface` turf, dirt; `distance` sprint, mile,
  medium, long; `strategy` front, pace, late, end; grades S–G), base stats per star (`stats.star1`–`star5`, her
  starting stars `rarity`), `growthRatePercents` (whole percent), `skills`. The rule reads the first three only.
- **Stable filters** (`STABLE_FACETS`, `album/rows.ts`): Collection, Aptitudes (surface, distance, style at A+), RPG
  (class, can upgrade). No template facet.

## 2. The template

### 2.1 What a template is (UT2)

**An Uma Template is three readings of her racing profile, and the five pieces they feed.** It is ours: assigned by one
rule, named in our own words, the same for her in every tier.

| Reading | From | Decides |
|---|---|---|
| **Calling** | Her best running style, with base Power against Wit; or her one-time pick (Pace, Late) | Her starting class, her trade, her channel (node II's defense), which class her Flourish and tip work in |
| **Stride** | The longest distance she runs at her best grade | Her Flourish's shape and her signature tip |
| **Knack** | Her top growth rate | Her moon's node I and her signature accessory's family |

**What a template never does:** add a damage kind, sit on a weapon, change a stat lean, a hand or a class's rules, speed
EXP, ranks, jobs or a profession, give a party synergy, read the original's skills, or take anything away. A template is
never a malus. It is outside the foe budget: the balance party has none (XR12).

### 2.2 The assignment rule (UT3)

One pure function of the outfit's master data, read by **stable id** (`umaTemplate(outfit, pick?)`), never a list of
Umas or names. Kept from variance U4–U7, re-run on today's release (2026-10-10): the same counts.

| Step | Reads | Rule | Ties |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Calling** | `aptitudes.strategy`, `aptitudes.distance`; base Power and Wit at her starting stars (`stats.star{rarity}`) | The first row below that fits; then her pick, if she has one | Styles in today's order (Front, Pace, Late, End); Power = Wit goes magical |
| **2. Stride** | `aptitudes.distance` | The **longest** distance at her best grade: Sprint or Mile → **Dash**, Medium → **Middle**, Long → **Stayer** | None: the longest wins |
| **3. Knack** | `growthRatePercents`; base stats | Her **highest** growth rate | The higher base stat, then Speed, Stamina, Power, Guts, Wit |
| **4. Template** | — | Calling × stride → her **type**; knack → its **word**: "{knack word} {type}" | — |
| **5. Family** | Her knack | Fleet → Bell, Mighty → Orb, Tireless → Pendant, Gritty and Clever → Insignia (UT7) | — |

**The calling, row by row** (the first that fits):

| # | Her racing profile | Calling | Umas today | The pick (CP29) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Best style Front | **Brawler** | 47 | — |
| 2 | Wit ≥ Power, and A in two of Pace, Late, End | **Summoner** | 33 | — |
| 3 | Best style End | **Summoner** | 13 | — |
| 4 | Best style Pace, Power > Wit, best distance Sprint | **Brawler** | 14 | — |

### 2.3 The calling pick (UT4)

**As approved (CP29, CP30), restated here so the template has one home.** A **Pace** Uma picks **Spell Blade or Mage**,
a **Late** Uma **Paladin or Cleric**, once, on the Uma sheet; her Power against her Wit pre-selects (rows 5–8 above).
**163 of 270** get the pick (102 Pace, 61 Late); the four signal rows stay fixed. The pick is open until confirmed, by a
tap or by her first won fight; owned Umas at save v27 start unconfirmed on the pre-selection.

| The pick moves | Pace: Spell Blade ↔ Mage | Late: Paladin ↔ Cleric |
|---|---|---|
| Her calling and starting class | Physical hybrid ↔ magical | Healer either way |
| Her type (the name) | Flashblade, Spellfencer, Longblade ↔ Flarecaster, Spellwright, Starkeeper | Shieldrunner, Warden, Stalwart ↔ Lamplighter, Mender, Pilgrim |
| Her trade (PP2) | Forest · Armorer ↔ Monster Den · Lapidary | Fields · Workbench either way |

| Her lead weapon (the tip's tree), branch 1 | Katana ↔ Fire Tome | Greatsword ↔ Light Tome |
| **Never** | Her stride, her knack, her signature family, her moon's node I; her class afterwards (she still switches freely) | The same |

If a master-data fix ever moves her out of a pick row, the rule's calling applies and the stored pick waits unused.

### 3.2 The calling

Her calling is identity, not power: it **starts** her in a class (new unlocks start in their calling or pick, CP29),
**names her trade** (PP2: Mine · Forge, Forest · Armorer, Fields · Workbench, Fishery · Charm Shop, Monster Den ·
Lapidary), **sets her channel** (her moon's node II) and **gates** her Flourish and signature tip (§3.8). It never locks
her class: she switches freely, and a switch moves no job (CL14, CP30).

### 3.3 The Flourish, re-checked against SB18 (UT6)

**The re-check.** Variance U9 gave the Flourish one MP twist by stride (Opening: her first skill each fight free; Even
pace: skills 25% cheaper; Late reserve: 3% of her max MP a round from round 3), priced as "MP, not power". SB18 then made
MP **a rule for every Uma** (0.8% of her max MP a round for each skill depth open) and showed that over a region **MP is
pace**: a damage dealer's deep casts run out late in a region and she Attacks instead. Re-run on SB18's region model
(stat-balance §7.2, A14: twelve stages of five acts, one Spring; rebuilt for this pass, it reproduces §7.2's coverage
within 0–4 points), Tier 1:

| Class, Lv 50 | Deep casts a region, SB18 alone | + Opening | + Even pace | + Late reserve | Late-region power |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brawler | 60% | 77% (+17 points) | 80% (+20) | 73% (+13) | **+15–23%** |
| Mage | 80% | 100% (+20) | 100% (+20) | 100% (+20) | **+19%** |
| Spell Blade, Summoner | 67% | 87% (+20) | 90% (+23) | 83% (+17) | **+18–25%** |
| Paladin, Cleric | 100% | 100% (0) | 100% (0), **refunds a deep cast** | 100% (0), **refunds** from Lv 40 | **0** |

- **Not negligible: too big.** Across Lv 10–50 each twist adds **+6% to +27%** to a damage dealer's region power, 3 to 5
  times the +5% the whole template may add (U12). Each extra deep cast is +3.6 power units over an Attack, so one
  free cast a fight is twelve a region.
- **Worthless for a healer from Lv 40:** SB18 already gives her every deep cast. Even pace at Lv 50 (her deep cast 11.7
  MP against her 12 back a round) and Late reserve from Lv 40 (10 + 7 against 15.6; at Lv 50, 12 + 9) **refund a deep
  cast in a round**, which SB18's check forbids.
- **An MP twist inside the budget is invisible:** +2.5% region power is about **3 MP a fight** for a Brawler, a
  number no chip can show.
- **And SB18's reason holds against it:** the owner retired MP regen accessories because he'd wear one on every class;
  SB18 gave every class one rule. A template that reopens MP per Uma undoes "every class on one footing".

**The reshape (UT6): the Flourish moves to HP, her stamina in this game** (Stamina feeds HP). Every stage starts at full
HP, so an HP twist resets each fight and **can't compound over a region** the way MP does. Each Flourish is worth **4%
of her max HP a fight**, given in her stride's shape (a dash spends early, a stayer has some left at the end):

| Stride | Flourish | What it does, as her calling | A 5-round fight | A 3-round fight | An 8-round fight |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Dash** | **Clean break** | When the fight starts, a **Barrier of 4% of her max HP**; it absorbs any damage first and lapses at round 1's end, as every skill Barrier | 4% | 4% | 4% |
| **Middle** | **Even pace** | At the end of each round she recovers **1% of her max HP**, up to 4% a fight | 4% | 3% | 4% |
| **Stayer** | **Late reserve** | From the end of round 3 she recovers **2% of her max HP** a round, up to 4% a fight | 4% | 2% | 4% |

- **At Lv 50, Tier 1 · 2 · 3:** a Brawler's 4% is **59 · 102 · 145 HP**; a Mage's 50 · 86 · 123; the four between 53 ·
  92 · 132. A share, so the same in every tier (SB1), and the same for every calling: a healer is hit too.
- **Not a heal skill:** no healing scale, focus or Auto weighting; it never overheals, and a fallen Uma gets none. Once a
  fight (a stage), not each wave; the breather between waves stays as it is.
- **Shown:** the root plate's line ("★ Late reserve · from round 3, 2% of her max HP a round, up to 4%"), the Battle
  tab's Sustain, the log's green tick ("★ +30"); Clean break's Barrier on her card at the start.
- **What it is worth:** +4% to what she can take in a hard fight (§4); her pace and the foes' don't move.

The [gives page](previews/uma-gives.html#flourish) draws the re-check (every class, Lv 10–50) and the three shapes by

### 3.4 The signature accessory (UT7, UT8)

**WP33, decided.** While she wears **any form of her family** in any slot, her **ATK and M.ATK each gain +0.2 a level ×
tier** (+0.2 · +0.36 · +0.52 a level; **+10 · +18 · +26 at Lv 50**). The item is the same as anyone's; on another Uma it
is the plain item; her bonus goes when she takes it off. A stat in the damage stack's step 1, outside the kind cap and
the foe budget, never on her mends or Barriers. **The size stays SB15's** (+4.3–6.1% matched at Lv 50).

**Her knack names the family (UT7):**

| Knack | Family | Why it reads |
|---|---|---|
| **Fleet** (Speed) | **Bell** | Quick feet: her weapon strikes once more after her Attack |
| **Mighty** (Power) | **Orb** | Power on one kind |
| **Tireless** (Stamina) | **Pendant** | Steady offense on all her kinds, left on all region long |
| **Gritty** (Guts) | **Insignia** | Grit against the big foes (sub-bosses and bosses) |
| **Clever** (Wit) | **Insignia** | Picking her moment: the wave's first act (Onslaught), or the boss |

- **Why the knack:** it is the reading that splits two Umas of one type (two Ironhides), so the family adds to the
  owner's "two front runners differ" instead of repeating the calling or stride. One table of five rows, read on her
  sheet next to her moon's node I.
- **Today's roster:** Insignia 84 (31%), Bell 69 (26%), Orb 69 (26%), Pendant 48 (18%); of the 90 templates, Insignia
  36, the others 18. Every calling holds at least three families (the Cleric's 13: Insignia 8, Pendant 3, Bell 2).
- **The calling pick never moves it** (the knack doesn't move).
- **Alternatives weighed:** by stride (three readings for four families, and it repeats the Flourish); by calling (every
  Brawler the same family; two front runners never differ); by knack and stride (a quarter each, but a 15-cell table to
  read); by a second growth (a fourth reading, 360 combinations).
