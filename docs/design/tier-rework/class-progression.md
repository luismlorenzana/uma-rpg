### 2.3 One healer a party, and the party shapes

A party fights with one Paladin or Cleric at most (C8, D178); everything else is free. **Two contexts, the owner's
distinction (2026-10-24):**

**The campaign (Lv 1–50, the world): balanced parties** (CP4). One of each fight type, on the defaults, holds every
element and every damage type. Foes stay element-focused (a foe's element names her weakness, through the ring and
the twins) and keep today's stats, which the owner finds right: a balanced party clears everything.

| Shape | Members | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **The default party** | Brawler, Mage, Spell Blade, Summoner, and a Paladin or a Cleric, on defaults | **All ten kinds** with either healer; **every Tier 1 twin set answered by at least two members** ([§4.5](#45-balanced-checked-cp4-cp7)); every single kind of Tiers 2 and 3 held at least once |
| Five of one class | Any | Allowed. On defaults five copies hold the same three kinds; spread picks (each a different three) answer every twin set three times |

**The Rift: specialized parties, rebuilt and re-specced per path** (CP7). Each front asks for a shape and the

## 3. Weapons: three slots and what fits them

### 3.1 Three slots, by channel (CP2)

- **Every class has three weapon slots, in every tier.** Depth comes from weapon branches, trees and Extra Stats, never more slots.
- **A slot has a channel, nothing else.** The class sets how many are Arms:

  | Class | Arms slots | Tome slots | Spreads to choose from | Today |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Brawler | 3 | 0 | 10 (any three of five Arms) | 1 (all five) |
  | Mage | 0 | 3 | 10 | 1 |
  | Spell Blade, Paladin | 2 | 1 | 50 (10 Arms pairs × 5 Tomes) | 100 |
  | Summoner, Cleric | 1 | 2 | 50 | 100 |

- **Three different kinds.** Any kind of the slot's channel fits; a kind sits in one slot at a time.
- **The slot order** is the lead channel first (an Arms slot first for the Brawler, Spell Blade and Paladin, a Tome
  slot first for the others). The first slot leads the preset's accessories ([§5](#5-the-class-template-per-tier)).
- **What fills them** is the kind's own tree **of her class** ([§6](#6-the-class-trees)): the weapon's shape (a
  Greatsword's heavy hit, a Lance's random thrusts) is the small variety the owner asked to keep, and a Brawler's
  Greatsword tree is not a Paladin's ([§6.5](#65-every-classs-trees)).

### 3.2 The default weapons (CP3)

**The owner's defaults (2026-10-24), adopted.** Two of them were a choice; both are resolved by the twins.

| Class | Slot 1 | Slot 2 | Slot 3 | Twin sets it answers ([§4.1](#41-tier-1-her-twins-cp5)) | Today's five (v2) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Brawler** | Greatsword | Axe | Lance | Fire, Ice, Lightning | All five Arms |
| **Mage** | Fire Tome | Blizzard Tome | Thunder Tome | Fire, Ice, Lightning | All five Tomes |
| **Spell Blade** | Katana | Lance | **Thunder Tome** | Lightning (both halves), Dark; Twilight | Greatsword, Lance, Katana, Fire, Thunder |
| **Summoner** | Dark Tome | Light Tome | **Bow** | Light (both halves), Dark; Twilight | Fire, Blizzard, Thunder, Greatsword, Lance |
| **Paladin** | Greatsword | Axe | Light Tome | Fire, Ice, Light | Greatsword, Axe, Lance, Blizzard, Fire |
| **Cleric** | Light Tome | Blizzard Tome | Bow | Light (both halves), Ice; Twilight | Light, Dark, Thunder, Katana, Bow |

**The two choices:**
- **The Spell Blade's Tome: the Thunder Tome** (the owner's "Fire/Lightning Tome"). Her Lance and the Thunder Tome are
  twins, so on a Fire foe (weak to the Lightning twins) **both halves of every act land weak**: her identity, the
  shield chipper, at its fullest. A Fire Tome would twin nothing she holds (its twin is the Greatsword) and leave the
  default party's Fire twins to the Brawler and the Mage alone either way.
- **The Summoner's Arm: the Bow** (the owner's "Bow/Katana"). The Bow and her Light Tome are twins, so on a Dark foe
  both her summons land weak. And it is **the only way a party with a Paladin holds all ten kinds:** the Paladin
  carries no Bow, the Cleric already brings one, and the Katana sits on the Spell Blade. With a Katana the Paladin's
  default party holds nine.

**The pattern** that makes the campaign's party balanced on defaults:
- **The pure classes are the ring, one in each channel.** The Brawler's Greatsword, Axe and Lance and the Mage's Fire,
  Blizzard and Thunder are the Fire, Ice and Lightning twins: 31.5 of the world's 48 signatures, two answers on each.
- **The hybrids and the Cleric are twins.** The Spell Blade's Lance and Thunder, the Summoner's Bow and Light, the
  Cleric's Bow and Light: one twin set held in both channels, so both halves of a pair (or both of the Cleric's acts)
  land weak on it. Their other kinds (Katana, Dark Tome) carry the Dark twins and Twilight.
- **The healers keep their identities:** the Paladin's Greatsword blows give Aegis, her Axe blows Enfeeble every foe
  from depth 4, her Light spells mend and give Faith; the Cleric's Light spells leave Regen, her Blizzard spells give
  Aegis, her Bow blows Barrier and Shatter.
- **All ten kinds** sit in the five fight types' defaults with either healer; every Tier 1 twin set has at least two
  answers ([§4.5](#45-balanced-checked-cp4-cp7)).

**Why each, for the class itself:**
- **Brawler:** her first tree (Heavy Slash) on the Greatsword, every foe on the Axe, random foes on the Lance: each
  reach she needs, one act a turn at full power.
- **Mage:** a nuke (Fire), every foe (Blizzard), random bolts (Thunder): the ring's three, each its own reach.
- **Spell Blade:** every default multi-hits (Katana 2, Lance 2–3, Thunder 2–3) and her Arms half adds one more; on her
  twin set both halves chip.
- **Summoner:** her Dark summons strike twice, her Light summons many times, her Bow the archer line (Slinger to
  Sagittarius); in Tier 2 the **Warlock** curses with exactly her two Tomes (Light and Dark).
- **Paladin:** Greatsword and Axe blows for Barriers, Aegis and Enfeeble; the Light Tome for mends, Faith and **her
  revive**, which moves to her Light tree (CP37, below).
- **Cleric:** Light for Regen and Rebirth, Blizzard for Aegis, the Bow's blows for Barriers and Shatter.

**The Paladin's revive follows her defaults (CP37).** Valkyrie's Oath sits today on the Paladin's Lance tree (D4,
branch B), which her defaults no longer hold. It moves to **her Light tree's D3, branch B** (Heavy Devotion today),
where the Cleric's Rebirth sits: both healers revive from the same place on a default tree, at Class Rank 20. Her
Lance D4 B becomes Heavy Transfix, as the Cleric's. Node ids stay (`paladin-tome-light.3b` gains the revive and the
name, `paladin-lance.4b` loses them); engine v16 data, fights before it replay on their own engine.

**Every alternative stays legal.** A slot takes any kind of its channel (CP2), so the Spell Blade may carry a Fire
Tome and the Summoner a Katana, and **she can keep several weapons raised and swap between fights:** grades sit on each
weapon kind, she owns all ten, and swaps are free outside fights. The defaults are where a new class starts and what
the campaign's balance is checked on; the Rift's guided builds ([§2.3](#23-one-healer-a-party-and-the-party-shapes))
re-spec on top of them.

**By weapon group (second pass, CP38, CP40):** the Brawler's and the Mage's three are all **Ring**, the Summoner's all
**Light/Dark**; the other three mix, two and one: the Spell Blade and the Paladin lean Ring (Lance and Thunder;
Greatsword and Axe), the Cleric Light/Dark (Light and Bow). **No default changes:** each mixed set's odd kind is there
for a reason the focus doesn't touch: the Spell Blade's **Katana** is the default party's only Katana and the Dark
twins' second answer; the Paladin's **Light Tome** holds her revive (CP37); the Cleric's **Blizzard Tome** holds her
Aegis and the Ice twins' third answer. In Tier 2 that kind is her set's off-focus weapon (§7.2).

**Before the owner's review** the defaults were drawn so the Rift's four parties held every answer on defaults
(Brawler Greatsword, Axe, Katana; Mage Fire, Thunder, Light; Spell Blade Lance, Bow, Dark; Summoner Blizzard, Dark,
Greatsword; Paladin Greatsword, Lance, Blizzard; Cleric Light, Dark, Axe). The owner's split, campaign against Rift,
moved that job to the guided builds.

### 3.3 Equippable kinds, per tier (CP12)

Every class can slot every kind of its channels in every tier. What changes per tier is the weapon, not the slot.

| Class | Arms slots take | Tome slots take | A Dual's second kind (Tier 2 on, WR3, WP32) | Two-kind weapons in her slots |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brawler | Greatsword, Axe, Lance, Bow, Katana | — | Another Arm: the weapon's fixed partner, of the other group | 1 in Tier 2 and 3 |
| Mage | — | Fire, Blizzard, Thunder, Light, Dark | Another element: its fixed partner, of the other group | 1 in Tier 2 and 3 |
| Spell Blade, Paladin | Any two Arms | Any one Tome | On an Arm its partner Arm; on her Tome its partner element | 1 in Tier 2, 2 in Tier 3 |
| Summoner, Cleric | Any one Arm | Any two Tomes | The same | 1 in Tier 2, 2 in Tier 3 |

*Follow-up to the weapon review, 2026-10-24:* a Dual's second kind is no longer picked at the step: each weapon has a
**fixed Tier 2 partner** (a Dual's and a Tempered's) and a **fixed Tier 3 partner** (an Evolved's), of its own channel and
always of the other weapon group: Greatsword, Axe → Bow; Lance → Katana; Fire → Dark; Ice, Lightning → Light (the
owner's); Bow → Greatsword, Katana → Axe, Light → Fire, Dark → Ice ([weapon-progression's partner table](weapon-progression.md#the-partner-table-wp32),
WP32). A partner she already slots is not a legal Dual; every class's default and reverse set keeps one
([weapon-progression §3.4](weapon-progression.md#34-the-limits-wp8)).

- **Tier 1:** a weapon is one kind, its tree and its grade's Might.
- **Tier 2:** at G5 a weapon takes a branch, **Pure** (weak hits ×1.5) or **Dual** (its fixed partner, of its own channel
  and the other group, weak ×1.3). The **slot doesn't change**: a Dual stays in its first kind's slot and channel and opens its first kind's
  tree; its second kind is **coverage only**: its hits carry both kinds for weaknesses and charged-move locks **in every
  class that slots it**, one shield point and one lock a hit whichever kind matched, and every bonus (the focus, the
  affinity, Pendants, Orbs, the weapon moons) reads the hit's own, first kind (WP7, one wording since the cross-check; GE7's
  class check retires, so the first round's "Greatsword + Fire" weapon is gone). **One Dual in her slots** (WR4,
  WP8), her three kinds all different.
- **Tier 3:** both lines fork: Pure → **Keen** (×1.7) or **Tempered** (+ its Tier 2 partner, both ×1.5), Dual →
  **Tempered** (keeps its partner) or **Evolved** (turns to its Tier 3 partner, both ×1.5);
  **two** two-kind weapons for the four classes between, **one for good** for the Brawler and the Mage, so no Uma ever
  holds a whole channel. A weapon never carries more than two kinds (owner, 2026-10-24: Prism dropped).
- Branches are the weapons doc's (WP6–WP9); this doc owns which kinds fit which class's slots, and the rule above for
  extra kinds, worded as WP7.
  [§3.5](#35-what-grows-on-her-three-weapons-tier-by-tier-cp33) sums up what grows on a weapon per tier.

### 3.4 Switching class, and what it costs

- **Free, as today**, outside fights. Each class keeps its own three weapons, picks and loadout; she can keep several
  weapons raised and swap any of them in between fights.
- **Gear follows her:** she owns every kind, and armor and accessory grades sit on the slot (XR6). A new class costs only
  the weapons of its three she hasn't raised: 0–30 Weapon Gems.
- **From the owner's defaults**, the classes share: **Brawler–Paladin two** (Greatsword, Axe) and **Summoner–Cleric
  two** (Light, Bow): one to raise, 10 gems. **One shared, two to raise (20 gems):** Brawler–Spell Blade (Lance),
  Mage–Spell Blade (Thunder), Mage–Cleric (Blizzard), Summoner–Paladin (Light), Paladin–Cleric (Light). **None (30):**
  Brawler–Mage, Brawler–Summoner, Brawler–Cleric, Mage–Summoner, Mage–Paladin, Spell Blade–Summoner, Spell
  Blade–Paladin, Spell Blade–Cleric.
- **The five fight types' defaults hold all ten kinds** (Lance, Bow, Ice, Lightning and Light twice with a Cleric): a
  roster that raised one Uma of each type raised every kind at least once.

### 3.5 What grows on her three weapons, tier by tier (CP33)

The owner (2026-10-24): "If we keep 3 weapons through the tiers, do the DPS classes unlock specialized hits on the 3
weapons, or more weapon damage types in later tiers?" **Both, and each has one home** (weapons own *which kinds* and
*how hard a weak hit lands*; classes and trees own *how she hits*: the [research note](weapon-research.md), WR1):
- **On the weapon: a branch from Tier 2** (WR2–WR5, decided in weapon-progression.md as WP6–WP9). At G5 each
  of her three weapons becomes **Pure** (one kind, weak hits ×1.5 instead of ×1.3; was "Specialized", renamed so it
  can't be read as a hit shape) or **Dual** (a second kind **of its own channel**, the weapon's **fixed partner**,
  always of the other weapon group: an Arm adds an Arm, a Tome an element; weak ×1.3). **No third branch:** every
  candidate (Break, lock, Executioner, imbue …) duplicates a tree or a
  class job, or trivializes. **Her slots hold one Dual in Tier 2 and two two-kind weapons in Tier 3; the Brawler and
  the Mage keep one for good**, so no Uma ever holds all five kinds of a channel. **Tier 3 forks both lines, never
  stacks:** Pure → **Keen** (×1.7) or **Tempered** (+ its Tier 2 partner, both ×1.5); Dual → **Tempered** (keeps its
  partner) or **Evolved** (turns to its Tier 3 partner, both ×1.5). A Dual's second kind
  keeps the hit's channel, offense and tree, counts for weaknesses and locks in every class that slots it, and earns no
  bonus: every bonus reads the hit's own, first kind (WR3, WP7). *Follow-up to the weapon review, 2026-10-24: fixed
  partners (WP32) and Tier 3's Evolved (WP9).*
- **In the trees: the specialized hits** (branch C, the Power or Break tip, capstones, the class branch). No tree and no
  template adds a kind (CP17).
- **In her class branch: the focus** (Tier 2, [§7.2](#72-class-branches-tier-2-the-weapon-group-focus-cp26-cp34-cp38cp41)):
  +15% on the Ring or the Light/Dark group's kinds. A percentage on a group, never a kind.
- **Beside them: damage on a kind,** her **accessories** (1 · 2 · 3 slots, all four families in every tier: Pendants,
  Orbs, Bells and Insignias in their base forms, two branches each from Tier 2, a step deeper in Tier 3;
  weapon-progression WP18–WP19, WP34; *follow-up to the weapon review, 2026-10-24*) and the Extra Stats **weapon moons**
  (Tier 2 one, Full or Split; Tier 3 a second; outside the kind cap; *follow-up to D194 (Extra Stats), 2026-10-24*: was
  the Halo). Where each sits on a
  hit: [weapon-progression's damage stack](weapon-progression.md#24-the-damage-stack-everything-on-one-hit-wp31) (WP31).

## 4. Foe weaknesses by tier

The ten kinds are the weakness types. A foe's card shows her kinds as icons, nothing else: "Weak: [Lance] [Thunder]".

### 4.1 Tier 1: her twins (CP5)

**Twins** are an Arm and the element that share a shape in the catalog (`RPG_KIND_PAIRS`, D86): **Greatsword and
Fire, Axe and Ice, Lance and Lightning, Bow and Light, Katana and Dark.** A twin set is named by its element: "the
Lightning twins" are the Lance and the Thunder Tome.

**A Tier 1 foe is weak to one twin set: the element that beats hers on the ring, and its twin Arm.**
- **The ring,** today's: Fire > Ice > Lightning > Fire; Light ⇄ Dark. A Fire foe is weak to Lightning and the Lance; an
  Ice foe to Fire and the Greatsword; a Lightning foe to Ice and the Axe; a Light foe to Dark and the Katana; a Dark foe
  to Light and the Bow.
- **Two elements:** she reads her lead element first (a pair signature's lead alternates by slot, W3); if its answer is
  one of her own, her second. A Fire + Lightning foe answers to Ice either way; an Ice + Light foe to Fire (lead Ice) or
  Dark (lead Light).
- **Light and Dark together** answer to no element: she's weak to the Arms of her own two, **the Bow and the Katana**
  ("Twilight"), as today's Light + Dark foes break on weapons alone.
- **Every foe, every rank:** regulars, sub-bosses, bosses and the capitals' Finals. Two kinds, one in each channel, one
  rule. Today: 2–5 kinds, half drawn at random, five rules.

**Over the world's 19 biomes and 48 signatures** (a pair's two leads count a half each): the Fire twins answer 11,
the Ice twins 11, the Lightning twins 9.5, the Dark twins 8, the Light twins 6.5, Twilight 2. The
[weaknesses page](previews/class-weaknesses.html#world) lists every biome.

**Why twins:** the Arm is read from the element, so one ring teaches both channels; each channel always has an answer
on every foe; a hybrid holding a twin set (a Lance and a Thunder Tome, the Spell Blade's default) lands both halves weak.

## 6. The class trees

### 6.1 The rules every tier shares (CP13–CP17, CP21–CP23)

- **One tree per weapon slot, three a class** (CP13). The catalog stays one tree per class and kind (50 trees, today's
  ids); her three slots pick which three she uses. **Every tree is her class's own:** the Brawler's, the Spell Blade's,
  the Summoner's and the healers' Greatsword trees are four different trees ([§6.5](#65-every-classs-trees)). The page
  draws her **root** (her class), from Tier 2 her **class tree** (her branch, [§7.2](#72-class-branches-tier-2-the-weapon-group-focus-cp26-cp34-cp38cp41))
  and her three weapon trees under it.
- **A weapon tree** has a **trunk** (depths 1–2, always hers), a **fork** at depth 3 into **branches**, and each
  branch's **tip** (depth 5); Tier 3 adds a **capstone** (depth 6).
- **No skill points, at any tier** (CP14). Class Rank opens a depth, and every node at an open depth on her path is hers
  at once. A fork's branches are exclusive and free; **branch A is the default**; swaps are free outside fights and her
  equipped skills follow (with Rend equipped, taking Combo puts Rending Combo in its slot).
- **A fork changes what she's for** (CP15): reach (one foe, random foes, every foe, a splash), hits (count or weight; a
  pierce), support (a status's weight, who a mend or Barrier reaches, a revive) or Breaks. Never a few percent alone.
  **A** is today's core line, the anchor the simulation plays; **B** and **C** are sidegrades.
- **Class Rank gates only** (CP16): depth 1 at Class Rank 1, 2 at 10, 3 at 20, 4 at 30, 5 at 40, 6 at 50 (Tier 3). The
  side gates (5, 15 … 45) retire. A weapon's grade gates no skill.
- **Trees never add a damage kind** (CP17): a skill hits with its weapon's kinds (and from Tier 2 a Dual's second kind,
  always of the weapon's own channel, WR3). The Brawler stays physical whatever she picks.
- **Names** (CP21): today's catalog names, the strikes' renames (SK14) **reverted**: "Mystic Cleave Flurry" and "Heavy
  Rupture" keep their names (the Spell Blade's Flurry line gives way to Pierce, CP42). New nodes are
  named by rule ([§6.6](#66-new-nodes-by-rule)). The Summoner's Axe summons
  "Berserker" and "Jotun Warlord" become **Marauder** and **Jotun King** (A4: the specialization names), ids unchanged.
- **Every skill ranks up by itself** (CP32, [below](#skill-ranks-cp32)): Rank 1 at its gate, one more every 2 Class
  Ranks, up to Rank 5.
- **The Skills tab draws a real tree** (CP23, SK10's design): the root plate, the class tree (Tier 2 on), three weapon
  tabs with a path glyph, the open tree as nodes and lines, her path filled, unheld branches dimmed, locked depths faded
  with their Class Rank, and **five rank pips on every node she holds** ("Cleave · Rank 3 · next at Class Rank 14"); a
  win that ranks a skill up says so on the result sheet ("Cleave reached Rank 3"). From Tier 2 her **focus trees** carry
  the focus's mark ("Ring · +15%"). The points UI retires.

#### Skill Ranks (CP32)

The owner (2026-10-24): "nothing to do between Class Ranks 1–10 for every 10 multiple; can skills rank up every 2 Class
Ranks, automatically, without skill point allocation?" **Yes.**

- **Every skill she holds has a Skill Rank, 1 to 5.** It is Rank 1 when its depth opens and gains a rank **every 2
  Class Ranks** after: a depth-3 skill (Class Rank 20) is Rank 2 at 22, 3 at 24, 4 at 26 and 5 at 28. Automatic: no
  points, no pick, no currency. Branch swaps keep ranks (a rank is read from her Class Rank, not stored per node).
- **A rank scales the skill's power:** ×0.95, ×0.975, ×1.00, ×1.025, ×1.05 at Ranks 1–5. A healer's mend and Barrier
  shares scale the same way; MP, hits, targets and statuses never change.
- **Balance-neutral by construction:** each rank lasts 2 of a depth's 10 Class Ranks, so the average over the window is
  ×1.00: today's depth power. A depth's Rank 5 never reaches the next depth's Rank 1, so a deeper skill is always the
  stronger one.
- **Something visibly improves every 2 Class Ranks:** 25 of a tier's 50 Class Ranks bring a depth or a rank-up (each
  depth's opening and its four rank-ups), never more than 2 apart. Tier 3 adds the capstones at 50.
- **What ranks:** every weapon-tree skill and, from Tier 2, every class skill (CP34). **What doesn't:** the
  branch's focus (CP39), the capstones (they open at Class Rank 50, the last, so they open whole, ×1.00), Limit
  Breaks (the halls' power at her open depth, as today) and template nodes.
- **Where it shows:** Lv 34–50 on one class, past Class Rank 48, every skill she holds sits at Rank 5, 5% above
  today's. The re-baseline measures it at Lv 50 (README §9.5 item 1, inside its 3 points); if it lands outside, the
  foe budget takes it back, as with the first round's Orb. *Follow-up to D196 (stat balancing), 2026-10-25: **the
  re-baseline reads Class Rank on the real pace** (2E a win: Class Rank 20 at Uma Lv 13, 50 at Lv 34), not Class Rank =
  her level (SB16). Measured: ×1.000 on the balance party over Lv 1–50, ×1.014 at a real player's pace (×1.05 from
  Lv 34); the bigger gap is the depth she opens sooner (Lv 20: depth 4 against 3), which the foes never priced, so the
  re-baseline measures what players hold ([stat-balance §9](stat-balance.md#9-skill-ranks-neutrality)).*
- **Engine:** v16. A fight's input carries each skill's rank multiplier, frozen at the fight's start (as Extra Stats
  points are); nothing stored in the save.

## 7. Class Rank, class branches, masteries

### 7.1 Class Rank (CP25)

- **Per class, per world, 1–50.** A win pays 2E Class EXP to the class she fought as (D102); the curve is Uma Level's
  (120 + 16 × rank to next). Past Rank 50 it banks at half rate as **Class Marks**; a Class Manual (1,000 Marks) gives
  1,000 Class EXP. Gear Marks become Class Marks one for one (GE15). The Rift's Echoes pay Class EXP at 2×.
- **It opens depths** ([§6.1](#61-the-rules-every-tier-shares-cp13cp17-cp21cp23)), **ranks every skill she holds**
  ([Skill Ranks](#skill-ranks-cp32), every 2 Class Ranks) and sets her Limit Break's depth. Fighting as one class,
  Class Rank 20 comes at about Uma Lv 13, 30 at Lv 20, 40 at Lv 27, 50 at Lv 34.
- **Promotion:** Class Rank 1 in every class in the new world; banked EXP past Lv 50 becomes Class Marks (T3, up to
  2,000: about Class Rank 10 for one class).
- **Nothing else gates a class:** no skill points, no weapon rank; a weapon's grade is the weapons doc's.

### 7.2 Class branches, Tier 2: the weapon-group focus (CP26, CP34, CP38–CP41)

**The owner's second pass (2026-10-24):** "I still don't like the class specializations in later tiers, feels like
almost nothing changes." His model: the Brawler's branch 1 adds damage on her three main Arms (Greatsword, Axe, Lance),
branch 2 only on the secondary Arms (Bow, Katana); the Mage's the same with her elemental Tomes (Fire, Blizzard,
Thunder) against Light and Dark; "then for all other classes we add reverse options, so in my party comps I would bring
one party with an Elemental Mage and a regular Summoner, but in another party a Light/Dark Mage and an Elemental
Summoner." (The first review's ask, "maybe each class can have its own unique skill trees", is still answered: every
class's weapon trees are its own, §6.5, and each branch keeps three class skills.)

**The answer: the class branch is a weapon-group focus.** Flipping it changes what a player sees:
1. **Her weapons:** each branch has its set; the reverse swaps two or three of her three, and with them two or three
   trees and their shapes (a Brawler's heavy blow, every-foe Axe and random thrusts become many arrows and the
   Katana's payoff on a Broken foe).
2. **+15% on the focus group's kinds,** marked on those trees ("Ring · +15%").
3. **Three different class skills** and a different Tier 3 capstone, cast through her focus weapons.
4. **For a healer, other statuses:** a Ring Paladin Armor Breaks, Enfeebles and gives Aegis; a Light/Dark Paladin
   Shatters, Hexes (a foe weak to Light and Dark) and gives Faith.

#### The two weapon groups (CP38)

| Group | Arms | Tomes | Twin sets | The owner's words | World's signatures answered (Tier 1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ring** | Greatsword, Axe, Lance | Fire, Blizzard, Thunder (Fire, Ice, Lightning) | The Fire, Ice and Lightning twins | "main" Arms, "elemental" Tomes | 31.5 of 48 (66%) |
| **Light/Dark** | Bow, Katana | Light, Dark | The Light and Dark twins, and Twilight (Bow, Katana) | "secondary" Arms, "Light/Dark" Tomes | 16.5 of 48 (34%) |

- **One split for both channels:** the owner's split per channel lines up through the twins: each main Arm is an
  elemental Tome's twin (Greatsword and Fire, Axe and Ice, Lance and Lightning), each secondary Arm a Light/Dark
  Tome's (Bow and Light, Katana and Dark). So one group word covers a hybrid's two halves, and her twin pair always
  sits in one group.
- **The names:** "Ring" is this doc's word for the Fire > Ice > Lightning ring (§4.1); "Light/Dark" is the owner's.
  Not "Arcane": that word is the Rift's Tome path (CP7). On screen: "Mage · Sage · Ring", "a Ring Mage".
- **Names (owner, 2026-10-24):** Arms: **Heavy** (Greatsword, Axe, Lance) and **Lite** (Bow, Katana). Tomes:
  **Elemental** (Fire, Ice, Lightning) and **Arcane** (Light, Dark). This doc's "Ring" group is Heavy + Elemental, its
  "Light/Dark" group Lite + Arcane; the names land everywhere at implementation. Two clashes to settle then: "Arcane" is
  also the Rift's Arcane front, and "Heavy" begins some skill names (Heavy Slash).
- **Three kinds against two:** a channel holds three Ring kinds and two Light/Dark ones, so a pure class's
  Light/Dark set carries one Ring weapon off-focus (her reach, below).

#### The focus (CP39)

- **One focus a branch:** branch 1 her default group, branch 2 the reverse. **+15% damage on hits of the focus
  group's kinds**, read on a hit's own kind (its weapon's first kind, its tree's: a Dual's second kind doesn't earn it,
  as with the affinity). A healer's focus also adds **+15% to those skills' mend and Barrier shares.** A percentage
  holds its share at every tier under SB1: nothing to rescale.
- **Why +15%:** the passives it replaces paid +15–25% in their context (the Sage and Sorcerer +15%, the Warlord and
  Conjurer +20%, the Templar and Bishop +25%); on her set two or three of her three weapons carry it, and in its context
  (a region weak to the group, below) her acts lean on them. **One number for both groups:** the Light/Dark group's
  narrower span is paid by her reach weapon, not by a bigger percentage. **Where it sits on a hit:**
  [weapon-progression's damage stack](weapon-progression.md#24-the-damage-stack-everything-on-one-hit-wp31) (WP31,
  stated once there): after the weak multiplier, a factor of its own beside an Insignia's, read on the hit's own kind.
  A weak hit in Tier 2 at most Pure ×1.5 × focus 1.15 ≈ ×1.73 before an Insignia; the stack goes into Tier 2's
  simulation whole (weapon-research risk 3); the first fallback is +10%. *Follow-up to D197 (Uma Templates),
  2026-10-25: was "beside the affinity … × affinity 1.06 ≈ ×1.83"; the affinity's factor leaves the stack (UT11).*
- **Her weapons follow it:** each branch carries a preset set (CP40); swapping branch offers her set in one tap ("Use
  the Light/Dark set: Bow, Katana, Axe"). She owns every kind and swaps are free outside fights; at Ascend every grade
  resets, so the reverse set costs nothing more than the default.
- **Her class skills cast through a focus weapon** (when she holds one; Auto takes the one that lands weak), so the
  focus's +15% rides them.
- **The twelve passives fold into the focus** (tier2-classes A5): one passive a branch, the visible one. The two
  skills that leaned on a passive carry its rule: Shifting Spell the Sage's rotation, Ravager's Wheel the Berserker's
  tapped foe.
- **Ascend preselects branch 1,** her default group: the campaign's balanced party (CP4). A6's stat rule retires: it
  would now re-spec her weapons by a stat.
- **Unchanged:** one of two a class, swapped freely on the class plate outside fights; no new class ids, no lean change
  (A2 dropped), Limit Break power the class's. Her chip reads "Spell Blade · Rune Knight · Ring".

#### The rules every branch shares

- **The focus is on from Class Rank 1;** the class skills open at **Class Rank 20, 30 and 40**, the gates of depths 3–5,
  and rank up like any skill (CP32).
- **A class skill has no weapon of its own: it is cast through one of her focus weapons** (CP17 holds; any weapon if
  she holds none of her focus). It carries that weapon's kind, channel, Might, weapon moons and branch (*follow-up to
  D194, 2026-10-24*: was "Halo"), and the focus's +15%; the
  skill gives the shape, power and effect. Auto casts it through the focus weapon that lands weak; on her bar she picks.
  A hybrid's class skill shapes **her pair** (her Arms half through an Arm, her Tome half through her Tome); a healer's
  is **a blow** (through an Arm) or **a spell** (through a Tome); a **utility** skill (a stance, a guard, a party buff)
  needs none.
- **Power and MP on today's depth tables:** Class Rank 20, 30, 40 are depths 3, 4, 5 (power 2.9, 3.7, 4.6; MP 18, 22,
  26); every foe ×0.55 and +4 MP, random foes ×1.05, as the trees. Her hand applies; skill MP ×1.7 in Tier 2.
- **tier2-classes §3 binds them:** no extra act, no Limit refund, no longer Break, at most one extra shield point an act
  (the shared cap), no new damage kind, no party support from a Summoner.
- **Held and equipped like any skill:** 18 held in Tier 2 (15 and her branch's 3), 3 → 10 equipped.
- **Priced like a branch** (SK §10): in its context a class skill beats the same depth's A-line skill by 10–20%;
  elsewhere it trails. The simulation tunes the numbers inside the shape.

### 7.3 Tier 3: the branch capstone and the mastery (CP27)

**The mastery becomes her branch's crown:** at **Class Rank 50** her held branch opens its **capstone** (the table
above) and, with it, **the class's mastery mechanic**, one a class, six in all, the same for both branches. Draft
effects, under tier2-classes §3's rules (no extra act, no Limit refund, no longer Break, at most one extra shield point
an act, no new damage kind, no party support from the Summoner); Tier 3's simulation sets the numbers (+8–15% where it
applies).

| Class | Mastery | On screen | Draft effect | Where it pays |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brawler | **Unstoppable** | "Her hits lose less to a standing shield." | An unbroken foe takes ×0.6 from her hits, not ×0.5 | Every fight before the Break; most where shields are high |
| Mage | **Resonance** | "Her deepest spells ring twice." | Her depth 5 and 6 spells (and her Class Rank 40 and 50 skills) strike again at 30% on the same targets; the second strike never removes a shield point | Long fights she can cast through |
| Spell Blade | **Spellbind** | "Both halves weak: +20%." | When both halves of her act land weak on the same foe, the act deals +20% | Tier 3 bosses keep their twins: a Spell Blade on her twin pair (the Rune Knight's Lance and Thunder, the Blademaster's Katana and Dark) lands it every act |
| Summoner | **Menagerie** | "Her summons stay a pair longer." | Each Tome summon lingers for two of her pairs, and strikes in farewell at both | Crowds; the Horde |
| Paladin | **Last Stand** | "Her Barrier saves a falling Uma once a fight." | Once a fight, an Uma under her Barrier who takes a fatal hit stays at 1 HP | The Duel's heavy hitters |
| Cleric | **Overflow** | "Mends past full become a Barrier." | A mend's healing past max HP becomes a Barrier, up to 10% of max HP | Spread damage |

- **Why at Class Rank 50:** the mastery was on from Class Rank 1 of Tier 3; as her branch's capstone it becomes the
  tier's last milestone, opening with depth 6 (Uma Lv 34 on one class), so Tier 3's Class Ranks build toward it.

## 15. Pacing

The time to level, the EXP and rewards per fight and the time to gear an Uma on curve keep today's order (README §2):

- **Class progression keeps today's pace:** Class EXP 2E a win, the same depth gates, no points to save. A pick is free
  and instant. **Skill Ranks add steps, not power:** a rank every 2 Class Ranks, the depth's average unchanged (CP32);
  past Class Rank 48 her skills sit 5% over today's, which the Lv 50 re-baseline measures, **on the real Class Rank
  pace** (2E: Class Rank 50 at Uma Lv 34; *follow-up to D196 (stat balancing), 2026-10-25, SB16*).
- **Damage a turn:** an Uma acts once a turn whatever she holds. **The weak-act share falls** from nearly all (five
  weapons answer most lists) to about half on the owner's defaults (one Uma: 34–66%; a hybrid on her twin set lands
  both halves), while every foe keeps at least two answers in the campaign's default party and the Rift's guided
  builds. Breaks come slower; the simulation re-sets shields (§4.4, drafts: sub-boss 5 · 7 · 6,
  boss 6 · 9 · 10 by tier; *follow-up to D196: bosses were 6 · 8 · 9, SB6*) so rounds to the first Break stay within
  ±0.5 of today's at Lv 10, 30, 50.
- **The re-baseline** (README §9.5 item 1): an on-curve party (three default weapons, branch A, one accessory at its
  draft value, the full map) wins every Tier 1 stage within 3 points of today's table; what the damage-oriented
  accessories add (weapon-progression.md) goes back through the foe budget, as the first round's Orb did. *Follow-up
  to D196 (stat balancing), 2026-10-25:* its balance party reads Class Rank on the real pace (2E), not Class Rank = her
  level (CP32, SB16), plays the base MP regen (SB18), and expects Tier 1's build within 2% of today's for five classes
  (stat-balance §3.7, §12).

  to D196: with the Summoner's Arms hand 0.48 and the hybrids' presets on their lead half the model holds the Tier 1

  all-A; a Sweep Spell Blade or a Hunt Summoner within ±15% of the mirror in its context; the Spell Blade's Pierce B
  (CP42) the same 10–20% on long shields.

  tips and the narrowed regulars are priced in Tier 2's term (Z4, XR15; *follow-up to D197: the affinity left the list, UT11*). *Follow-up to D196 (stat
  balancing), 2026-10-25:* **Tier 2's term +27%, Tier 3's 1.08 → 1.48, bosses × 1.1 (SB3–SB5)** on the foes' HP and

  party's pace back to 1.03–1.11× Tier 1's at every level, Z4's ±10% at its edge ([stat-balance §3](stat-balance.md#3-the-damage-stack-and-the-foe-budget)). **The focus:** each class's two
  branches within ±5% of each other across the world's regions, and each ahead by 10–20% in its own (a Ring branch on
