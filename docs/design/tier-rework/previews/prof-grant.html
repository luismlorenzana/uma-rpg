The first launch: Craft Levels reset, Speed Tickets for the time

The Tier rework's one save migration (v27), as the owner directed on 2026-10-24: "reset all farm/craft levels to 1, and then grant all tickets based on the amount of time it took to get all those levels." Every Uma's Craft Level goes back to 1, and her world is paid one Speed Ticket for every 30 minutes of work her Craft EXP records. From profession-progression.md §14 (PP19, PP20, PP24, PP25).

The rule

The game pays 2 Craft EXP for every minute of every job, at her level, before the place's speed. So her lifetime Craft EXP is her working time, to the minute, and the save already holds it.

Speed Tickets for an Uma = ⌈ (EXP her Craft Level took + her craft.exp) ÷ 60 ⌉
EXP to Craft Lv L = 1,425 (L − 1) + 7.5 L (L − 1); her bank past Lv 50 sits in craft.exp and counts. 60 EXP is 30 minutes of the manual's job time; a Speed Ticket takes 30 minutes off a running job.

Speed Tickets by Craft Level

The grant for an Uma at each Craft Level (no bank), against what re-earning that level takes in the new places, which work ×1.25 from day 1. The gap is the quarter's margin of PP24: the ×1 days before today's facilities reached level 3, Finish's rounding, inputs bought at the Exchange. Hover for each level.

Three players at the update

Each world's grant, what re-levelling its Umas in the new places takes, and the making time of the pieces it loses (the floor). The grant always covers the re-level; a world whose crafters are home never needs the floor.

Show the numbers

The floor: pieces whose makers have left

Gear and Star Gems are folded into the grant: their gathering, refining and making are already in the Umas' EXP. Two kinds of piece aren't: gear left in a world's stock by Umas who ascended (their Craft EXP left with them), and pieces made of items bought at the Exchange. So a world is never paid less than its deleted pieces' own making time (a gear piece 203 minutes, a Star Gem 221 on average, at Craft Lv 25's times).
