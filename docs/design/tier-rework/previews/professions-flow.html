From the gathering places to what she wears

What one maxed Uma's gear and Extra Stats take, as a flow: gathered at the five gathering places, processed one for one at the Workbench, made at the Forge, the Armorer, the Charm Shop and the Lapidary, and worn in her slots and on her Extra Stats map. Band widths are refined items. From professions.md §5.2–§5.5, §6 and §7; the places and their art are on the map.

One Uma, maxed

Gather → process → make → wear. Each gathering profession offers a low type (grades 1–5, Craft Lv 1) and a high one (grades 6–10, Lv 26); from Tier 2 a tier pair joins and feeds only that tier's new pieces (fork gems, Halo Jewels). Hover a band or a node for its amount.

Tier by tier

Every tier's base items are Tier 1's 485, type for type; a tier's new pieces add 25 (Tier 2) or 35 (Tier 3) refined items of its pairs, inside the budget of a tenth more work (48). Bars are refined items per maxed Uma, her moon left out.

Recipes: one fixed set an item

Grades 1–5 take each line's low types, 6–10 its high types. A piece takes one gather's time per refined item in it. From Tier 2 a piece made at grade 5 adds one low type of each of its tier's pairs, at grade 10 one high type.
