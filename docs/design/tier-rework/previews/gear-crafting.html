Refined: the Forge 120 (Tier 2–3: 108 + 27), the Armorer 125, the Charm Shop 120. With Extra Stats' jewels (120), one Tier 1 Uma takes 485 refined items, against today's 472; her own moon adds 8 (493). A party of five takes five times the column.

One fixed set an item

Each item has one recipe for grades 1–5 (the low types, Craft Lv 1) and one for 6–10 (the high types, Craft Lv 26). No rotation: anyone can say what an item takes.

Settings: 12 with one accessory slot (Tier 1), 6 with two, 4 with three, so the Charm Shop stays near 120 refined per Uma. Weapons take ingots and planks; light armor cloth and shells, heavy armor iron, oak and a cut stone; accessories cloth and shells. Every class needs the same items.
