Accessories: every one deals damage

The owner (2026-10-24): defensive accessories are never picked over damage, and MP regen is always picked, so all ten supports retire and armor takes their defense. Four damage families replace them, unlocked multi-element first, focused damage later: Pendants and Bells in Tier 1, Orbs in Tier 2, Insignias in Tier 3, one slot a tier and one of each family at most. Below, each family's best member against the best, by class and context. From weapon-progression.md §5; the other pages: weapon trees · armor · materials.

What retires

All ten, in every tier. Their defensive worth moves into armor (one support's worth: a regular's hit 20% → 18.7% of her HP); MP comes from her level, armor's legs and her template's MP twists, and the Bell makes her free Attack worth taking.

The catalog, by tier

Pick a tier and a grade: every accessory with what it adds there. Flat values (Pendants, Orbs) are Tier 1's × 1.8 and × 2.6; a Bell's power and an Insignia's percentage are shares and stay. The slot holds the grade; the type swaps freely among the unlocked families.

Values by grade

A Pendant spreads 1.2 Orbs of offense over its kinds (0.4 an Orb on each of three, 0.6 on each of two); a Bell's follow-up grows 0.1 a grade to a full Attack; Onslaught 2% a grade, Slayer 1.5%.

What it showsEvery family is someone's best: Pendants left on all region long (four classes), the Bell for the Brawler in the campaign and in long fights, the Orb when she tunes to a region or a front and on waves, Slayer on bosses, Onslaught close behind on waves. In the campaign left alone the Pendant, the Orb and Onslaught sit within 6.1–7.5% for the damage classes. The Bell is a niche on purpose for the healers and the Summoner, whose acts are skills; for the hybrids its worth is also in chips, which these numbers leave out.
The model: a flat point is worth its place in offense² ÷ (offense + defense) (foe defense about 190: +34 on her lead kind is +18%); her damage split by kind from the 48 signatures; her Attack share of acts by her MP (Brawler 30%, Mage 20%, hybrids 25%, healers 12%; × 1.7 in long fights, × 0.6 on waves); a skill averages 4.7 single-target units at Lv 50. Contexts: Campaign, one accessory left on all region long; Tuned, swapped for a region or a Rift front (75% of her damage on her best kind); Long fight, a boss or the Duel, MP runs dry; Waves, the Horde's regulars.
