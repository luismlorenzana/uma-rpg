Accessories: every family, every tier

Every accessory deals damage; the ten defensive and MP supports retire and armor takes their defense. The owner's review (2026-10-24): all four families are there from Tier 1 in a simple base form (one slot, one of four), each branches in two at a slot's G5 from Tier 2, and Tier 3 takes each branch a step deeper; a branch is a form she sets, so nothing new is crafted. Uma uniqueness rides an accessory too: her template's signature family adds ATK and M.ATK +0.2 a level while she wears it. What retires

All ten, in every tier. Their defensive worth moves into armor (one support's worth: a regular's hit 20% → 18.7% of her HP); MP comes from her level, armor's legs, Vigor and her template's MP twists, and a base regen every Uma has (a rule, never an item: 0.8% of her max MP a round for each skill depth open, 4% from Class Rank 40), and the Bell makes her free Attack worth taking. Follow-up to D196 (stat balancing), 2026-10-25: without the regen accessories a party ran dry over a region; the regen the owner would have worn on every class is now every class's, as a rule (SB18).

The trees: a base, two branches, a step

One shape for every family, like the weapons': the Great branch keeps the base's reach and deepens it (Pure's way); the other changes what it reaches (Dual's way). Pick a tier to light what a slot can hold there (branches from the slot's G5 in Tier 2, their step from its G5 in Tier 3). Values at G10; flat ones in Orbs, an Orb being half a G10 weapon's Might (+34 · +61 · +88). Follow-up to D196 (stat balancing), 2026-10-25: the Great Pendant 0.55 (Tier 3 0.575) and the Toll Bell +0.5 a ring at G10 (+0.05 a grade), so both are someone's best (SB8).

Slots, families and the kind cap

1 · 2 · 3 slots, untyped. One accessory of each family at most, so every tier keeps a choice of family: one of four, two of four, three of four. Flat offense on one kind from the Pendant and Orb forms stops at a G10 weapon's Might × tier; the Great Orb with a Channel Pendant is the pair that reaches it. Her weapon moons (Extra Stats, from Tier 2) sit outside the cap, bounded by their own size, so the flat on one kind is at most Might + the cap + a Full moon: 334 in Tier 2, 484 in Tier 3. The signature accessory: Uma uniqueness through accessories

The owner: "maybe we don't have it affect weapons, but instead Umas equip slightly stronger accessories … exactly the same as any other Uma's, but the stats get a fixed added bonus on every level up." Her template names one family; while she wears any form of it, ATK and M.ATK +0.2 a level × tier, about +5% at each tier's Lv 50. Recommended to replace class-progression's affinity (CP36); which family each template names is the Uma Templates pass's.
