A = an Arms slot, T = a Tome slot. Three slots for every class, in every tier. The healers sit under the hybrid with the same slots; one healer a party.

The five fight types

PhysicalBrawler 3 Arms · physical armor · heavy single hits, the Axe for areas
MagicalMage 3 Tomes · magical armor · elements, the Blizzard Tome for areas
Physical hybridSpell Blade 2 Arms + 1 Tome · physical armor · a blade and a spell each act, the blade landing one extra hit: the shield breaker
Magical hybridSummoner 2 Tomes + 1 Arm · magical armor · a Tome summon and an Arms summon each act; the Tome summon's farewell strikes every foe
HealerPaladinCleric 2 + 1, either way · the Paladin's two Arms give Barriers and debuffs, the Cleric's two Tomes mends and buffs · half-hand damage

Party shapes against Tier 1's strikes

Each Tier 1 foe is weak to one strike. Every class holds one weapon of each strike, so on the world map every member of every party lands weak hits. The bars count the members who chip on each strike.
