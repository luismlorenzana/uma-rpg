Gear materials: every recipe and one Uma's totals

Four things are crafted for gear: Weapon Gems and, from Tier 2, one Branch Gem a weapon at the Forge, Armor Fittings at the Armorer and Accessory Settings at the Charm Shop. Each has one fixed amount, and a grade decides its material (profession-progression's ladder, PP9): in Tier 1 the low type of a pair for grades 1–5 and the high type for 6–10; Tier 2 moves G5 and G10 onto its own pair, Tier 3 G4 and G9 onto Tier 2's and G5 and G10 onto its own. The Branch Gem is the G5 Weapon Gem in its tier's pair. One Uma's gear is 365 refined items in every tier. The owner's review (2026-10-24) changes no count: a Dual's partner is fixed, so the Branch Gem still carries only the branch, and an accessory's branches are forms its slot's G5 Setting opens, with no item of their own.
