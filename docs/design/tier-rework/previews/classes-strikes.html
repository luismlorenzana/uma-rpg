For classes.md §2.2–§2.3. Tier 1 foes are weak to one strike, not to a list of kinds. Every class holds one weapon of each strike, so every Uma always has a weak weapon.

The rule

A foe is weak to the strike of the element that beats hers: the ring stays (Fire > Ice > Lightning > Fire, Light ⇄ Dark), read one level up. The Thunder Tome deals Lightning, the Blizzard Tome Ice.

Fire foeLightning beats FireFlurry
Ice foeFire beats IceHeavy
Lightning foeIce beats LightningArea
Light foeDark beats LightHeavy
Dark foeLight beats DarkFlurry

Two elements: read her first one. If one of her own elements is in that strike, she's weak to the strike none of hers is in: a Fire + Lightning foe is weak to Area. Her own element is never weak, so every kind of the strike chips her.

Charged moves

A lock breaks to a weak act: each Uma whose act lands a weak hit breaks one. "Charging · 3 locks" asks three weak acts. On the Duel they alternate an Arms act and a Tome act.
