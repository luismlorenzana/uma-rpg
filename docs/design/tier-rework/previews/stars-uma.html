The slot

On its own small orbit beside the three moons, starred and named for her template. Two nodes in every tier, never more; rebuilt at promotion with the tier's values; outside the foe budget (the balance party has no template). Hover a node.

Variance U4 (calling), U6 (knack), U10 (the moon); CP36's note and WP33 for node II. Ids template:1, template:2 in every world.

Five knacks: node I

Her knack is her top growth rate (ties: the higher base stat, then Speed, Stamina, Power, Guts, Wit). Node I gives +4 points to its stat × tier; Fleet and Gritty give shares, which don't scale. With Valor and Swiftness retired, they are the map's only evasion and Limit start.

Her lead: node II

Read from her calling: physical (Brawler, Spell Blade, Paladin) or magical (Mage, Summoner, Cleric). The first draft put +8 on her calling channel's offense; CP36's note and the signature accessory (WP33) move it to her calling channel's defense, so the template's damage stays one piece's. The Uma Templates pass decides.
