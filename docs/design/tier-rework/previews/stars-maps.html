    Full or Split: the owner's Pure or Dual, for the moons

    A weapon moon is five nodes of flat offense on her hits of a kind. Full: one kind at the full values. Split: two kinds at 0.7 each, the same five jewels. In Tier 3 a second moon joins: Full + Split serves all three of her weapons. Drawn for the Brawler's default set at Lv 50.

    What the Tier 2 moon adds to her act

    The choice, in one rule

    A Split is 1.4 Full moons spread over two kinds. Full beats Split when her damage on the Full's kind is more than 2⅓ times her damage on the Split's second kind.

    • The campaign (her acts spread over her weapons): Split wins for every class.
    • A tuned front (75% of her acts on one kind: a Rift path, a region she farms): Full wins for the pure classes and the healers.
    • A hybrid: a Split, as the Twin Orb is hers; her preset puts it on her lead half's two kinds and her Tier 3 Full on her lead half too, for her 70/30 (follow-up to D196 (stat balancing), 2026-10-25: was her lone half, SB10, EP29).
    • Tier 3: Full + Split beats Full + Full for every class in the campaign; Split + Split isn't offered (she slots three kinds).
