The Flourish, re-checked against SB18

As written (variance U9) the Flourish was an MP twist: Opening (her first skill each fight free), Even pace (skills 25% cheaper), Late reserve (3% of her max MP a round from round 3). SB18 then gave every Uma an MP regen and showed MP is pace over a region. Re-run on SB18's region model (stat-balance §7.2, A14; Tier 1): the share of a region's acts she casts her deepest skill, Lv 10 → 50. A red ring marks a twist that refunds a deep cast in a round.

Show the numbers: coverage and late-region power, every class and level
Not negligible: too big, and empty for the healers.Each twist adds +6% to +27% to a damage dealer's region power (Lv 50: +15–25%), 3 to 5 times the whole template's +5%. A healer already casts deep every act from Lv 40, so hers is worth nothing, and Even pace and Late reserve refund her deep cast in a round, which SB18 forbids. An MP twist small enough for the budget is about 3 MP a fight: nothing a chip can show.

Reshaped onto HP: 4% of her max HP a fight, in her stride's shape (UT6)

Stamina is HP in this game, and every stage starts at full HP, so an HP twist resets each fight and can't compound over a region. The same 4% for every stride and calling; only its shape differs. HP she has back, round by round, of a fight in which she is hit.

The budget, per calling

U12: a matched Uma (her calling's class, her family worn) gains about +5%, never less than nothing; the foes are priced without templates. Damage is her act (stat-balance §8): the signature alone. Defense is what she can take in a hard fight: five rounds, a regular's 100% hit every round, half physical and half magical, through 12% evasion; the bar spans her knacks (Clever and Gritty low, Fleet high), the tick is today's median Uma (Mighty). Pace is 0: no template piece touches MP. The band is 4–6%.
