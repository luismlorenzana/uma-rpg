What it meansExtra Stats alone keeps her within 1.6 points of the budget's 28% from Lv 10 to Lv 50; armor, with the support's worth it took in (WP15), is the margin. The budget's formula prices points, so no foe moves, and raising Guard or Vigor would only raise the foes' offense with them. MP isn't in the budget either: the Springs test checks it, its knobs legs' MP first, then Vigor's MP rate.
