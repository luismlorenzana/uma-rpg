Tier 1 Apprentice: 40 huts

40 huts, 4 a yard, drawn 2 × 2. Four pips under each name: one per hut. No upgrades; every hut works at today’s level-3 speed (×1.25). Each Uma works her own row: gathering on the left, making on the right.

Tier 2 Adept: 60 huts

60 huts. Each yard adds two (ringed in the Tier 2 colour), drawn 3 × 2. Pips show three faces and “+3”. Gathering yards add the Tier 2 pair; a secondary profession lets an Uma gather one other yard’s base pair.

Tier 3 Master: 80 huts

80 huts. Two more a yard (ringed in the Tier 3 colour), drawn 4 × 2: four 24 px tiles, 96 px, inside a yard’s 112 px. Pips show three faces and “+5”. The secondary reaches its yard’s Tier 2 pair too.
