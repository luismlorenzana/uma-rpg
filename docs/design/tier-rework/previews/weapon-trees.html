Weapon trees, every kind and tier

Weapons own which kinds she hits with and how hard a weak hit lands, never a hit's shape. Tier 1 is a straight line; at Tier 2's G5 a weapon becomes Pure or Dual with its fixed partner; in Tier 3 both lines fork again, Pure to Keen or Tempered, Dual to Tempered or Evolved. The owner's review (2026-10-24) fixed the partners and gave the Dual line its Tier 3 fork. Pick a kind and a tier: every grade with its Might, gate and item (hover), both partners, and the caps. Below, all ten trees, the partner map, and the numbers that keep weaknesses a puzzle. From weapon-progression.md §3; the other pages: armor · accessories · materials.

Every set can branch

Her slotted kinds stay all different, partners included, so a weapon whose partner she already slots can't be Dual in that set. The partners were picked so every class's default and reverse set keeps a legal Dual in Tier 2, and the four classes between keep two two-kind weapons in Tier 3 (a mirror of an owner's pair would lock the Rune Knight, the Bishop or the Sorcerer out).

Coverage: weaknesses stay a puzzle

How many members of the campaign's default party answer a regular foe, weighted by the world's 48 signatures. Tier 1 foes are weak to a twin set; from Tier 2 a regular is weak to one kind of it. The rule: never above Tier 1's line. With the fixed partners and the caps the party lands at 2.0 and 2.30; without the count cap it would pass Tier 1.
