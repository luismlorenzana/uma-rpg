Weapon trees, every kind and tier

Weapons own which kinds she hits with and how hard a weak hit lands, never a hit's shape. Tier 1 is a straight line; at Tier 2's G5 a weapon becomes Pure or Dual; Tier 3 deepens it to Keen or Tempered. Pick a kind and a tier: every grade with its Might, gate and item (hover), the branches, the second-kind options and what each answers, and the caps. Below, the numbers that keep weaknesses a puzzle. Coverage: weaknesses stay a puzzle

How many members of the campaign's default party answer a regular foe, weighted by the world's 48 signatures. Tier 1 foes are weak to a twin set; from Tier 2 a regular is weak to one kind of it. The rule: never above Tier 1's line. The recommended caps land there; free or uncapped Duals would not.

Who holds each kind

Members of the default party holding each kind, on the recommended picks. Tier 1 leaves five kinds with one holder; each member's one Dual in Tier 2 fills exactly those five, so every kind has two; Tier 3's second two-kind weapons add a third to some.

Bosses keep their twins, so their shields grow with their answers

Regulars narrow to one kind from Tier 2; bosses keep both twins, so every Dual widens the party's reach on them. The draft scales a boss's shield with her answers, which keeps rounds to the first Break (shield ÷ answers) level across the tiers.

Might by grade and tier

A weapon adds its grade's Might to the offense of its hits: Tier 1's table × 1.8 and × 2.6 (SB1), so its share of her offense holds at every tier's Lv 50. The branches' multipliers don't scale: a share already holds.
