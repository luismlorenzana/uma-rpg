Weapon tree

Every weapon kind's progression, Tier 1 to 3 (gear.md §3–§6). Tier 1 is a line. Each later tier adds one fork at G5. Forms carry through promotion; grades restart at G0.

OverviewTier 1Tier 2Tier 3 FormsBy class

The whole tree

A weapon takes one step a tier. One that never reached G5 in Tier 2 takes Dual or Pure in Tier 3. Every kind follows this tree: five Arms, five Tomes.

Scroll the line. Each step spends 1 Weapon Gem of that grade, once she has the level.

  • One kind: the weapon opens its tree, and its hits carry its kind for weaknesses.
  • Might adds to the offense of its own hits. Nothing else changes.
  • No choices but which three weapons her class slots.
Today it takes a crafted piece per grade, bound in order, worn up to a Weapon Rank earned by using it. Proposed: the weapon is hers at G0; a gem raises it; her Uma Level is the only gate.

G6–G10: Might +26 … +68
  • A second kind is coverage, not a second channel: a Greatsword + Ice hit stays physical, with the Greatsword's Might.
  • One weak hit still removes one point and breaks one lock, whichever kind matched.
  • Breaks stay the skill trees' job: no form removes an extra shield point; Break +1 is a Tier 2 skill pick.
  • Re-forging takes another fork gem; the grade stays.

Each fork gem shares its form's name. Every form keeps the Might of its grade. A weapon's kinds are all different. Limits: three weapons for good, one fork a tier, a weak hit at most ×1.7. Breaks stay the trees'.

A weapon is shared by every class that slots it, so the rule reads at the class. A Greatsword + Ice held by a Brawler hits as a plain Greatsword; its sheet says "Ice: not as a Brawler". Slot splits are classes.md's to set; these are the draft.
