Extra Stats: the jewels

Two jewel kinds in every tier, Planet and Moon, a numeral for the node each opens. The owner's review made every class equal: 20 · 25 · 30 jewels an Uma, whatever her class. The weapon moons are moons, so they take Moon Jewels; the Halo Jewels retire. The recipes are the interface for profession-progression, which owns the amounts: proposed, a Planet Jewel of 12 so the Lapidary stays at 120 in Tier 1. Revised 2026-10-24. Every jewel, by grade and tier

Grade g is made at Craft Lv 5g − 4 and set at Uma Lv 5g − 4. The type at each grade is profession-progression's ladder (PP9): Tier 2 moves grades 5 and 10 onto its pair; Tier 3 grades 4 and 9 onto Tier 2's and 5 and 10 onto its own. Colour is the gathering profession; the left stripe marks a Tier 2 or Tier 3 type.

Recipe options for profession-progression

Prowess cuts Tier 1 from 25 jewels to 20 for the four classes between. At today's recipes the Lapidary would make 100 an Uma and the magical trade (Monster Den · Lapidary) carry 17% of the work. Three options; each dot is one trade's share of an Uma's work (gather, process, make), the band the fifth each should carry.
