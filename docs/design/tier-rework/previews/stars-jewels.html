Three kinds, a numeral for the node it opens

Planet Jewel III opens Planet III; Moon Jewel III opens node III of any moon (the moon is picked when it is set); Halo Jewel IV opens Halo IV. Hover a jewel for its recipe. Faded: not in the tier you picked.

When each can be made and set

Grade g is made at Craft Lv 5g − 4 and set at Uma Lv 5g − 4 of her tier, the gear rule. In Tier 1 grades 1–5 take a profession's low type, 6–10 its high type (gathered from Craft Lv 26); from Tier 2 the ladder moves the top of each band onto the tier's pairs (profession-progression PP9). Something opens every five levels.

Recipes: the interface for profession-progression.md

One fixed amount a jewel, the same in every tier (PR10: base recipes never change); which tier's type fills it at each grade is profession-progression.md's ladder (PP9), outlined in gold where it moves. A moon is cut stones on a metal mount; the Planet a bit of everything; a Halo Jewel is its tier in one jewel, one refined item from each gathering profession's new pair.

Per Uma, per tier

Shared jewels, with her moon's two beside them. Tier 2 adds Valor's two Moon Jewels and Halo Jewels I–II; Tier 3 Swiftness's two and Halo Jewels III–IV.

Materials an Uma, type by type

Every moon open, her moon apart (it adds 3 Cut Quartz, 1 Copper Ingot, 3 Cut Garnet and 1 Iron Ingot in every tier).

A party and a run

A party on the owner's defaults (two of five, the Brawler and the Mage, skip a moon); a Rift run is twenty Umas, four parties. Make time at the Lapidary is one gather's time a refined item: 0.6 h at Craft Lv 25 in a ×1.25 slot.

Against the first round's plan (README §2.3 before the cross-check)

Tiers 2 and 3 drop the first round's "resized from Tier 2": no single amount fits 27 and 29 jewels at 120. With the branch gem a G5 Weapon Gem (profession-progression), every tier sits inside PR10's tenth: +3.7%, +7.4%.
