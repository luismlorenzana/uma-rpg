Weaknesses v2: every foe her own list

Today every foe of a stage shares one two-kind list, read from the stage's element, so half the party has nothing to break. The proposal brings back v15's variety and rank progression, raises the floor to three kinds for every foe, and keeps today's shields.

Design: docs/design/tier-rework/weaknesses-v2.md (WK1–WK12). The party is the default five: Brawler (Greatsword, Axe, Lance), Mage (Fire, Blizzard, Thunder), Spell Blade (Katana, Lance, Thunder), Cleric (Light, Blizzard, Bow), Summoner (Dark, Light, Bow). Solid chips are Arms, dashed ones elements. Under each foe, the members who can hit her weak are green (Br Brawler, Ma Mage, Sp Spell Blade, Cl Cleric, Su Summoner).

Engine v17, the five-class party on curve, first try on Auto, every Tier 1 stage at the level, 8 seeds; only the weakness lists change. Win rates are 100% in every cell. The proposal is within ±0.3 rounds and ±0.4 Breaks of v15, about 10% shorter than today; bosses break in 92–100% of fights (today 74–99%).

The calls

WK1 · Lists by rank: regulars (and Keys and guards) 2 Arms + 2 elements; sub-bosses and bosses 3, 2 + 1 or 1 + 2 seeded per foe. Both channels on every foe.

WK2 · The ring leads the elements: the element that beats hers first, a pair's second answer next, the rest dealt from the stage's element deck, never her own; Light + Dark draw from Fire, Ice and Lightning.

WK3 · The Arms come from the stage's deck: five shuffled by the stage's seed, dealt in slot order across the waves; no Arm twice, never the previous foe's Arms again.

WK4 · Fixed per stage: seeded by stage and slot, shown on the stage sheet, the same on every visit and Sweep.

WK5 · Shields stay: regulars 2–3, sub-bosses 6, bosses 8: the simulation brings back v15's pace without a change.

WK6 · Locks stay: 2–4 drawn from her list; a longer list spreads them.

WK7 · Icons only on a fight card: four icons and the shield fit a quarter-width card; the stage sheet keeps "Weak:".

WK8 · Tiers 2 and 3: every rank at 3: a regular's split by her weaker defense (strikers and guards 1 + 2, casters and skirmishers 2 + 1); shields by CP8, set by R4 and R5.

WK9 · The Rift unchanged: its fronts draw by front (CP7), the specialized parties' place; the twins stay as its vocabulary.

WK10 · One helper for every campaign generator: regions, capitals' Finals, scenario chapters.

WK11 · Engine v18: generation only; v17's fights replay and its generation is frozen; R3–R5 become v19–v21.

WK12 · The checks: every foe both channels and 2+ members; no stage with one list; every member 75%+ of foes; the simulation within ±0.5 of v15.
