Foe weaknesses, by kind and by tier

The ten weapon kinds are the weakness types (the owner, 2026-10-23). A Tier 1 foe is weak to her twins: the element that beats hers on the ring, and that element's twin Arm. Later tiers narrow the list by rank; bosses keep both in every tier. Every default and guided party answers every Tier 1 foe at least twice. From class-progression.md §4. The ring and the twins

Twins are an Arm and the element that share a shape in the catalog (D86). The ring is today's: Fire beats Ice, Ice beats Lightning, Lightning beats Fire; Light and Dark beat each other. Pick a foe's element: she is weak to what beats it, and to its twin Arm.

Two elements: she reads her lead element first (a pair signature's lead alternates by slot); if its answer is one of her own, her second. Light and Dark together answer to no element: she is weak to the Arms of her own two, the Bow and the Katana (Twilight), as today's Light + Dark foes break on weapons alone.

By tier and rank: the list narrows

A Fire foe at each rank and tier. Regulars narrow to one kind in Tier 2 (the channel her weaker defense guards: strikers and guards her element, casters and skirmishers her Arm), sub-bosses in Tier 3; bosses keep both, so every Uma can always answer a boss. Gold edge: narrowed this tier.

The Rift's fronts

The fronts draw by the run's seed, from the front, never the face. Today's counts and wards stay in Tier 1; the Duel's and the Horde's draws become twins, so the guided parties always hold an answer.

Coverage: who answers what

Members whose default weapons answer each set (an Arm or the element of the twins; one kind for the single-kind rows). Tier 1's tests ask for two or more everywhere: every cell meets it. Tier 2's single kinds are answered by the default party on defaults; the Horde's keys ask for a pick or a Dual (red: nobody on defaults). Hover a cell for who.
