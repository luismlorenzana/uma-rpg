Tier 1 · Apprentice · dayThe base: one rule per system

Three weapons by channel on the owner's defaults; foes weak to their twins. Every piece owned at G0, one gate. One fork a tree, no points. The Planet and three moons. One trade per Uma. Her template's calling, Flourish, signature accessory and moon.

Tier 2 · Adept · duskOne more choice in every category

A class branch (a weapon-group focus), Pure or Dual at G5, a second accessory slot and its branches, branch C and the tip fork, a weapon moon, a secondary profession, her signature tip. The foes price it.

Tier 3 · Master · nightDepth on the same frame

The branch's capstone and the class's mastery, Keen, Tempered or Evolved, a third accessory slot a step deeper, two tree branches held and depth 6, a second weapon moon. Every flat value × 2.6.

Choices a player owns, per Uma

Every build decision she keeps, counted for a Spell Blade (a class with every kind of choice), at the tier's Lv 50. Today's Tier 1 asks for 68; the rework's Tier 1 asks for 8, Tier 2 for 23 and Tier 3 for 31.

Counted as: Classes, her class (+ her class branch from Tier 2; the capstone follows the branch, the mastery is the class's). Gear, one weapon a slot, each weapon branch (Pure or Dual; Tier 3's step), armor halves where she picks them (the four between, from Tier 2), each accessory slot's family and form. Skill trees, each fork and tip held (CP §6.8). Extra Stats, her weapon moons' settings (EP §7). Professions, the secondary (PP14). Her template asks only the one-time calling pick of a Pace or Late Uma; the signature tip is a tree pick.
