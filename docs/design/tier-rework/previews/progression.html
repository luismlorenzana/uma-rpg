Numbers are the cross-referenced drafts (README §2 and §10). One Uma's page at each tier is character-tiers.

Tier 1 · Apprentice · dayThe base: one rule per system

Three weapons, one per strike. Every piece of gear climbs G0 → G10 on one gate. One fork per tree, no points. One planet and four moons. One trade per Uma.

Tier 2 · Adept · duskOne more choice in every category

A specialization, a weapon form at G5, the Damage slot, a third branch and a tip fork, the Halo and Valor, a secondary profession, the signature tip.

Tier 3 · Master · nightDepth on the same frame

A mastery, a second weapon form, a second Support, two branches held and depth 6 capstones, the Halo's second kind and Swiftness.
