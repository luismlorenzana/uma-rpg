What each tier adds

Tier 1 The base
  • Classes: six, three weapon slots one per strike; foes weak to one strike
  • Gear: 3 weapons, 5 armor, 1 Support, each G0–G10; one item a grade
  • Skill trees: 3 trees, one free pick each
  • Extra Stats: a planet and four moons, 25 nodes, all counting
  • Professions: one trade, two yards, two material types each
  • Variance: calling, Flourish, her moon (2 nodes)
Picks: 3 trees + a weapon a strike + a support0 currencies
Tier 2 One more choice in every category
  • Classes: a specialization (1 of 2); foes weak to 2 kinds of their strike
  • Gear: a G5 fork a weapon (Dual or Pure); the Damage slot; armor halves per piece
  • Skill trees: branch C and a tip fork on every tree (+3 picks)
  • Extra Stats: the Halo (one kind, a free setting) and Valor (+4 nodes)
  • Professions: +2 huts a yard, a Tier 2 pair, a secondary gathering profession
  • Variance: the signature tip
+1 class pick+3 forms+3 tree picks+1 Halo kind+1 secondary
Tier 3 Depth on the same frame
  • Classes: a mastery; foes weak to 1 kind of their strike
  • Gear: a second fork a weapon (Prism, Tempered, Keen); a second Support
  • Skill trees: depth 6; two branches held (+3 picks)
  • Extra Stats: the Halo's second kind and Swiftness (+4 nodes)
  • Professions: +2 huts a yard, a Tier 3 pair; the secondary reaches its Tier 2 pair
  • Variance: the tip at both forks of her signature tree
+3 forms+3 tree picks+1 Support+1 Halo kind
