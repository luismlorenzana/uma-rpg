Tier 1 · ApprenticeSix classes, three weapons each

Slots follow the class's Arms count. Foes are weak to two kinds, twins. One fork a tree, no points. A one-time calling pick for Pace and Late Umas.

Tier 2 · Adept+ a specialization

One of two passives a class, swapped freely. Branch C and the Power or Break tip. Regular foes narrow to one kind; bosses keep two.

Tier 3 · Master+ a mastery

One mechanic a class. Two branches held, depth 6 capstones, the master slot. Sub-bosses narrow too; bosses keep two.

Party shapes and what they answer

One healer a party; everything else is free. The default party is one of each fight type; the four Rift parties are the fronts' guidance (rift §5). Each cell counts the members whose default weapons answer a Tier 1 foe's twins (her Arm or her element): two or more everywhere. Hover a cell for who.

Twins: the world, the Duel, the Horde

A Tier 1 foe is weak to one twin set: an Arm and its twin element.
