Party shapes: the campaign and the Rift

The owner's distinction (2026-10-24). The campaign (Lv 1–50) is balanced parties: one of each fight type on the defaults holds every element and damage type, foes keep today's stats and stay element-focused. The Rift is where specialized parties come in, rebuilt and re-specced per path: each path's guided build is its composition and every member's three weapons (gold ring: re-specced). Cells count the members who answer a Tier 1 foe's twins (her Arm or her element).
