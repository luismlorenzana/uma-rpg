Armor by slot, grade and tier

With the defensive Talismans and Amulets retired, armor carries every defense: HP, DEF, M.DEF, MP and evasion, plus a little offense on her half. It takes one support's worth more than today, so a regular's hit lands where the foes' budget expects, with nothing for the owner to pick. Values are Tier 1's × 1.8 and × 2.6 (SB1); evasion is a share and stays. From weapon-progression.md §4; the other pages: weapon trees · accessories · materials.

The suit, at any grade

Five slots; the slot holds the grade, one Armor Fittings item a grade. The half only picks the piece she shows and the offense it adds; every defensive stat is the slot's. Tier 1 wears her class's half; from Tier 2 the four between pick a half per piece (flip a slot).

Per slot, by grade

Each slot's stat by grade in Tier 1, 2 and 3, against today's (dashed, the same in every tier). Defense doubles, HP grows half again, MP a half; evasion stays 1.2% a grade in every tier.

The defensive budget

A regular foe's 100% hit as a share of her HP at the tier's Lv 50, fully built (the foe's offense set so today's on-curve Brawler takes 20%). One support used to cut one channel 15% (the Iron Talisman, the Ward Amulet); armor now takes about half of that on both channels: every class 1.3–1.8 points better than today's armor, in every tier.

Show the numbers
Why this muchH60 priced every support at about the same win-rate gain, so one support's worth is the yardstick: the Iron Talisman takes a Brawler's physical hit from 20% to 17%. Spread over both channels, since armor guards both, that is about 18.5% on each; armor's +50 HP, +20 DEF, +20 M.DEF land at 18.7%. A full support on both channels would be a support and a half, and the foes' offense budget would have to rise to match.

The halves, by class and tier

The half sets the piece's name and its offense (ATK on a physical piece, M.ATK on a magical one), never its defense, so changing half never trades a defensive point. Armor sets stay dropped: nothing counts a suit.
