Max stats by tier, and where every point comes from

An Uma's full sheet at each tier's Lv 50, fully built: every gear slot at G10, every Extra Stats node open, her template matched to her class. Each stat is split by source, in points and as a share. Pick a class.

Assumed Uma: the balance simulation's average outfit (base 110 in every race stat, +10% growth in Speed, Stamina and Power), at lifetime Lv 50, 100 and 150. Formulas: HP = 100 + 5 Stamina + 2 Power + Guts; MP = 20 + 1.5 Wit; ATK and M.ATK from Speed; DEF from Power; M.DEF from Power and Wit; each × her class's lean. Build: armor G10 (+20 DEF, +100 HP, +20 MP, +20 M.DEF, +25 to her half's offense, 12% evasion); the planet (+21 to all six; HP points ×5); her class's moons (+30, Vigor +150 HP; a Brawler or Mage skips the other offense moon); her template's lead node (+8 to her channel). Offense on her lead kind adds the weapon's Might (+68 at G10), and from Tier 2 the Orb (+34) and the Halo (+34) on that same kind.
