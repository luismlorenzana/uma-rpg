Tier 2: the class branch is a weapon-group focus

The owner's second pass (2026-10-24): "I still don't like the class specializations in later tiers, feels like almost nothing changes." His model: the Brawler's branch 1 adds damage on her main Arms, branch 2 only on the secondary ones; the Mage the same with her Tomes; reverse options for every other class, "so in my party comps I would bring one party with an Elemental Mage and a regular Summoner, but in another party a Light/Dark Mage and an Elemental Summoner." Each branch is now a focus: +15% damage on its group's kinds, its own set of three weapons (one tap; free at Ascend), and three class skills. From §7.2 (CP38–CP41).

What grows on her three weapons, tier by tier

The owner (2026-10-24): "do the DPS classes unlock specialized hits on the 3 weapons, or more weapon damage types in later tiers?" Both, each with one home: kinds grow on the weapon (a Dual adds its fixed partner, of its own channel and the other group; two kinds at most), how hard a weak hit lands grows on the weapon too (Pure, then Keen), specialized hits grow in the trees, and a percentage on a group in her class branch's focus. Pick a class; in Tier 2 click a weapon's branch (one Dual in her slots), in Tier 3 fork it again (a Pure Keen or Tempered, a Dual Tempered or Evolved; two two-kind weapons, the Brawler and the Mage one). From §3.5.

Redrawn to the research note (second pass).Its two flags on this page and §3.5: a Brawler drawn with all five Arms in Tier 2 and cross-channel Duals (a Katana + Dark Tome). Now, as WR2–WR5 recommend and weapon-progression.md decides (WP6–WP9; the drawn picks are its §3.8's): at G5 Pure (weak ×1.5) or Dual (a second kind of its own channel), no third branch; one Dual in her slots in Tier 2, two in Tier 3, the Brawler and the Mage one for good. Prism stays dropped (owner, 2026-10-24). Follow-up to the weapon review (2026-10-24): a Dual's second kind is the weapon's fixed partner, always of the other weapon group (WP32); Tier 3 forks both lines, Pure to Keen (×1.7) or Tempered, Dual to Tempered or Evolved (its Tier 3 partner, WP9). A partner she already slots is refused.

Her template on the trees: affinity

The owner asked for a proposal that "won't explode across the 3 tiers", maybe "% damage bonuses for specific weapon types". The call (CP36): each Uma Template names one twin set, an Arm and its element, and she deals +6% damage with those two kinds, every tier, in any class. From Tier 2 her ★ signature tip sits on her affinity kind's tree; the Flourish stays at the root. Which set a template names is the Uma Templates pass's (draft: her stride's pair). From §6.9. Second pass: kept as it is; the owner finds Uma uniqueness "still pretty weak, but let's not overcomplicate until we see Uma uniqueness on all other classes", so the Uma Templates pass revisits it. Proposed follow-up (the owner's weapon review, 2026-10-24): weapon-progression's signature accessory (WP33) replaces it, decided in the Uma Templates pass (§6.9's note).

Lv 50 stats under SB1
