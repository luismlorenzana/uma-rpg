Class templates, weapons and stats

Every class's template sheet at Tier 1, 2 and 3: its three weapon slots, five armor pieces and one to three accessories, drawn with the standard preset in them. Then which weapons fit which slot, what a class switch costs, and each class's Lv 50 sheet under the stat budget rule (SB1). What grows on her three weapons, tier by tier

The owner (2026-10-24): "do the DPS classes unlock specialized hits on the 3 weapons, or more weapon damage types in later tiers?" Both, each with one home: kinds grow on the weapon (a Dual branch, two kinds at most), specialized hits grow in the trees and in the Specialized branch, and flat offense in the Orb and the Halo. Pick a class; in Tier 2 click a weapon's branch, and Tier 3 deepens the one she chose. From §3.5.

Owner, 2026-10-24: Prism dropped; designed in weapon-progression.md.Three kinds on one weapon "could trivialize the weakness concept". From Tier 2 each weapon takes one branch at G5: Specialized (more damage on its own kind), Dual (one more kind, coverage only, never more than two) or a third branch still to be designed; Tier 3 deepens the chosen branch and never stacks kinds. The first round's forms (Dual or Pure; Prism, Tempered or Keen) are history: gear.md §4.1, §5.1, the first-round weapon tree.

Equippable weapons

Each row is one slot of one class; each column a kind. A filled cell is the default (the number is the slot), a tinted cell fits. Any three different kinds of the slots' channels fit, in every tier. From Tier 2 a weapon's Dual branch may add a kind: the last column says which second kinds count for that class (GE7; the branches are weapon-progression.md's).

What a class switch costs, from the defaults

She owns every kind; armor and accessory grades sit on the slot. A switch costs only the new class's weapons she hasn't raised: 10 Weapon Gems each to bring one from G0 to G10. Cells count the new class's defaults she would still have to raise, from the row class's defaults fully raised.

Her template on the trees: affinity

The owner asked for a proposal that "won't explode across the 3 tiers", maybe "% damage bonuses for specific weapon types". The call (CP36): each Uma Template names one twin set, an Arm and its element, and she deals +6% damage with those two kinds, every tier, in any class. From Tier 2 her ★ signature tip sits on her affinity kind's tree; the Flourish stays at the root. Which set a template names is the Uma Templates pass's (draft: her stride's pair). From §6.9.
