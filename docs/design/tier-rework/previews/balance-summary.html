Stat balance: three problems, three fixes

The balancing pass checked every number the four approved docs set, tier by tier. Almost everything holds. Three things don't. Here is each one in a sentence, why it happens, the fix, and one picture of before and after. Every chart opens its numbers underneath.

Later tiers outrun the foes

SB3SB4SB5SB6
What goes wrong

A Tier 2 or Tier 3 party clears its fights far faster than a Tier 1 party does, so the later tiers stop feeling like a challenge.

Why

Each approved doc added its own new tools for Tier 2 and 3 and said the foes would pay for them, but the foes' formula was sized before all the tools arrived: together they add about half again to a party's damage in Tier 2 and nearly double it in Tier 3.

The fix

Make the foes' per-tier toughness count the whole toolbox: Tier 2's +15% becomes +27%, Tier 3 gets its own (+8% rising to +48%), bosses a tenth more, and boss shields one more point.

The hybrid mirror drifts

SB10SB11
What goes wrong

The Spell Blade and the Summoner should hit equally hard, each 70% her own way; on the new maps the Summoner pulls ahead and turns nearly half and half.

Why

The new offense moon gives the Summoner real ATK for her Bow, whose hand was set high back when it had none, and the preset accessories pile flat damage onto her minor half.

The fix

A hybrid's single-kind accessory and her Full weapon moon go on her lead half, and the Summoner's Arms hand drops from 0.56 to 0.48.

MP runs dry over a region

SB18 (new)SB14
What goes wrong

Over a region's twelve stages the Mage, the Summoner and the Cleric run out of MP, and late in a region the whole party fights at under half today's power.

Why

The MP regen accessories were removed because everyone would always wear one, and a bigger MP bar took their place; but a bigger bar empties just the same, because nothing brings MP back.

The fix

Every Uma regains a little MP each round, as a rule of the game rather than an item, so it is never a pick: 0.8% of her max MP for each skill depth she has, 4% once all five are open.
