Who works where

An Uma works her trade, by her calling. From Tier 2 she also picks a secondary, any of the other eight professions, and does its base work. The tier's own pair, and every grade made of it, stay the main trade's: "the main one reaches its higher tiers".

A world with no Uma of a trade opens that trade's two places to everyone (the open shop, PP16), until one arrives.
