The star chart

The Stars tab's Extra Stats map, redrawn: every body a true constellation, every node numbered and tappable, nothing placed by eye. The rules, gates and values on this page are the live game's. Tap any star or the middle of a body.

Today, as live

Proposed

What was wrong

  1. The big loop isn't a circle. Three arcs of one radius forced through three points that aren't equally far from the Planet, so it bulges and cuts through Prowess's label.
  2. The stars sit off their rings. The Planet's ring has radius 34 and its nodes radius 38. Prowess's label is centred 10 px away from its own nodes.
  3. ★ Longblade overflows its ring, and the longest template name is longer.
  4. Nothing says what comes next. Twenty-two identical circles: no order, no numerals, no line between stars.
  5. The map tells you nothing without a tap. A name is the only text, and the totals below it are detached from it.

What the chart does instead

  • Each body draws its own figure: Prowess a spearhead, Vigor a pentagon, Guard a shield, the Planet a true ring.
  • Numerals on the stars are the jewels' numerals: a Moon Jewel III opens a III.
  • Lines light up as nodes open, always clockwise from I. A finished body closes its figure.
  • One tap on a star or a body opens its sheet, with all five values and the jewel.
  • Green stays Ready's, and only Ready pulses.

Four states, and only four

Colour says which body; fill says how far along. A tired eye can read the chart by shape alone.

Iconography: the brief for the art packet

Drafts, to fix the silhouettes. The art packet draws them as pixel art in uma-art's ui atlas, in the house style of the class emblems: five 24 px crests and eight 16 px stat marks.

The geometry, checked

Computed on this page from the same table the packet ships. At 375 wide the chart is 343 px, so a viewBox unit is 0.95 px.
