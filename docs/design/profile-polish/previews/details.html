Details: damage first

The Details sheet opens on what her hits are made of. Every raw number stays, one tap behind All stats. Each weapon's number is the exact sum a fight uses before the foe's defense, with every source on its row: her base ATK or M.ATK, the weapon's Might, her accessories, her armor, her Extra Stats and her signature.

What each number is

Each row adds up every source, and its total is what a fight uses, so it can't drift. A test builds a real fight to prove it.

Your questions

  • Is damage always one of the ten kinds? Yes: every Uma hit has a kind, and each row is one of her three.
  • Did armor lose its ATK? No. Each piece still adds 1–5, by grade, to ATK or M.ATK by her class's half. It was hidden inside "gear 13", which was armor 10 + Charm 3.
  • Why did the colors go? The old Battle tab colored gear blue and Extra Stats green. Details (H119) printed them plain, and no decision asked for that. They come back, and ATK and M.ATK get their own.
  • Why didn't the text cleanup catch this? H117 cut sentences to labels and numbers. "Might · Fire Tome +8" passes that test: the fault was too many rows, not too many words.

What this is not

  • Not the hit you'll see in a fight. A hit is this number² ÷ (this number + the foe's DEF), × the skill's power, weakness and the rest. It's the number you can raise.
  • Bosses +3% stays apart: the Insignia multiplies, so it can't be added in. It shows beside the title, only when she has one.
  • A healer's Mends joins the tiles below: it's the number a Paladin or a Cleric is built on.

The colors, measured

Each color is on the panel and the raised card, in both themes, as 11 px text. Neighbours stay apart under colour-blind simulation, and the order never changes, so nothing depends on colour alone.
