Reclass: three classes for every calling

Approved and built (reclass.md, D290, D291). The Warrior and the Mage promote into three Tier 1 classes, the Spellblade, the Summoner and the Healer into four: the pure roots stay pure, the Spellblade and the Summoner are kin and each opens a healer, and the Healer opens both hybrids' other halves. Six new links, nothing closed.

Before

Spellblade, Summoner and Healer reached two classes inside their family.

Built

Every Tier 0 reaches three or four. The new links are gold.

The four limits

1 · The pure roots stay pureThe Brawler opens only from the Warrior, the Sorcerer only from the Mage: the physical and the magical root, never a hybrid's side door.
2 · Each hybrid opens a healerThe Spellblade opens the Paladin (Arms), the Summoner the Cleric (Tomes); the owner's change. One healer a party still holds.
3 · The Spellblade and the Summoner are kin"Pacts with familiars" and "familiars, summoned": the Spellblade opens the Arms Summoner, the Summoner the Tome Spellblade, each on the side it leans to.
4 · The Healer opens both hybrids' other halvesThe Arms Spellblade and the Tome Summoner, one of each channel: her four are symmetric, Arms and Tome, healer and hybrid (the owner's change).
