# Skill names: one grammar for every tree

**Status:** a proposal for the owner's review, 2026-11-04. Nothing is built. The review page is
[previews/skill-names.html](previews/skill-names.html): all 70 trees and their 560 skills, today's name beside the proposed
one, with what each does. Its numbers come from the live catalog (engine v21).

## The owner's point

The Sorcerer's trees were named the Final Fantasy way, Fire → Fira → Firaga, but as the trees grew the names went all
over the place, and weapon skills can't follow that pattern. Propose consistent names for every skill.

## What's wrong today

- **Five patterns for one job.** The Sorcerer climbs a word (Fire, Fira, Firaga, Firaja), the Arms Spellblade puts a
  prefix ladder before a noun (Rune, Arcane, Mystic, Astral, Celestial Cleave) and the Tome Spellblade another (Edged,
  Honed, Whetted, Gleaming, Sovereign), the Summoners name a new creature at each depth, and the Brawler and the healers
  pick five unrelated words a tree (Heavy Slash, Cleave, Sunder, Rend, Meteor Strike).
- **Line B attaches four ways:** a word after (Firaga Wave, Knight Captain Pack), a word before (Piercing Mystic Cleave,
  Bonded Fire Drake, Heavy Rupture), a word in the middle (Rending Combo), or a new name (Skewer, Dragon Fang, Gungnir;
  Valkyrie's Oath, Rebirth).
- **Names that name something else:** the healers' Light tree has Faith (a status), their Dark tree Clarity and their
  Katana tree Hex (two retired statuses), their Lance tree Pierce Mind and Impale (two B lines), their Blizzard tree
  Sanctum (a Hall); the Brawler's Axe opens on Sweep (a shape and the Sweep Ticket), the Summoners' Greatsword climbs to
  Aegis Champion (a retired status), and the Spellblades' Thunder Bolt and Katana Draw are the Sorcerer's and the
  Brawler's B lines.
- **Names that say the wrong thing:** Triple Shot lands one arrow and Twin Arrows one; the healers' Axe tree (Hamstring,
  Cripple, Maim) wounds nothing, it Barriers.
- **Borrowed words:** Fira, Firaga, Thundaja and Holy are another game's spells; Gungnir and Raijin are a named spear and
  a god.

## 1. The grammar

**A tree has one word.** Every depth uses it, the same way in all 70 trees:

| Depth | Line A | Line B | The Brawler's Greatsword |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | the word | — | Cleave |
| 2 | **Greater** + the word | — | Greater Cleave |
| 3 | **Grand** + the word | A's name + the line's word | Grand Cleave · Grand Cleave Combo |
| 4 | **Supreme** + the word | A's name + the line's word | Supreme Cleave · Supreme Cleave Combo |
| 5 | **the capstone**, the tree's own | the capstone + the line's word | Meteor Strike · Meteor Strike Combo |

- **It reads like the old Fire → Firaga:** the word stays, the ladder says how far down the tree a skill is, and the
  capstone is the reward at the bottom, as Flare was.
- **Line B says three things at once:** which tree, which depth, which line. B is a variant of A at its depth, so its
  name is A's with the line after it.
- **Mirrored classes share their family's names,** as the Summoners and the healers do today: the Arms and Tome
  Spellblades share one set, so there are 40 tree words, not 70. Their B words still follow each class's line (a
  Summoner's Oath or Pack).
- **At most three words and 21 characters** ("Supreme Flurry Impale").

## 3. What changes and what doesn't

- **524 of the 560 skills change name** (the rest already fit: Meteor Strike, Maelstrom, Moonfall, Flare Star, Black
  Hole, Sword Saint, Phoenix, Ruin, Exaltation, Benediction…). There are 350 distinct names.
- **Two B lines are renamed** on the Class tab and in the manual: the Summoners' **Bonded → Oath** (a Bond is the Halls'
  Bond level) and the healers' **Heavy → Surge** (Heavy is the Greatsword's and Fire's shape). The others keep their
  names.
- **Unchanged:** node ids, saves and fights (a name is display only: no save or engine bump); Tier 0's five skills (Drill
  Strike, Feint, Vigil, Beckon, Primer); the sixteen Limit Breaks; the trees engine v18 and older replay with.
- **To build:** one packet: the names in `rpg/catalog.ts` for the trees the live engine plays, `RPG_BRANCH_B_NAME`'s two
  lines, the tests that pin names, and `pnpm docs:update` for [skill trees](../../game/skill-trees.md). Its packet confirms
  the replay pins don't read names.

## 4. Decisions to take

| Id | Call | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| SN1 | One grammar for every tree: a word for the tree, one ladder for its depths, a capstone at depth 5 | Yes |
| SN2 | The ladder: depth 1 the word, 2 Greater, 3 Grand, 4 Supreme, the same in all 70 trees | Yes |
| SN3 | Depth 5 is the tree's capstone, one or two words, in its family's theme | Yes |
| SN4 | Line B is line A's name at that depth with the line's word after it | Yes |
| SN5 | Mirrored classes share their family's names: both Spellblades, as the Summoners and the healers already do | Yes |
| SN6 | Two line renames: the Summoners' Bonded → Oath, the healers' Heavy → Surge | Yes |
| SN7 | A name never names something else: a status (retired ones too), another line, a shape, a place, a Limit Break or a Finals route | Yes |
| SN8 | No hit counts in names (Twin, Triple, Double) | Yes |
| SN9 | Original words only: the Fira and Firaga words, named gods and named weapons go | Yes |
| SN10 | Capstone themes: Brawler the falling sky, Sorcerer the cosmos, Spellblade an Edge, Summoner a legend, healer a sanctity or a doom | Yes |
| SN11 | A healer's tree is named for what it gives, never a wound it doesn't deal | Yes |
| SN12 | The Light tree's depth-3 B, which also revives, follows the rule (Grand Grace Surge); Valkyrie's Oath and Rebirth go | Yes |
| SN13 | At most three words and 21 characters | Yes |
| SN14 | Tier 0's five skills and the Limit Breaks keep their names | Yes |
| SN15 | Display only: ids, saves and fights unchanged; the live trees take the new names, older generations keep theirs for replays | Yes |

**Other ladders weighed for SN2:** numerals (Flame II, III, IV) read clearest but clash with Skill Ranks, Generals I–III
and window steps; made-up endings (Flamera, Flamega) are Final Fantasy's own trick.
