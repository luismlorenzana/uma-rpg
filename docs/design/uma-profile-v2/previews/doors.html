The map: doors in, the profile, links out

Show

Three layers, one rule each

The owner's three questions: where information is only for display, where it is complex and visual, and where changes happen (UP5).

1 · Header

Display: who she is, what she is doing

Portrait, name, title, rarity, level and EXP, the target. Party and Work are doors (Back returns); Details opens the one read-only sheet: battle stats with their parts, core stats, Craft Level.

2 · Tabs

Complex and visual: each system drawn

Class (the tree), Gear (the grid), Stars (the map), Template (her template). Facts sit on the drawing as labels and numbers. Four tabs, fixed: a new system joins one, never a fifth.

3 · Sheets

Changes: one sheet, one action

Tap an element: its detail, its choices (free, on tap) and one footer action (spending with cost and before → after; the one-time pick with a confirm). A sheet never opens a sheet.

Every door

Routes are /town/stable/outfit/:id/:tab. A door that names a tab wins; otherwise the tab remembered on this device. Every door pushes with the kit's back: true, so the place header's Back pops to it.
