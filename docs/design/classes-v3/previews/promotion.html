1 · A Tier 0 Uma: Recruit, Rank 7

Uma B, Lv 7. Her class tile counts to 10; her tree is one node, her skill in the selected weapon’s kind.

Class tab · Tier 0

What’s on the tab

  • Class tile: “Recruit · R7/10”, the bar to 10. Tap: the Classes sheet (the tree, §3 below).
  • Weapon tiles: any three of the ten; no line letter (Tier 0 has no fork).
  • Equipped 3/3: her skill in each weapon’s kind; the badge is her Class Rank (the skill’s rank).
  • The tree: one node, “Drill Strike · Lance · one foe · 1 hit · 1.97 · 10 MP”.
  • Promotion at R10 · 3 to go: the next moment, with its bar.
  • No dot until Rank 10: nothing to do yet.

Numbers

  • Tier 0 power by Class Rank: 1.52, 1.60, 1.67, 1.75, 1.82, 1.90, 1.97, 2.05, 2.12, 2.20.
  • Rank r arrives with Uma Lv r when she fights as it from Lv 1: 3,600 Class EXP to Rank 10.

2 · Rank 10: the promotion sheet

Uma A, Lv 10, Recruit Rank 10 with 400 Class EXP waiting. The Class tab carries the dot; the class tile reads Promote. The sheet is open: pick a class and Promote. Close it to see the tab; promote to see her new class at Rank 10.

The promotion sheet

The sheet

  • Tiles show what differs: slots, the three defaults, a one-line role. One is Suggested (Recruit: the Brawler).
  • Before → after in one line: “Recruit R10 → Brawler R10 · depths 1–2 · 6 skills · +400 EXP”.
  • One action: Promote to …, free. No confirm: it closes nothing, and the siblings stay open.
  • After: she fights as it at Rank 10, its default weapons on; the tab redraws on her first weapon’s tree; the dot goes.

Rules behind it (§2.6)

  • Each child opens at Class Rank 10: Tier 0 is the old Ranks 1–10, so every depth comes before the world’s foes expect it.
  • EXP past Rank 10 waits on the Tier 0 and goes with her next promotion.
  • Promoting into a sibling later is free: her Recruit stays at Rank 10.

3 · The Classes sheet: the class tree, the reclass view

Uma C, Lv 34: a Brawler R34 who also holds Arms Spellblade R12, has her Recruit at Rank 10 and started an Acolyte (R4). Tap any tile: the footer becomes that class’s one action.

Classes sheet

States (profile v2’s state language, UP23)

  • On (fill): her active class. Footer: Standard preset.
  • Available (raised): a class she holds. Footer: Fight as …, free.
  • Ready (green dot): a child of a Tier 0 at Rank 10. Footer: Promote to … (the promotion sheet).
  • Open (dashed): a Tier 0 she hasn’t started. Footer: Start … · R1, free.
  • Closed (faded): footer disabled with its gate, “Opens with Duelist R10 or Apprentice R10”.

Replaces

  • Profile v2’s Class sheet (“six class tiles in two rows”) and its Class Ranks grid: every rank is on its tile.
  • Class Manuals › is a door to the Exchange’s sheet with her picked; Back returns here (UP30: no spending in the profile).
