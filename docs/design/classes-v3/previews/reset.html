The rule

Her Manuals

Σ over her classes of ⌈ ( EXP + min(EXP, 1,800) ) ÷ 1,000 ⌉

  • A class’s EXP: what its Class Rank took on today’s curve (120 + 16 × rank a rank), plus its bar.
  • Its first 1,800 twice: the Tier 0 ladder to Rank 10 costs 3,600 where the old Ranks 1–10 cost 1,800. So every class she had rebuilds to its rank, each from its own Tier 0.
  • Rounded up, class by class: a Manual is read onto one class, 1,000 at a time.
  • Later tiers: no Tier 0, so 1:1.

Why Class Marks

A Class Manual isn’t an item you hold: the Exchange reads Manuals for 1,000 Class Marks each, at once, onto one class of one Uma. So the reset pays 1,000 Class Marks for every Manual she’s owed, into her world’s wallet, and the Exchange’s sheet spends them: no new item, no new screen. Pooled, they can go first to the Umas who fight. Each Uma’s share is recorded, so the sheet lists it and the Exchange offers it as a count.

Weapon Rank: there is none to reset. Weapon Ranks were retired in R2 (save v28); since then a weapon has a grade, G0–G10, which belongs to the weapon kind and serves every class. Grades stay; nothing is refunded for them.

What resets, what stays

Resets

  • Her class: her calling’s Tier 0 at Rank 1 (later tiers: her mapped Tier 1 class at Rank 1)
  • Every class’s Class Rank, weapons, lines and equipped skills
  • Calling picks (the new rule has none)
  • A moved trade’s running job: finished into the Warehouse with its Craft EXP; its queue dropped

Stays

  • Uma Levels, EXP, Shards
  • Weapon, armor and accessory grades; her accessory family and kind
  • Extra Stats map and her moon
  • Craft Levels; Bonds; Hall seats: this reset moves none (Halls v2’s healer merge has its own line on the sheet)
  • Parties (every Uma is a Tier 0, so no party breaks the healer rule)
  • Laurels, tickets, items, Class Marks already banked; achievements

Mid-way

  • An open fight plays out on engine v18 with the class it started with
  • A Rift run keeps its seals, roster and attempt; its Umas rebuild before the next fight; the guidance reads held classes
  • Targets follow her active class: her Tier 0, then her promotion
