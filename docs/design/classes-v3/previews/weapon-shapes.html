The five shapes

Line A on the Brawler’s and the Sorcerer’s trees and on the Summoners’ summons. The tapped foe is ringed; a dot is a hit, its size its share.

Show

The Spellblades strike one foe with every skill, so for them a shape is only its hits: Katana and Light 2 at every depth, Bow and Dark 2 → 4, Lance and Lightning 2 → 3, the rest 1 → 2, and her lead half one hit more. The healers strike one foe once; no shape change touches them.

Why a volley, not a second sweep

What one act is worth, in single-target units at its depth, by how many foes stand. The volley favours the foe she aims at: the best act on two foes, second to the sweep on a crowd, better than it on one.
