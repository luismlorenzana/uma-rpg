The migration: 24 seats to 20

Seat n stays seat n, id and all, in the four halls that continue: their Heroes, Learners and Bonds don't move. The Paladin Bastion becomes the Healer Sanctum's seats 1–2 and the Cleric Chapel its seats 3–4, two old seats into each new one (shaded): the only data that moves. Rides Classes v3's engine v19 and save v32.

1 · BondsEach Uma's Bond with the new seat is the higher of her two. Nothing is summed, nothing lost.
2 · The HeroIf both old seats had one, the Hero with the lower Bond keeps the seat; the other becomes its first Learner at her Bond. Two Bond 10 Heroes lose nothing.
3 · LearnersBoth lists pooled, each Uma once: the displaced Hero first, then by Bond. Ten sit; anyone beyond keeps her Bond.
4 · Shared LearnersA Learner under both old seats now has one Hero there, and a free place for another.
5 · One lineOn Classes v3’s one-time sheet, “The classes are rebuilt”: every Uma who moved.

Example. Bastion 2 has Hero A (Bond 4) and six Learners; Bastion 3 has Hero B (Bond 10) and five, one shared. Healer 2: A stays Hero; B becomes its first Learner at Bond 10, still casting at 100%; nine of the other ten Learners sit by Bond, the tenth keeps her Bond; the shared Learner sits once and gains a free place.
