## 3. Her window: the board

### 3.1 Six tracks, five steps and the Rift

> **Since save v36 (D289), seven tracks:** Weapons joined after Gear, five weapon kinds at G2, G4, G6, G8 and G10, so the
> board is 36 steps (35 in Tier 3). The manual's [windows](../../game/loops.md#windows) has the board as built.

A window has **31 steps**: six tracks of five on a board, and the Rift. Each step lights about a thirtieth of her
panes; the 31st lights her face ([§4.2](#42-the-order-outside-in-her-face-last)). Six rules shape the board:

- **The owner's five things are five tracks** (Level, Class, Craft, Gear, World); **Stars** is the sixth, because the
  Extra Stats map is the other thing a profile shows that fills up (CO11).
- **Five steps a track,** spread over a tier's levels, so her window starts lighting on her first day and keeps lighting
  through the tier, instead of all at once at Lv 50 (from Genshin's phases and Arknights' elites).
- **Every step is a rule the game measures already:** her level, her ranks, her Craft Level, her grades, her nodes.
  Only World needs a new record, the capital champions she has beaten.
- **The same board every tier,** read at that tier's numbers ([§3.3](#33-tiers-2-and-3)).
- **The Rift is the 31st,** because every Uma who ascends today has conquered it, and the window keeps that.
- **Nothing outside her own progress:** no win counts, no luck, nothing that depends on which Umas you own.

- **World** asks for the three champions, not every stage: a continent has 480 stages, which is the world's job, not

### 3.3 Tiers 2 and 3

The same board. What changes:

| Track | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Level | Lv 10 · 20 · 30 · 40 · 50 of Tier 2 | The same |
| Class | I: a Tier 1 class at R10 (no Tier 0 above Tier 1, CV10); II–V as Tier 1 | The same |

| Rift | Tier 2's Rift | **None yet:** Tier 3's Rift waits for its fifth scenario's rules, so Tier 3's window has 30 steps and its face lights with the 30th |

A Tier 3 window, once full, stays full if Tier 3 later gains a Rift: completion latches like a step (CO2). Tier 3 has
nowhere to ascend; its full window is the end of the collection, drawn in Tier 3's frame ([§4.4](#44-tier-frames)).

### 3.4 How a step is earned

- **Latched:** the first time her state meets a step, it is hers with that day's date, for good. Classes v3's kind of
  reset, a class switch, a lower grade after switching, an ascension: nothing takes a step back (CO2).
- **Checked after every change to her:** a fight's result, a collected job, a raised grade, a set jewel, a read Manual
  or Log, a promotion. One pure rule, `windowSteps(uma, world, tier)`, says which steps her state meets; the save adds
  the new ones. Opening the save runs it once for everyone.
- **A step lit plays a short light-up** on her window, wherever her window is on screen, and the fight result or the
  job's toast says "Window +1 · Lv 30" (the copy test: ≤ 6 words).
- **Per tier:** a step is earned in the tier she's in. Her Tier 1 window stays as it was when she ascended.

### 3.5 What it takes: the long poles

**Her own Craft Level is the long pole.** Every other track can be pushed by the roster: a strong party levels nothing
of hers, but Drills' Logs level her, Trials and Manuals rank her, and the roster's forges make her gear and jewels. Her
Craft Level comes only from her own work: 588 hours at the places' ×1.25, **24.5 days nonstop**, about 30 at the 80% a
player who collects a few times a day keeps.

**And twelve slots a trade.** She works only her own trade's three places, four slots each. So the roster's windows are
capped by the slots, not by play:

| | Nonstop | At 80% |
|---|---|---|
| One Uma, Craft 1 → 50 | 24.5 days | 30.6 days |
| One trade's slots | 12 | 12 |
| Craft 50s a trade a month, slots full | about 14.7 | about 11.8 |
| The whole Wilds a month, all sixty slots full | about 73 | about 59 |
| The largest trades (about 72 Umas each in the 270-outfit release), all of them | about 4.9 months | about **6 months** |

**The owner's call (2026-11-02): not a bottleneck in play.** Many Umas level at once, and Speed Tickets fast-forward the
ones that need it, so Craft V stays at Lv 50 (CO15). The cap doesn't touch the first twenty: a fresh world's first Rift run takes about
as long as its first Craft 50s ([board page](previews/board.html), the chart).

**The first month, one Uma** (the [board page](previews/board.html) draws it with sliders; assumptions: her party wins

## 4. The reveal

### 4.1 Unlit: dark glass

The owner offered gray or black. **Dark glass** (CO3), between the two:

- **Unlit panes** keep a sixth of their light, three quarters of the color drained, a cold tint: a church window at
  night.
- **The lead lines are always drawn,** at full strength. The window's structure is hers, so her outline, her hair and
  the shape of her face show from step 0: you know whose window it is, and nothing of her color.
- **The face's enamel lines** (the original art kept on the face sheet) show faintly through the dark.
- **Why not gray:** in gray she's fully readable at step 0, so color is the only reward and the face reveal is gone.
  **Why not black:** pure black hides the lead too, so every unlit window is the same black square in a list.

The [window page](previews/window.html) toggles dark and gray on a made-up window.

### 4.2 The order: outside in, her face last

Each step lights about a thirtieth of her panes, in one fixed order a window (CO4):

1. **The frame:** the border's bars and corner squares, the first two or three steps.
2. **The glass behind her:** the sunburst, from its rim inward toward her head.
3. **Her figure, from the bottom up:** shoulders and dress, then hair.
4. **Her face, last:** at 30 of 31 every pane is lit but her face, the one sheet of glass that is her; the 31st lights
   it. That's the "one more" moment, and the face appears exactly when she can ascend.

Around step 15 a window reads as a dark figure against lit glass, a silhouette; through the twenties her colors fill in
from below. A step lights **panes, not area**, so each step's share of the picture varies by window; what's even is the
count.

Two orders were tried on real windows and are on the [window page](previews/window.html) on a made-up one:
**rising** (everything by height, like light coming over the sill) and **mosaic** (panes at random, face last). Rising
reads well but lights her dress before the frame; mosaic shows her colors from the first steps but reads as noise and
loses the silhouette. Outside-in tells one story: frame, light, her, her face.

### 4.3 How it's drawn

The bake already cuts every window into panes and knows the face sheet, the figure and the frame. It writes one more
file a window (CO5):

- **`outfit-glass-order`,** a new release family: a 512 × 512 grayscale map the size of the window, each pixel its
  pane's place in the order, 1–250, the face 255 and the lead 0. Lossless WebP, about 8–15 KB a window (8 KB measured on one real window without its lead).
- **The game draws** the window and its map on a canvas: a pixel whose value is at most 250 × lit ÷ 30 is drawn as is,
  any other as dark glass, the lead always as is. One pass over 262,144 pixels, a few milliseconds, cached by the
  share lit.
- **Nothing in the game cuts glass,** and the order can change with a rebake and no game release.
- The map comes from the same bake as the window, so a pinned face moves both.

### 4.4 Tier frames

A full window gets **its tier's frame**: a ring in the tier's color around the window, Tier 1 day gold, Tier 2 dusk
amber, Tier 3 night violet (the tiers' looks), and the tier's crest in its corner (CO6). On arriving in Tier 2 her
window starts dark again; the Gallery shows her best full window with its frame, so a Tier 2 Uma keeps her Tier 1 gold
in the collection while her Tier 2 window lights.

## 5. Promotion: only a full window ascends

### 5.1 Ascend, one Uma at a time

- **When:** her window in this tier is full, all 31 (CO16). No fight of hers open, and not at work ("Recall or collect"),
  as today.
- **Where:** the Gallery's **Ascend**, for any number of ready Umas at once; her Window sheet says "Ready to ascend" and
  is the door to it ([§7.4](#74-ascend)).
- **What happens to her** is today's Ascend, for one Uma: she leaves the old world's Stable, parties, targets, carried
  MP, Hall seats and work; her grades' made items go back to the old world's Warehouse; she arrives at Lv 1 with Class
  Rank 1 everywhere, every grade G0, an empty map, Craft Lv 1 and no Bonds; her purse goes to the new world, 1,000
  Laurels and 5 Speed Tickets, and her banked EXP as Class Marks.
- **Parties:** an Ascend of several fills the new world's parties as today (a party whose five all ascend moves with its
  name); one Uma joins the first party with a free slot.
- **Her history** gets the entry it gets today: the tier, the date, and the run she conquered.

### 5.2 The Rift run

- **Sign-up stays at twenty Lv 50 Umas** (CO17). Gating sign-up on a 30-of-31 window would hold the Rift back a month
  behind Craft; the window gates the ascent instead.
- **A won run lights the Rift step** for its twenty at once; those whose windows are full can ascend that moment, the
  others when they finish.
- **A new run can sign up once the last one is won** (CO18), not once its twenty have ascended: a run's Umas no longer
  move together.
- **An Uma who already has the Rift step** may sign up again, to fill a run of twenty; it lights nothing more.
- Everything else about the Rift stays: fronts, Convergence, retries, achievements, the finale.

### 5.3 What doesn't change

Uma Tickets and Shards, the account's achievements and titles, the purse, the arrival rules, the Tier sheet, D102's
"Uma EXP is a share of her own level" (a Log is one fight's share, [§6.4](#64-drills-the-daily-farm)).

### 6.2 The Campaign

Unchanged: the world map, its continents and capitals. One new record: when a capital champion fight is won, each
Uma in the party has that champion added to her World record ([§8](#8-save-and-code)).

### 6.3 Trials: the rank content

**The Echoes already are this:** fights for Lv 50 parties at Lv 45, 50 and 55, one a front, paying twice a stage's
Class EXP to the class she fights as, Class Marks past Rank 50, sweepable with Sweep Tickets ([loops](../../game/loops.md#the-rift)).
A fresh class reaches Rank 30 in about 9 wins at Lv 50 and Rank 50 in about 20. They sit at the bottom of the Rift page,
which is why they're easy to forget.

**Recommended: move them out of the Rift into their own place, named Trials** (CO20): the same twelve fights, the same
pay, the same Sweeps, the same records (`rift.echoes` keeps its name in the save). The Rift page keeps only the run.
They open with the Rift, at five Lv 50 Umas. On screen "Iron Trial · Lv 55".

**Why not a new kind:** a second rank content would pay the same Class EXP through a different door. If the owner wants
more than a move, the alternative is per-class Trials: one a class, fought as that class, paying ×3 (CO20's
alternative), worth building only if Trials feel too samey in play.

### 6.4 Drills: the daily farm

**EXP or items: items** (CO21). A Drill pays **Training Logs**, which any Uma below Lv 50 reads, anywhere and any time.

- **Why items:** the party that farms is your strongest; the Umas who need levels are the ones it leaves on the bench.
  Items let a win go to whoever needs it, which is the play pattern this game already has (one party fights while dozens
  work). Every game in §1 that farms EXP pays it as items. And the Class Manual already works this way for Class EXP.
- **The case for EXP:** no new item, no reading step. It would only level the five who fought, who are the five who need
  it least.
- **The name:** "Tome" is a weapon here (Fire Tome, Blizzard Tome), and "Manual" is the Class Manual's, so the item is a
  **Training Log** (CO22).

**What a Log holds** (CO22): one fight's EXP, measured in her own level, D102's rule:

- **Uma EXP:** a third of what she needs for her next level, ⌈EXP to next ÷ 3⌉. Three Logs, one level, at any level; 147
  Logs take an Uma from Lv 1 to 50.
- **Class EXP:** twice that, to the class she fights as, by a fight's rules: a Tier 0 at Rank 10 keeps it waiting for her
  promotion, past Rank 50 it banks as Class Marks at half. So a Log-leveled Uma is as ranked as a fought one.
- **Shards** come with the levels, as from fights (Tier 1).
- **Not at Lv 50:** "Lv 50 · Trials pay ranks".

**The Drills** (CO24): five fights, one a continent's band, at the tier's Lv 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50, each a fixed stage
(a squad on a fixed seed, its foes frozen into the fight as every fight's are). The first win is a real fight; after
that a Drill is **swept free** (no Sweep Ticket), ×1 or ×5 at a time.

| Drill | Level | Logs a win | A day's 10 runs | Levels a day |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Drill I | Lv 10 | 2 | 20 | 6.7 |
| Drill II | Lv 20 | 3 | 30 | 10 |
| Drill III | Lv 30 | 4 | 40 | 13.3 |
| Drill IV | Lv 40 | 5 | 50 | 16.7 |
| Drill V | Lv 50 | 6 | 60 | 20: one Uma from Lv 1 to 50 every 2.5 days |

**The daily limit** (CO23): **10 runs a day**, fights and sweeps alike; the day turns at 04:00 on the phone's clock; runs
not used **bank up to 30**, so three days away lose nothing. "Runs 18 · +10 at 4:00".

**Measured against the Campaign:** the Campaign levels a party of five through 49 levels in about 120 wins, about two
levels a win; a top Drill run pays two levels. So a Drill run is worth a Campaign win, aimed at whoever you choose, ten
times a day. The [content page](previews/content.html) has the calculator.

### 6.5 The Rift

The run as today, without its Echoes (they're Trials now) and without its Ascend (the Gallery's now). Its page after a
win: "Rift won · 3 ready" and a door to the Gallery.

## 9. The switch

The first time a save opens at v35, every world, every Uma:

- **Her window latches every step her state meets,** dated that day: her level, her ranks, her Craft Level, her grades,
  her nodes.
- **World, retroactive** (CO25): continent n's step for every Uma whose level is at least the continent's top (10n in
  Tier 1) in a world that has won all three of its champions. She was very likely in those fights; the alternative is
  fifteen easy replays for every Uma.
- **The Rift step** for every Uma on the roster of a run already won.
- **Umas who ascended before windows:** their lower tiers' windows are full, dated the day they ascended.
- **A run won but not yet ascended** at the switch: its twenty keep the Rift step and ascend when their windows are full.
  **Worth knowing before the switch:** if a run of yours is waiting to ascend, ascend it first, or its Umas wait for
  their windows.
- No Uma, item, rank or level changes. The Observatory's route goes to the Gallery.
