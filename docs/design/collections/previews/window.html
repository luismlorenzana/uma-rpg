The window reveal

Each Uma's stained-glass window starts as dark glass and lights pane by pane, one step of her board at a time, her face with the last. A made-up window, cut the way the bake cuts the real ones: a frame of bars, a sunburst behind her, her figure in panes and her face as one sheet. Doc §4.

The three orders

All three keep the face for the last step, so a full window always means "ready to ascend". On real windows the same three were tried on the bake's own panes; those images went to the owner only.

How the game draws it

The artoutfit-glass, 512 × 512, already in the release for every outfit
The pane mapoutfit-glass-order (new): the same size, grayscale, each pixel its pane's place in the order, 1–250; the face 255; the lead 0. Lossless WebP, about 8–15 KB
LitA pixel whose value is at most 250 × lit ÷ 30 is drawn as is; at 31 the face too
UnlitA sixth of its light, three quarters of its color drained, a cold tint. Lead always as is
CostOne canvas pass over 262,144 pixels, cached by the share lit
