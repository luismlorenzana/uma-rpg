# Capitals v2: the Finals as a mini Rift

**Status:** live on save v37, engine v21 (H162, D302), following H159's foes, H160's rules and H161's screens.
Approved 2026-11-03 (D293); CA14 needs no engine bump. The preview page is
[previews/capitals.html](previews/capitals.html): a run's shape, the three screens and the pacing against today's.

## The owner's points

- Rework the capitals fully into a mini version of the Rift, still generalist: built for balanced parties, not for one
  family.
- Run them as **instances** of four parties at a time. To fight a capital at all, slot four full parties first; that
  creates a fresh instance.
- Inside it, each party fights several **generals**, and the instance ends on **four final bosses**.

## Why it makes sense

Today a capital is three routes, each a Vanguard and then a Champion: six fights shared by the whole world. The first
party opens each route; every later party can only re-fight the three Champions as replays, at a quarter of the pay, and
can't sweep them. The window's World step needs her in the party for all three Champions, so 20 parties take 63 capital
fights a continent, 57 of them replays that pay almost nothing. The capital matters to one party and is a chore for the
other nineteen.

An instance fixes both: each run is fresh, so every party's first run of a continent pays as the first party's does
today, and the World step becomes "on a won run" (as the Rift step is "on a won Rift run"), one run for twenty Umas.

## 1. A run at a glance

A capital's content becomes the **Finals run** (the owner's instance; "run" on screen, as the Rift's):

| Part | What |
|---|---|
| **Sign-up** | Pick four full parties from the Party Hall, twenty different Umas, and give each a **lane**. The run is created fresh |
| **Lanes** | Four lanes, one per party, each the capital's authored theme ("Sprint Final", "Mile Final" …). A lane is **three generals**, fought in order by its own party |
| **The Final** | Opens once all four lanes have beaten their generals: **four final bosses**, one per lane, each fought by that lane's party, in any order |
| **Won** | When all four final bosses fall: the run is won, and World n lights on all twenty windows |

Sixteen fights a run: twelve generals and four final bosses. The Rift is 52 (48 front stages and the Convergence's 4).

## 2. Starting a run

- **Four full parties,** chosen on the capital's page ("New run"): each lane takes one, so the twenty are the parties'
  members as they stand then. The run keeps them: changing a party in the Party Hall afterwards doesn't touch the run.
- **One run per Uma:** an Uma in an open run can't be signed up to another until it's won or abandoned. She can still
  fight anywhere else, work, and change her class, skills and gear between fights.
- **Any number of runs at once,** across continents or on the same capital: with 20 parties, up to five.
- **No level gate:** the foes are the capital's level (Lv 10 of its band); a party below it is simply outmatched.
- **One healer a party,** as everywhere: a lane's party with two healers can't fight until it's fixed.
- **Abandon:** an open run can be abandoned at any time, freeing its twenty; its progress is lost. Nothing is paid back,
  and nothing was spent to start it.

## 3. Lanes and generals

- **Four lanes a capital,** each one of its authored routes: today's three ("Sprint Final", "Mile Final",
  "Long-Distance Final" in Ura Finale) and a **fourth, authored new** for every scenario (Ura Finale's "Medium Final").
  Each lane keeps its two elements and its escorts' roles, so the four lanes together ask for a balanced party, never a
  specialist.
- **Three generals a lane,** in order. A general is a sub-boss leader (shield 6) with two escorts, at the capital's
  level: General I and III lead with the lane's first element, General II with its second. Like the Rift's fronts, the
  next general is the only one open, and a beaten one isn't re-fought in that run.
- **Generalist by design:** no ward and no guidance; the page shows each lane's elements and its foes, and any balanced
  party can take any lane.

## 4. The Final

- **It opens when every lane has beaten its three generals:** the four lanes converge on the capital's four final
  bosses, one at the end of each lane.
- **Each final boss** is a boss (shield 8) with two sub-boss escorts, in two waves (the escorts first, then the boss with
  two more), led by the lane's champion element as today's Champion is.
- **Each is fought by its own lane's party, in any order,** with free retries. The run is won when all four fall.
- **Not a gauntlet:** unlike the Rift's Convergence, a lost final costs nothing but the try. A capital is the story's
  regular progression, not the end-of-tier test.

## 5. Fights

- **HP and MP full** at the start of every run fight, as in the Rift; the world's carried MP stays as it was.
- **The world map's open fight:** a run fight is the map's one open fight, as a Drill is; one fight at a time on the
  map.
- **Retries** are free and unlimited, each with a fresh seed against the same foes.
- **No Sweeps** of run fights, as capitals today.
- **The faces** are drawn fresh for each run from the capital's franchise (PriConne, as today), fixed when the run
  starts, so a second run looks new while its lanes play the same.

## 6. Pay and pacing

A fight pays as any map fight: a share of her own next level, less when she's above the foes. Generals pay as today's
Vanguards (a sub-boss's weight, halved), the final boss as a two-wave boss.

| Per Uma, a continent's capital | Today | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| In the world's first party | 3 Vanguards and 3 Champions: about **1.3 levels** | Her first run: 3 generals and her lane's final boss: about **1.3 levels** |
| In any later party | 3 Champion replays at a quarter: about **0.16 levels** | Her first run pays the same as everyone's: about **1.3 levels** |
| Again, a later run | — | A quarter: about 0.3 levels |
| Fights for 20 parties | 63 (57 replays) | 80 (five runs), every Uma's first run at full pay |

- **Her first run of a continent pays full,** read from her window: until her World n step is lit. After it, a run of
  that capital pays a quarter, as a replay does.
- **The rest as today:** Laurels by the fight's kind (triple on a first run), Class EXP at twice her Uma EXP, Uma Shards
  in Tier 1.
- **The level curve holds** (D97): two fresh regions and the capital take a party across a continent's ten levels, now
  for all twenty parties instead of one.

## 7. The window

- **World n:** "Continent n: a won capital run", lit for the twenty of a run when its fourth final boss falls, dated that
  day, like the Rift step. The rule text changes; the step's place on the board doesn't.
- **The champions mask goes:** the record's 15-bit mask of Champions beaten (D283) has no use once the step is "on a won
  run", so the record drops it (save v37). A World step already lit stays lit; partial progress was only ever in the mask,
  and your save has none.
- **The board's next tile** reads "Not run", "Run 3 · 2 of 4 finals" while she's in an open run, or the day it lit.

## 8. Screens

- **The continent map:** the capital castle reads "Capital · Lv 10 · 2 runs won" (or "Open run" while one is).
- **The capital page** (`capital/:continent`): its name and level, "Runs won: 2", each open run as a card ("Run 3 ·
  Parties 1, 2, 5, 6 · 7/16") and **New run**.
- **New run** (a sheet): the four lanes, each with its name, line and elements and a party picker (full parties not in
  an open run; a party with two healers flagged), then **Start run**.
- **A run's page:** four lane cards (the party's five faces, "General 2 of 3 · Lv 10", the next general's foes and
  **Fight**), then the **Final** card: locked with "Beat every lane's generals" until it opens, then its four bosses, each
  with its lane's party and **Fight**; **Abandon run** at the foot, with a confirmation.
- **The fight:** the shared fight screen, titled "Sprint Final · General 2" or "Sprint Final · Final"; its result says
  "General 2 of 3", "Final 3 of 4", or "Capital won · World I for 20 Umas".

## 9. Next to the Rift

| | The Rift | A capital run |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Twenty Lv 50 Umas | Four full parties, any level |
| Parts | Four fronts of twelve stages, then the Convergence's four fights | Four lanes of three generals, then four final bosses |
| Foes | Specialised: each front wards one channel, with a guided build | Generalist: each lane two elements, any balanced party |
| The end | The Convergence: four fights in order, a loss ends the attempt | Four final bosses, any order, free retries |
| How many | One run at a time a world | Any number at once, one per Uma |
| Pays | Achievements only | EXP, Class EXP and Laurels, full on her first run |
| Its window step | The Rift: on a won run | World n: on a won run |
| Its fight slot | Its own, beside the map's | The map's |

## 10. Save and code

- **Save v37,** each world: `capitalRuns`, up to five open runs (id, continent, start time and four lanes, each its Party
  Hall index for display, its signed five, generals beaten and final's win time), and `capitals`, by continent:
  `started` numbers every run, including abandoned ones; `won` counts wins; `recent` keeps six wins, newest first, each
  its id, win time and four signed fives. A retry's seed comes from the map's fight counter, as a Drill's does. The account's window records lose
  `champions`. A save before v37 had no runs, so it gains them empty; its old capital wins stay in the record harmlessly.
- **No engine bump** (D293): a fight replays from its frozen input, and the run's foes come from a new generator, not a
  rule change. An open fight on an old capital stage at the switch settles as a regular fight at its foes' level, as a
  removed node always has.
- **A run's wins live in the run,** never in `world.run.wins`, which holds one entry a map stage (D292's bound): sixteen new
  ids a run would grow it without end.
- **The fourth route** joins `rpg/campaign/finals.ts` for each of the fourteen scenarios (names and lines original).
- **Rules** in a new `save/capital.ts` behind a lazy `@rpg/domain/capital`, like the Rift's; screens in
  `places/scenario-gate/capital/`.

## 11. Alternatives considered

- **A. A Convergence at the end** (the four finals in order, a loss ends the attempt): the Rift's tension, but harsh for
  regular progression, and a loss would send four parties back. Kept for the Rift.
- **B. Each lane's final opens on its own** (no waiting for the other lanes): simpler, but the run stops being one
  instance; four parties could each finish alone.
- **C. Two or four generals a lane:** two makes the run 12 fights and the capital thin; four makes it 20 and longer than
  two regions. Three keeps a run near today's pay with one more fight per Uma.
- **D. Keep shared Champions and only add instances on top:** two systems for one step.
