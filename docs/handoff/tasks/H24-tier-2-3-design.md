# H24 — Tier 2 and 3 design

**Priority P4 (parked) · the owner and Claude · uma-rpg · M.** After H23. Needs Q7.

## Why

The design gives each tier its own new mechanic (game design §2.2), and Tier 3 has four chapters, but none of it is
specified. The world map (D72) changed the frame: a tier is now a world of five continents.

## The decision first

**Q7** (`docs/open-questions.md`): a third profession vertical, and in which tier?

**Recommendation:** let Tier 2 introduce it as its new mechanic. The two existing verticals are Farming + Cooking
and Gathering + Crafting (§8), so Tier 2 could add a third, for example Fishing + Alchemy, with its own families
feeding new recipes. Tier 3's mechanic stays open until Tier 2 is played.

## Then (Claude, with the owner)

1. A design page, `docs/design/tiers.md`, covering:
   - what each tier adds: its mechanic, its continents' biomes and leans, its foe budget and rewards;
   - how Tier 3's four chapters map onto the world;
   - what carries between tiers (from H23).
2. Folded into game design §2.2, and Q7 recorded as a decision.

## Done when

The owner accepts `tiers.md`.
