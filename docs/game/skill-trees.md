# Skill trees

Every tree, every node and every number, as engine v21 plays them (Classes v3, D240; the shapes and lines D246, the
balance D249; the combat pass D282). The eight Tier 1 classes have 70 trees, one per class and weapon: the Brawler's five Arms, the Sorcerer's
five Tomes and all ten for the Arms and Tome Spellblades, the Arms and Tome Summoners, the Paladin and the Cleric. Each
has eight nodes: the **trunk** (depths 1–2), **line A** and **line B** (depths 3–5), B named in each tree's heading. Her
three slotted weapons open three of her class's trees. A Tier 0's tree is one node a weapon, her one skill, 50 in all
([classes](classes.md#tier-0-and-promotion)); they aren't listed here. The rules (slots, lines, Skill Ranks) are in
[classes](classes.md#skills-depths-lines-and-ranks). Fights from engine v18 and before replay with their own trees: the
six classes' 50, with the Bow and Light many hits on one foe, Dark two, the Brawler's Bow B Rain, and the Mage's Light B
Cascade and Dark B Pierce.

Code: `packages/domain/src/rpg/catalog.ts` (every node, `RPG_VOLLEY`, `RPG_LINE_B_POWER_V19`, `RPG_PIERCE_SCALE`),
`rpg/node-identity.ts` (ids, `RPG_BRANCH_B`, `RPG_BRANCH_B_NAME`, depth power and MP), `rpg/rules.ts` (held skills, the
loadout, Skill Ranks), `rpg/tree-view.ts` (the Class tab's model), `rpg/battle/engine.ts` (how a skill lands). The list
below is generated by `packages/domain/src/docs/render.ts`.

## How to read a row

- **Node** is the id the save and the engine use: `<tree>.<depth>` for the trunk and line A, `<tree>.<depth>a|b` for
  line B (the catalog's side letter: B is `b` on most trees, `a` on some, by class and kind).
- **Line** says where it sits: the trunk, line A or line B.
- **MP** is what it costs her, after her hand.
- **What it does** gives the targets, the number of hits and the total power before her hand, at Skill Rank 3 (×1; her
  rank moves it ×0.95 to ×1.05). Every-foe power is per foe (×0.55 of a single target's), and random hits include their
  ×1.05. A volley reads "one foe · 2 hits · 2.32 · 1.015 on every other foe": its hits on the tapped foe (0.8 of the
  depth's power), then its one splash hit on each other foe (0.35). Then a healer's status, a summon's linger share, and
  a healer's support: a mend, a Barrier, Aegis, Regen, a cleanse, a drain or a revive. Mend and Barrier shares are of
  max HP, before her healing scale and before the ×0.4 a share takes when it reaches every ally
  ([combat](combat.md#support)).
- A Spellblade's lead half lands one hit more than listed, the same total: the Arms Spellblade's Arms skills, the Tome
  Spellblade's Tome skills. Only a Summoner's lead summons linger (then strike every foe once in farewell,
  [combat](combat.md#the-actions)): the Arms Summoner's Arms, the Tome Summoner's Tomes. The linger share printed on her
  other half's summons is never used.

## What a row leaves out

The rows print the hits and a healer's kit. These parts of a skill are not in them:

- **No riders:** from engine v21 no skill puts a status on the foes it strikes as a rider. The depth-5 skills that had one play
  at a higher power instead (`RPG_PURE_POWER_V21`, folded into the rows): the Brawler ×1.1, the Arms Spellblade ×1.26, the Tome
  Spellblade ×1.15, the Tome Summoner ×1.16, the Arms Summoner ×1.08 and the Sorcerer ×1.
- **Specials** at depth 5: the Katana and the Light Tome deal ×1.3 to a Broken foe; the Dark Tome deals ×1.3 to a foe under
  half her HP. The Brawler's and the Sorcerer's line A keep theirs, the Sorcerer's Dark B, Barrage, too, and the Summoners' line
  B on the Katana. A Spellblade's or a Summoner's line A on a secondary weapon carries a status instead. Pierce is below.
- **A volley's splash is plain:** no special on a foe only the splash struck; a weak splash still chips a shield and breaks a
  lock.
- **Extras:** the Brawler's Reap (Axe B) adds one hit on the tapped foe at 0.5 × the depth's power after its every-foe
  hits.
- **Stacks:** a status holds 3 stacks at most, so a Heavy depth 5 lands 3.

## Pierce

**Pierce** (CP42, CV14) is both Spellblades' line B on every tree, the Brawler's Katana B (Draw) and the Sorcerer's Light
B: 66 nodes at depths 3–5. Each lands one hit at ×1.15 the depth's power (×1.035 for the Brawler's, after her ×0.9, and
about ×0.98 for the Sorcerer's, after her ×0.85) that **ignores the halving a standing shield gives** (×0.5 until the foe
is Broken). The Rift's ward still halves it. On a Spellblade's lead-half tree her extra hit splits it into two, both
piercing. The Class tab says a Pierce "cuts through the shield".

## Equipped skills

She equips skills she holds on her three slotted trees: three at Uma Lv 1 and one more at Lv 10, 20, 30 and 40, seven at
most ([classes](classes.md#skills-depths-lines-and-ranks)). Until you pick, they are chosen for her (each tree's deepest
held skill in turn). A line swap moves an equipped skill of that tree to the other line's node at the same depth, and
unslotting a weapon takes its tree's skills out of the loadout. A Tier 0 holds three skills, one a weapon, all equipped.
